# CC Antya 2.43

**Bengali:**

jagadānanda haya tāhāṅ, teṅho bhikṣā dibe
sabāre kahiha, — e vatsara keha nā āsibe”

**Transliteration:**

jagadānanda — Jagadānanda; haya — is; tāhāṅ — there; teṅho — he; bhikṣā dibe — will give offerings of food; sabāre kahiha — inform all of them; e</em> <em>vatsara — this year; keha nā āsibe — no one should come.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jagadānanda — Jagadānanda; haya — is; tāhāṅ — there; teṅho — he; bhikṣā dibe — will give offerings of food; sabāre kahiha — inform all of them; e</em> <em>vatsara — this year; keha nā āsibe — no one should come.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Jagadānanda is there, and he will give Me offerings of food. Inform them all that no one should come to Jagannātha Purī this year.”
