# CC Antya 1.10

**Bengali:**

madhya-līlā-madhye antya-līlā-sūtra-gaṇa
pūrva-granthe saṅkṣepete kariyāchi varṇana

**Transliteration:**

madhya-līlā-madhye — within the madhya-līlā chapters; antya-līlā-sūtra-gaṇa — a synopsis of the antya-līlā; saṅkṣepete — in brief; kariyāchi varṇana — I have described.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
madhya-līlā-madhye — within the madhya-līlā chapters; antya-līlā-sūtra-gaṇa — a synopsis of the antya-līlā; saṅkṣepete — in brief; kariyāchi varṇana — I have described.

**Translation:**

Translation
I have briefly described the antya-līlā within the description of the madhya-līlā.
