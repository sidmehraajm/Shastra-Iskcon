# CC Antya 1.221

**Bengali:**

prabhura bhakta-gaṇa-pāśe vidāya la-ilā
punarapi gauḍa-pathe vṛndāvane āilā

**Transliteration:**

prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhakta-gaṇa-pāśe — from the devotees; vidāya la-ilā — took leave; punarapi — again; gauḍa-pathe — by the way through Bengal; vṛndāvane — to Vṛndāvana; āilā — returned.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhakta-gaṇa-pāśe — from the devotees; vidāya la-ilā — took leave; punarapi — again; gauḍa-pathe — by the way through Bengal; vṛndāvane — to Vṛndāvana; āilā — returned.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī took leave of all the devotees of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and returned to Vṛndāvana by the path through Bengal.
