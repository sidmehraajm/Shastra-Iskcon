# CC Antya 1.46

**Bengali:**

haridāsa-ṭhākura tāṅre bahu-kṛpā kailā
‘tumi āsibe, — more prabhu ye kahilā’

**Transliteration:**

haridāsa-ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; tāṅre — unto him; bahu-kṛpā kailā — showed much affection because of love and mercy; tumi āsibe — you will come; more — me; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ye — that; kahilā — informed.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
haridāsa-ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; tāṅre — unto him; bahu-kṛpā kailā — showed much affection because of love and mercy; tumi āsibe — you will come; more — me; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ye — that; kahilā — informed.

**Translation:**

Translation
Out of affectionate love and mercy, Haridāsa Ṭhākura told Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī, “Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu has already informed me that you would come here.”
