# CC Ādi 13.53

**Bengali:**

আগে অবতারিলা যে যে গুরু–পরিবার ।
সংক্ষেপে কহিয়ে, কহা না যায় বিস্তার ॥ ৫৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āge avatārilā ye ye guru-parivāra
saṅkṣepe kahiye, kahā nā yāya vistāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āge — first of all; avatārilā — allowed to descend; ye ye — all those; guru-parivāra — family of spiritual masters; saṅkṣepe — in brief; kahiye — I describe; kahā — to describe; nā — not; yāya — possible; vistāra — expansively.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Kṛṣṇa therefore first allowed His family of superiors to descend to the earth. I shall try to describe them in brief because it is not possible to describe them fully.
