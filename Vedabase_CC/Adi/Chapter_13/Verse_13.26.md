# CC Ādi 13.26

**Bengali:**

বাল্য বয়স—যাবৎ হাতে খড়ি দিল ।
পৌগণ্ড বয়স—যাবৎ বিবাহ না কৈল ॥ ২৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bālya vayasa — yāvat hāte khaḍi dila
paugaṇḍa vayasa — yāvat vivāha nā kaila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bālya vayasa — childhood age; yāvat — until the time; hāte — in His hand; khaḍi — chalk; dila — was given; paugaṇḍa vayasa — the part of boyhood known as paugaṇḍa; vivāha — marriage; nā — not; kaila — did take place.

**Translation:**

Translation
His childhood lasted until the date of hāte khaḍi, the beginning of His education, and His age from the end of His childhood until He married is called paugaṇḍa.
