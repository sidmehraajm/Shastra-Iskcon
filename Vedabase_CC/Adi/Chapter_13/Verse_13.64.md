# CC Ādi 13.64

**Bengali:**

গীতা–ভাগবত কহে আচার্য–গোসাঞি ।
জ্ঞান–কর্ম নিন্দি’ করে ভক্তির বড়াই ॥ ৬৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gītā-bhhāgavata kahe ācārya-gosāñi
jñāna-karma nindi’ kare bhaktira baḍāi

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gītā — the Bhagavad-gītā; ācārya-gosāñi — Advaita Ācārya; jñāna — the path of philosophical speculation; karma — fruitive activity; nindi’ — decrying; kare — establishes; bhaktira — of devotional service; baḍāi — excellence.

**Translation:**

Translation
In these meetings of the Vaiṣṇavas, Advaita Ācārya used to recite the Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, decrying the paths of philosophical speculation and fruitive activity and establishing the superexcellence of devotional service.
