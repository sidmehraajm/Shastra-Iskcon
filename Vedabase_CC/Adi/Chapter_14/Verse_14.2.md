# CC Ādi 14.2

**Bengali:**

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য, জয় নিত্যানন্দ।
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র, জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya, jaya nityānanda
jayādvaitacandra, jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jaya jaya — all glories; śrī-caitanya — to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; jaya — all glories; nityānanda — to Nityānanda Prabhu; jaya</em> <em>advaita-candra — all glories to Advaita Ācārya; jaya — all glories; gaura-bhakta-vṛnda — to all the devotees of the Lord.

**Translation:**

Translation
All glories to Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Nityānanda Prabhu, Advaita Prabhu and all the devotees of Lord Caitanya!
