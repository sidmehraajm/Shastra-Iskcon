# CC Madhya 24.15

**Bengali:**

‘মুনি’-শব্দে মননশীল, আর কহে মৌনী ।
তপস্বী, ব্রতী, যতি, আর ঋষি, মুনি ॥ ১৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘muni’-śabde manana-śīla, āra kahe maunī
tapasvī, vratī, yati, āra ṛṣi, muni

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
muni-śabde — by the word muni; āra — also; kahe — it means; maunī — one who is silent; tapasvī — an ascetic; vratī — one who keeps great vows; yati — one in the renounced order of life; āra — and; ṛṣi — a saintly person; muni — they are called muni..

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘muni’ refers to one who is thoughtful, one who is grave or silent, an ascetic, one who keeps great vows, one in the renounced order and a saint. These are the different meanings of the word ‘muni.’
