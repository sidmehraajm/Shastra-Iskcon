# CC Madhya 24.83

**Bengali:**

জ্ঞানমার্গে — নির্বিশেষ-ব্রহ্ম প্রকাশে ।
যোগমার্গে — অন্তর্যামী-স্বরূপেতে ভাসে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

jñāna-mārge — nirviśeṣa-brahma prakāśe
yoga-mārge — antaryāmi-svarūpete bhāse

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jñāna-mārge — the process of philosophical speculation; nirviśeṣa-brahma — the impersonal Brahman effulgence; prakāśe — becomes manifest; yoga-mārge — by practicing mystic yoga; bhāse — appears.

**Translation:**

Translation
“If one follows the path of philosophical speculation, the Absolute Truth manifests Himself as impersonal Brahman, and if one follows the path of mystic yoga, He manifests Himself as the Supersoul.
