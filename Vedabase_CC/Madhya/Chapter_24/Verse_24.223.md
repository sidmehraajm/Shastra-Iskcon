# CC Madhya 24.223

**Bengali:**

চ-শব্দে ‘অন্বাচয়ে’ অর্থ কহে আর ।
‘বটো, ভিক্ষামট, গাঞ্চানয়’ যৈছে প্রকার ॥ ২২৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ca-śabde ‘anvācaye’ artha kahe āra
‘baṭo, bhikṣām aṭa, gāṁ cānaya’ yaiche prakāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ca-śabde — by the word ca; artha — meaning; kahe — says; āra — another; baṭo — O brahmacārī; gām ca ānaya — also, at the same time, bring the cows; yaiche prakāra — in this way.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘ca’ can also present a secondary thing to be done at the same time. This way of understanding the word ‘ca’ is called anvācaye. An example is ‘O brahmacārī, go out to collect alms and at the same time bring in the cows.’
