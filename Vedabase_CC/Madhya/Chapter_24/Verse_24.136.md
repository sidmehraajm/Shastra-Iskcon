# CC Madhya 24.136

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণ-বহির্মুখ-দোষে মায়া হৈতে ভয় ।
কৃষ্ণোন্মুখ ভক্তি হৈতে মায়া-মুক্ত হয় ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa-bahirmukha-doṣe māyā haite bhaya
kṛṣṇonmukha bhakti haite māyā-mukta haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa-bahir-mukha — of going against Kṛṣṇa consciousness; doṣe — by the fault; māyā haite — from the illusory energy; bhaya — fear; kṛṣṇa-unmukha — in favor of Kṛṣṇa consciousness; bhakti — devotional service; haite — from; māyā-mukta — liberated from māyā.

**Translation:**

Translation
“By opposing Kṛṣṇa consciousness, one becomes conditioned and fearful due to the influence of māyā. By executing devotional service faithfully, one is liberated from māyā.
