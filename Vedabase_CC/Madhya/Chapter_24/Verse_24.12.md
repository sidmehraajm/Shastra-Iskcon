# CC Madhya 24.12

**Bengali:**

“আত্মা দেহমনোব্রহ্মস্বভাবধৃতিবুদ্ধিষু । প্রযত্নে চ” ইতি ॥ ১২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

“ātmā deha-mano-brahma-svabhāva-dhṛti-buddhiṣu,
prayatne ca” iti

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ātmā — the word ātmā; manaḥ — the mind; brahma — the Absolute Truth; svabhāva — nature; dhṛti — firmness; buddhiṣu — in the sense of intelligence; prayatne — in endeavor; ca — and; iti — thus.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘The following are synonyms of the word “ātmā”: the body, mind, Absolute Truth, natural characteristics, firmness, intelligence and endeavor.’

**Purport:**

Purport
