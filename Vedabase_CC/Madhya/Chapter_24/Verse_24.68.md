# CC Madhya 24.68

**Bengali:**

অপি-শব্দে মুখ্য অর্থ সাত বিখ্যাত ॥ ৬৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

api-śabde mukhya artha sāta vikhyāta

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
api-śabde — by the word api; artha — meanings; sāta — seven; vikhyāta — celebrated.

**Translation:**

Translation
“There are seven chief meanings of the word ‘api.’ They are as follows.
