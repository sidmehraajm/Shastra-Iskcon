# CC Madhya 24.343

**Bengali:**

সর্বত্র প্রমাণ দিবে পুরাণ-বচন ।
শ্রীমূর্তি-বিষ্ণুমন্দিরকরণ-লক্ষণ ॥ ৩৪৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sarvatra pramāṇa dibe purāṇa-vacana
śrī-mūrti-viṣṇu-mandira-karaṇa-lakṣaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sarvatra — everywhere; pramāṇa — evidence; dibe — you should give; purāṇa-vacana — quoting from the Purāṇas; viṣṇu-mandira — of the Viṣṇu temple; karaṇa-lakṣaṇa — the characteristics of constructing.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Whatever you say about Vaiṣṇava behavior, the establishment of Vaiṣṇava temples and Deities, and everything else should be supported by evidence from the Purāṇas.
