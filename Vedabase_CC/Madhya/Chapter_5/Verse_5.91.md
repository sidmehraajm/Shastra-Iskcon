# CC Madhya 5.91

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণ কহে, — বিপ্র, তুমি যাহ স্ব-ভবনে ।
সভা করি’ মোরে তুমি করিহ স্মরণে ॥ ৯১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa kahe, — vipra, tumi yāha sva-bhavane
sabhā kari’ more tumi kariha smaraṇe

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa kahe — Lord Kṛṣṇa says; vipra — My dear brāhmaṇa; yāha — go back; sva-bhavane — to your own home; sabhā kari’ — calling a meeting of all the men; more — of Me; tumi — you; kariha — just do; smaraṇe — remembering.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Kṛṣṇa replied, “My dear brāhmaṇa, go back to your home and call a meeting of all the men. In that meeting, just try to remember Me.
