# CC Madhya 5.21

**Bengali:**

ছোটবিপ্র কহে, — “শুন, বিপ্ৰ-মহাশয় ।
অসম্ভব কহ কেনে, যেই নাহি হয় ॥ ২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

choṭa-vipra kahe, “śuna, vipra-mahāśaya
asambhava kaha kene, yei nāhi haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
choṭa-vipra — the younger brāhmaṇa; śuna — hear; vipra-mahāśaya — my dear brāhmaṇa; kaha — you say; kene — why; yei — which; nāhi — not; haya — happens.

**Translation:**

Translation
The younger brāhmaṇa replied, “My dear sir, please hear me. You are saying something very unusual. Such a thing never happens.
