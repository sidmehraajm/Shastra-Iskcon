# CC Madhya 5.107

**Bengali:**

ব্রাহ্মণেরে কহে, — “তুমি যাহ নিজ-ঘর ।
এথায় রহিব আমি, না যাব অতঃপর ॥” ১০৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

brāhmaṇere kahe, — “tumi yāha nija-ghara
ethāya rahiba āmi, nā yāba ataḥpara”

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
brāhmaṇere kahe — He asked the brāhmaṇa; yāha — go; nija-ghara — to your own home; ethāya — here in this place; rahiba — shall stay; āmi — I; nā — not; yāba — shall go; ataḥpara — hereafter.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord told the brāhmaṇa, “Now you can go home. I shall stay here and shall not leave.”
