# CC Madhya 5.35

**Bengali:**

দেশে আসি’ দুইজনে গেলা নিজ-ঘরে ।
কত দিনে বড়-বিপ্র চিন্তিত অন্তরে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

deśe āsi’ dui-jane gelā nija-ghare
kata dine baḍa-vipra cintita antare

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
deśe āsi’ — after returning to their own country; dui-jane — both of them; gelā — went; nija-ghare — to their respective homes; kata dine — after some time; baḍa-vipra — the elderly brāhmaṇa; antare — within.

**Translation:**

Translation
After returning to Vidyānagara, each brāhmaṇa went to his respective home. After some time, the elderly brāhmaṇa became very anxious.
