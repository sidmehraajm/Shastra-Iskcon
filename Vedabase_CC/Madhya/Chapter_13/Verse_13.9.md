# CC Madhya 13.9

**Bengali:**

কতক দয়িতা করে স্কন্ধ আলম্বন ।
কতক দয়িতা ধরে শ্রীপদ্ম-চরণ ॥ ৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kataka dayitā kare skandha ālambana
kataka dayitā dhare śrī-padma-caraṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kataka dayitā — some of the dayitās; skandha — of the shoulders; ālambana — capturing; kataka — some; dayitā — servants called dayitās; śrī-padma-caraṇa — the lotus feet of the Lord.

**Translation:**

Translation
While carrying the Deity of Lord Jagannātha, some of the dayitās took hold of the shoulders of the Lord, and some caught His lotus feet.
