# CC Madhya 13.20

**Bengali:**

শত শত সু-চামর-দর্পণে উজ্জ্বল ।
উপরে পতাকা শোভে চাঁদোয়া নির্মল ॥ ২০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śata śata su-cāmara-darpaṇe ujjvala
upare patākā śobhe cāṅdoyā nirmala

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śata śata — hundreds upon hundreds; su-cāmara — beautiful white whisks; darpaṇe — with mirrors; ujjvala — very bright; upare — on the top; patākā — flag; śobhe — looks beautiful; cāṅdoyā — canopy; nirmala — thoroughly cleansed.

**Translation:**

Translation
The decorations included bright mirrors and hundreds and hundreds of cāmaras [white whisks made of yak tails]. On top of the car were a neat and clean canopy and a very beautiful flag.
