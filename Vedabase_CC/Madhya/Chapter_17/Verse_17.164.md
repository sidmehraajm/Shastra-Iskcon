# CC Madhya 17.164

**Bengali:**

তবে মহাপ্রভু সেই ব্রাহ্মণে লঞা ।
তাঁহারে পুছিলা কিছু নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe mahāprabhu sei brāhmaṇe lañā
tāṅhāre puchilā kichu nibhṛte vasiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — after that; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; sei — that; tāṅhāre — unto him; puchilā — inquired; kichu — something; nibhṛte vasiyā — sitting in a solitary place.

**Translation:**

Translation
After this, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu took the brāhmaṇa aside. Sitting in a solitary place, the Lord began to question him.
