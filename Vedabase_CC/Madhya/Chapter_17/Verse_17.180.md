# CC Madhya 17.180

**Bengali:**

তথাপি পুরী দেখি’ তাঁর ‘বৈষ্ণব’-আচার ।
‘শিষ্য’ করি’ তাঁর ভিক্ষা কৈল অঙ্গীকার ॥ ১৮০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tathāpi purī dekhi’ tāṅra ‘vaiṣṇava’-ācāra
‘śiṣya’ kari’ tāṅra bhikṣā kaila aṅgīkāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tathāpi — still; purī — Mādhavendra Purī; dekhi’ — after seeing; tāṅra — of the brāhmaṇa; śiṣya kari’ — accepting him as his disciple; tāṅra bhikṣā — food offered by him; kaila aṅgīkāra — accepted.

**Translation:**

Translation
Although the brāhmaṇa belonged to the Sanoḍiyā community, Śrīla Mādhavendra Purī had seen that he behaved like a Vaiṣṇava and had therefore accepted him as his disciple. The food he had cooked had also been accepted by Mādhavendra Purī.
