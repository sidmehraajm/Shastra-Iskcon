# CC Madhya 17.17

**Bengali:**

ইঁহার সঙ্গে আছে বিপ্র এক ‘ভৃত্য’ ।
ইঁহো পথে করিবেন সেবা-ভিক্ষা-কৃত্য ॥ ১৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

iṅhāra saṅge āche vipra eka ‘bhṛtya’
iṅho pathe karibena sevā-bhikṣā-kṛtya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
iṅhāra saṅge — with him; āche — is; bhṛtya — servant; iṅho — this man; pathe — on the way; karibena — will do; sevā — service; bhikṣā-kṛtya — and arrangements for cooking.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In addition, You may take another brāhmaṇa who will act as a servant en route and make arrangements for Your food.
