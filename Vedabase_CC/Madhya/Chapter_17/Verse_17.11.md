# CC Madhya 17.11

**Bengali:**

‘উত্তম ব্রাহ্মণ’ এক সঙ্গে অবশ্য চাহি ।
ভিক্ষা করি’ ভিক্ষা দিবে, যাবে পাত্র বহি’ ॥ ১১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘uttama brāhmaṇa’ eka saṅge avaśya cāhi
bhikṣā kari’ bhikṣā dibe, yābe pātra vahi’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
uttama brāhmaṇa — a high-class brāhmaṇa; saṅge — along; avaśya — certainly; cāhi — we want; bhikṣā kari’ — collecting alms; bhikṣā dibe — will give You food; yābe — will go; pātra vahi’ — bearing Your waterpot.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Our Lord, please take one very nice brāhmaṇa with You. He will collect alms for You, cook for You, give You prasādam and carry Your waterpot while traveling.
