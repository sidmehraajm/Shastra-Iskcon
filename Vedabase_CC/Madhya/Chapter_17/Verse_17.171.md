# CC Madhya 17.171

**Bengali:**

শুনিয়া বিস্মিত বিপ্র কহে ভয় পাঞা ।
ঐছে বাত্‌ কহ কেনে সন্ন্যাসী হঞা ॥ ১৭১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śuniyā vismita vipra kahe bhaya pāñā
aiche bāt kaha kene sannyāsī hañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuniyā — after hearing; vismita — astonished; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; bhaya pāñā — being afraid; aiche bāt — such a statement; kaha — You say; kene — why; sannyāsī hañā — although You are a sannyāsī..

**Translation:**

Translation
Upon hearing this, the brāhmaṇa became afraid. He then said, “Why do You speak like this? You are a sannyāsī.
