# CC Madhya 4.152

**Bengali:**

এক বিপ্র, এক সেবক, চন্দন বহিতে ।
পুরী-গোসাঞির সঙ্গে দিল সম্বল-সহিতে ॥ ১৫২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka vipra, eka sevaka, candana vahite
purī-gosāñira saṅge dila sambala-sahite

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka vipra — one brāhmaṇa; candana — the sandalwood; vahite — to carry; purī-gosāñira — Mādhavendra Purī; saṅge — with; dila — gave; sambala-sahite — with the necessary expenditure.

**Translation:**

Translation
One brāhmaṇa and one servant were given to Mādhavendra Purī just to carry the sandalwood. He was also given the necessary traveling expenses.
