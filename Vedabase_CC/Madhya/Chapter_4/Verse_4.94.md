# CC Madhya 4.94

**Bengali:**

পূর্বদিন-প্রায় বিপ্র করিল রন্ধন ।
তৈছে অন্নকূট গোপাল করিল ভোজন ॥ ৯৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

pūrva-dina-prāya vipra karila randhana
taiche anna-kūṭa gopāla karila bhojana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pūrva-dina-prāya — almost as on the previous day; vipra — all the brāhmaṇas; randhana — cooking; taiche — similarly; anna-kūṭa — heaps of food; gopāla — the Deity of Lord Gopāla; karila — did; bhojana — eating.

**Translation:**

Translation
The next day, almost as before, there was an Annakūṭa ceremony. All the brāhmaṇas prepared foods and Gopāla accepted them.
