# CC Madhya 4.209

**Bengali:**

নাম-সংকীর্তনে সেই রাত্রি গোঙাইলা ।
মঙ্গল-আরতি দেখি’ প্রভাতে চলিলা ॥ ২০৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nāma-saṅkīrtane sei rātri goṅāilā
maṅgala-ārati dekhi’ prabhāte calilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nāma-saṅkīrtane — in congregational chanting; sei — that; rātri — night; goṅāilā — passed; maṅgala-ārati — the early ārati; prabhāte — in the morning; calilā — departed.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu passed that night at the temple engaged in congregational chanting. In the morning, after seeing the maṅgala-ārati performance, He departed.
