# CC Madhya 4.11

**Bengali:**

ভিক্ষা লাগি’ একদিন এক গ্রাম গিয়া ।
আপনে বহুত অন্ন আনিল মাগিয়া ॥ ১১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhikṣā lāgi’ eka-dina eka grāma giyā
āpane bahuta anna ānila māgiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhikṣā lāgi’ — for prasādam; eka grāma — to one village; giyā — going; āpane — personally; bahuta — a great quantity; anna — of rice and other eatables; ānila — brought; māgiyā — begging.

**Translation:**

Translation
Each day Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu personally went to a village and collected a great quantity of rice and other grains for the preparation of prasādam.
