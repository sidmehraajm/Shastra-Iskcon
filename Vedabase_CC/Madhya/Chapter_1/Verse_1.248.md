# CC Madhya 1.248

**Bengali:**

মধ্যলীলার কৈলুঁ এই সূত্র-বিবরণ ।
অন্ত্যলীলার সূত্র এবে শুন, ভক্তগণ ॥ ২৪৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

madhya-līlāra kailuṅ ei sūtra-vivaraṇa
antya-līlāra sūtra ebe śuna, bhakta-gaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
madhya-līlāra — of the madhya-līlā, the middle portion of His pastimes; kailuṅ — I have made; ei — this; sūtra — synopsis; vivaraṇa — description; antya-līlāra — of the pastimes at the end, known as antya-līlā; ebe — now; śuna — hear; bhakta-gaṇa — all devotees.

**Translation:**

Translation
Thus I have given a synopsis of the madhya-līlā, the middle pastimes of the Lord. Now, O devotees, kindly hear the synopsis of the final pastimes of the Lord, known as the antya-līlā.
