# CC Madhya 1.172

**Bengali:**

ভিখারী সন্ন্যাসী করে তীর্থ পর্যটন ।
তাঁরে দেখিবারে আইসে দুই চারি জন ॥ ১৭২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhikhārī sannyāsī kare tīrtha paryaṭana
tāṅre dekhibāre āise dui cāri jana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhikhāri — beggar; sannyāsī — mendicant; kare — does; tīrtha — of holy places; paryaṭana — touring; tāṅre — Him; dekhibāre — to see; āise — come; dui cāri jana — only a few people.

**Translation:**

Translation
Keśava Chatrī informed the Muslim king that Caitanya Mahāprabhu was a mendicant touring different places of pilgrimage and that, as such, only a few people came to see Him.
