# CC Madhya 1.261

**Bengali:**

তুষ্ট হঞা প্রভু তাঁরে পাঠাইলা বৃন্দাবন ।
অদ্বৈতের হস্তে প্রভুর অদ্ভুত ভোজন ॥ ২৬১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tuṣṭa hañā prabhu tāṅre pāṭhāilā vṛndāvana
advaitera haste prabhura adbhuta bhojana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tuṣṭa hañā — being very pleased; prabhu — the Lord; tāṅre — him; pāṭhāilā — sent back; vṛndāvana — to Vṛndāvana; advaitera — of Advaita Ācārya; haste — in the hands; prabhura — of the Lord; adbhuta — wonderful; bhojana — feasting.

**Translation:**

Translation
Being pleased, the Lord sent Sanātana Gosvāmī back to Vṛndāvana. After that, He was fed wonderfully by the hands of Śrī Advaita Ācārya.
