# CC Madhya 11.236

**Bengali:**

গজপতি রাজা শুনি’ কীর্তন-মহত্ত্ব ।
অট্টালিকা চড়ি’ দেখে স্বগণ-সহিত ॥ ২৩৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gajapati rājā śuni’ kīrtana-mahattva
aṭṭālikā caḍi’ dekhe svagaṇa-sahita

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gajapati rājā — the King of Orissa; śuni’ — hearing; kīrtana-mahattva — the greatness of saṅkīrtana; dekhe — sees; svagaṇa-sahita — along with his personal associates.

**Translation:**

Translation
Hearing the greatness of the saṅkīrtana, King Pratāparudra went up to the top of his palace and watched the performance with his personal associates.
