# CC Madhya 11.237

**Bengali:**

কীর্তন দেখিয়া রাজার হৈল চমৎকার ।
প্রভুকে মিলিতে উত্কণ্ঠা বাড়িল অপার ॥ ২৩৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kīrtana dekhiyā rājāra haila camatkāra
prabhuke milite utkaṇṭhā bāḍila apāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kīrtana dekhiyā — seeing the performance of kīrtana; haila — there was; camatkāra — astonishment; prabhuke — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; milite — to meet; utkaṇṭhā — anxiety; bāḍila — increased; apāra — unlimitedly.

**Translation:**

Translation
The King was very much astonished to see Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s kīrtana, and the King’s anxiety to meet Him increased unlimitedly.
