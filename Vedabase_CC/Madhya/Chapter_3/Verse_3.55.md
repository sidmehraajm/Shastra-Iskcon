# CC Madhya 3.55

**Bengali:**

দুই পাশে ধরিল সব মৃৎকুণ্ডিকা ভরি’ ।
চাঁপাকলা-দধি-সন্দেশ কহিতে না পারি ॥ ৫৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

dui pāśe dharila saba mṛt-kuṇḍikā bhari’
cāṅpākalā-dadhi-sandeśa kahite nā pāri

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dui pāśe — on two sides; dharila — kept; saba — all; mṛt-kuṇḍikā — earthen pots; bhari’ — filling; cāṅpā-kalā — a kind of banana known as cāṅpā-kalā; nā — not; pāri — I am able.

**Translation:**

Translation
In two places there were earthen pots filled with another preparation made with yogurt, sandeśa [a sweetmeat made with curd] and banana. I am unable to describe it all.
