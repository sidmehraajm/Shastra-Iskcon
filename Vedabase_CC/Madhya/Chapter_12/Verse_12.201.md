# CC Madhya 12.201

**Bengali:**

প্রভুর অবশেষ গোবিন্দ রাখিল ধরিয়া ।
সেই অন্ন হরিদাসে কিছু দিল লঞা ॥ ২০১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhura avaśeṣa govinda rākhila dhariyā
sei anna haridāse kichu dila lañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhura — of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; avaśeṣa — remnants; govinda — Govinda; rākhila — saved; dhariyā — keeping; sei anna — that prasādam; kichu — some; dila — delivered; lañā — taking.

**Translation:**

Translation
Govinda saved some remnants of food left by Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and kept them carefully. Later, one portion of these remnants was delivered to Haridāsa Ṭhākura.
