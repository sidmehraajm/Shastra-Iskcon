# CC Madhya 12.200

**Bengali:**

তবে পরিবেশক স্বরূপাদি সাত জন ।
গৃহের ভিতরে কৈল প্রসাদ ভোজন ॥ ২০০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe pariveśaka svarūpādi sāta jana
gṛhera bhitare kaila prasāda bhojana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — thereafter; pariveśaka — the distributors of prasādam; sāta jana — seven men; gṛhera bhitare — within the room; kaila — did; prasāda bhojana — eating of prasādam..

**Translation:**

Translation
The seven persons headed by Svarūpa Dāmodara who were engaged in distributing prasādam to others then took their meals within the room.
