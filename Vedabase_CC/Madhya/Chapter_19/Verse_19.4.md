# CC Madhya 19.4

**Bengali:**

দুইভাই বিষয়-ত্যাগের উপায় সৃজিল ।
বহুধন দিয়া দুই ব্রাহ্মণে বরিল ॥ ৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

dui-bhāi viṣaya-tyāgera upāya sṛjila
bahu-dhana diyā dui brāhmaṇe varila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dui-bhāi — the two brothers; viṣaya-tyāgera — of giving up material activities; upāya sṛjila — discovered a means; bahu-dhana — much money; diyā — paying; dui brāhmaṇe — two brāhmaṇas.

**Translation:**

Translation
The two brothers devised a means whereby they could give up their material activities. For this purpose, they appointed two brāhmaṇas and paid them a large amount of money.
