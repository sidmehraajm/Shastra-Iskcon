# CC Madhya 14.94

**Bengali:**

অপরাহ্ণে আসি’ কৈল দর্শন, নর্তন ।
নিশাতে উদ্যানে আসি’ করিলা শয়ন ॥ ৯৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

aparāhne āsi’ kaila darśana, nartana
niśāte udyāne āsi’ karilā śayana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aparāhne — in the afternoon; āsi’ — coming; kaila — performed; darśana nartana — visiting the Lord and dancing; niśāte — at night; udyāne — in the garden; āsi’ — coming; karilā śayana — took rest.

**Translation:**

Translation
In the afternoon, Śri Caitanya Mahāprabhu went to the Guṇḍicā temple to visit the Lord and dance. At night He went to the garden to take rest.
