# CC Madhya 14.193

**Bengali:**

ললিত-ভূষিত রাধা দেখে যদি কৃষ্ণ ।
দুঁহে দুঁহা মিলিবারে হয়েন সতৃষ্ণ ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

lalita-bhūṣita rādhā dekhe yadi kṛṣṇa
duṅhe duṅhā milibāre hayena satṛṣṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
lalita-bhūṣita — decorated with lalita-alaṅkāra; dekhe — sees; yadi — if; kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; duṅhe — both of Them; duṅhā — the two of Them; milibāre — to meet; hayena — become; sa-tṛṣṇa — very eager.

**Translation:**

Translation
“When Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa happens to see Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī decorated with these lalita ornaments, They both eagerly want to meet each other.
