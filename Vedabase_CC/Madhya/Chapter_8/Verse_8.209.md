# CC Madhya 8.209

**Bengali:**

রাধার স্বরূপ — কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-কল্পলতা ।
সখীগণ হয় তার পল্লব-পুষ্প-পাতা ॥ ২০৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

rādhāra svarūpa — kṛṣṇa-prema-kalpalatā
sakhī-gaṇa haya tāra pallava-puṣpa-pātā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rādhāra svarūpa — the spiritual nature of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; kṛṣṇa-prema — of love of Kṛṣṇa; kalpa-latā — a creeper; sakhī-gaṇa — the gopīs; tāra — of that creeper; pallava — the twigs; puṣpa — flowers; pātā — and leaves.

**Translation:**

Translation
“By nature, Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī is just like a creeper of love of Godhead, and the gopīs are the twigs, flowers and leaves of that creeper.
