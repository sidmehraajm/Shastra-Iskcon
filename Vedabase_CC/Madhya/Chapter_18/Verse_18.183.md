# CC Madhya 18.183

**Bengali:**

প্রভু কহেন, — ঠক্ নহে, মোর ‘সঙ্গী’ জন ।
ভিক্ষুক সন্ন্যাসী, মোর নাহি কিছু ধন ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahena, — ṭhak nahe, mora ‘saṅgī’ jana
bhikṣuka sannyāsī, mora nāhi kichu dhana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahena — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu replied; ṭhak nahe — they are not rogues; mora saṅgī jana — My associates; bhikṣuka — beggar; nāhi — are not; kichu — any; dhana — riches.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said, “These are not rogues. They are My associates. Being a sannyāsī beggar, I do not possess anything.
