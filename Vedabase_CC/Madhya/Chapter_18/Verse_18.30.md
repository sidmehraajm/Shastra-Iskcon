# CC Madhya 18.30

**Bengali:**

বিপ্রগৃহে গোপালের নিভৃতে সেবন ।
গ্রাম উজাড় হৈল, পলাইল সর্বজন ॥ ৩০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vipra-gṛhe gopālera nibhṛte sevana
grāma ujāḍa haila, palāila sarva-jana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vipra-gṛhe — in the house of a brāhmaṇa; nibhṛte — very secretly; sevana — worship; grāma — the village; ujāḍa haila — became deserted; palāila — fled; sarva-jana — all the people.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Gopāla Deity was kept in the house of a brāhmaṇa, and His worship was conducted secretly. Everyone fled, and thus the village of Annakūṭa was deserted.
