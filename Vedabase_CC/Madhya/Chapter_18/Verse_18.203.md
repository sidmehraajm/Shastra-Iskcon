# CC Madhya 18.203

**Bengali:**

তোমা দেখি’ জিহ্বা মোর বলে ‘কৃষ্ণনাম’ ।
‘আমি — বড় জ্ঞানী’ — এই গেল অভিমান ॥ ২০৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tomā dekhi’ jihvā mora bale ‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’
‘āmi — baḍa jñānī’ — ei gela abhimāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tomā dekhi’ — by seeing You; jihvā — tongue; mora — my; bale kṛṣṇa-nāma — chants the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; baḍa jñānī — very learned scholar; ei — this; gela abhimāna — false prestige has gone away.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Now that I have seen You, my tongue is chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra. The false prestige I felt from being a learned scholar is now gone.”
