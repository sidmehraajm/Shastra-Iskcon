# CC Madhya 25.25

**Bengali:**

উপনিষদের করেন মুখ্যার্থ ব্যাখ্যান ।
শুনিয়া পণ্ডিত-লোকের জুড়ায় মন-কাণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

upaniṣadera karena mukhyārtha vyākhyāna
śuniyā paṇḍita-lokera juḍāya mana-kāṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
upaniṣadera — of the Vedic version known as the Upaniṣads; mukhya-artha — the original meaning; vyākhyāna — explanation; śuniyā — hearing; paṇḍita-lokera — of the learned scholars; juḍāya — satisfies; mana-kāṇa — the minds and ears.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu explains the direct meaning of the Upaniṣads. When all learned scholars hear this, their minds and ears are satisfied.
