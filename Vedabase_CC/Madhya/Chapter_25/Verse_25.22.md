# CC Madhya 25.22

**Bengali:**

প্রভুরে প্রণত হৈল সন্ন্যাসীর গণ ।
আত্মমধ্যে গোষ্ঠী করে ছাড়ি’ অধ্যয়ন ॥ ২২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhure praṇata haila sannyāsīra gaṇa
ātma-madhye goṣṭhī kare chāḍi’ adhyayana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhure — unto Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; praṇata haila — offered obeisances; sannyāsīra gaṇa — all the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs; goṣṭhī kare — discussed; chāḍi’ adhyayana — giving up so-called studies of Vedānta.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs offered their obeisances unto Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and then began to discuss His movement, giving up their studies of Vedānta and Māyāvāda philosophy.
