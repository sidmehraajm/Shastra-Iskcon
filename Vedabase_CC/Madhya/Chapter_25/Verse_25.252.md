# CC Madhya 25.252

**Bengali:**

ত্রয়োদশে — রথ-আগে প্রভুর নর্তন ।
চতুর্দশে — ‘হেরাপঞ্চমী’-যাত্রা-দরশন ॥ ২৫২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

trayodaśe — ratha-āge prabhura nartana
caturdaśe — ‘herā-pañcamī’-yātrā-daraśana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
trayodaśe — in the thirteenth chapter; ratha-āge — in front of the Jagannātha ratha; caturdaśe — in the fourteenth chapter; herā-pañcamī — Herā-pañcamī, which takes place on the fifth day of Ratha-yātrā; yātrā — festival; daraśana — visiting.

**Translation:**

Translation
In the thirteenth chapter I have described Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s dancing before the chariot of Jagannātha. In the fourteenth chapter, there is an account of the Herā-pañcamī function.
