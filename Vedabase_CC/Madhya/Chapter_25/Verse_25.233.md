# CC Madhya 25.233

**Bengali:**

জগন্নাথ-সেবক আনি’ মালা-প্রসাদ দিলা ।
তুলসী পড়িছা আসি’ চরণ বন্দিলা ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

jagannātha-sevaka āni’ mālā-prasāda dilā
tulasī paḍichā āsi’ caraṇa vandilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jagannātha-sevaka — the priests who were servitors of Lord Jagannātha; āni’ — bringing; mālā-prasāda dilā — distributed flower garlands and prasādam; āsi’ — coming; caraṇa vandilā — worshiped the lotus feet of the Lord.

**Translation:**

Translation
The priests immediately brought them flower garlands and prasādam. The temple’s watchman, who was named Tulasī, also came and offered his obeisances to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
