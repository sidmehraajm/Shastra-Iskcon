# CC Madhya 6.72

**Bengali:**

সার্বভৌম কহে, — ‘ইঁহার নাম সর্বোত্তম ।
ভারতী সম্প্রদায় ইঁহো — হয়েন মধ্যম ॥’ ৭২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sārvabhauma kahe, — ‘iṅhāra nāma sarvottama
bhāratī-sampradāya iṅho — hayena madhyama’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sārvabhauma kahe — Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya replied; iṅhāra — His; nāma — name; sarva-uttama — first-class; bhāratī-sampradāya — the community of the Bhāratī sannyāsīs; hayena — becomes; madhyama — middle-class.

**Translation:**

Translation
Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya said, “ ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa’ is a very good name, but He belongs to the Bhāratī community. Therefore He is a second-class sannyāsī.”
