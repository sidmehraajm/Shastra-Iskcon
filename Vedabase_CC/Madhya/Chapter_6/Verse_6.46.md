# CC Madhya 6.46

**Bengali:**

এত বলি’ পীঠা-পানা সব খাওয়াইলা ।
ভিক্ষা করাঞা আচমন করাইলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta bali’ pīṭhā-pānā saba khāoyāilā
bhikṣā karāñā ācamana karāilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bali’ — saying this; pīṭhā-pānā — many kinds of cakes and condensed-milk preparations; saba — all; khāoyāilā — made to eat; bhikṣā karāñā — after offering prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
After saying this, he made them all eat the various cakes and condensed-milk preparations. After feeding them, he offered them water to wash their hands, feet and mouths.
