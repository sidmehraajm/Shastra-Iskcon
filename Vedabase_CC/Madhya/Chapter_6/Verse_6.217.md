# CC Madhya 6.217

**Bengali:**

পূজারী আনিয়া মালা-প্রসাদান্ন দিলা ।
প্রসাদান্ন-মালা পাঞা প্রভু হর্ষ হৈলা ॥ ২১৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

pūjārī āniyā mālā-prasādānna dilā
prasādānna-mālā pāñā prabhu harṣa hailā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pūjārī — the priest; āniyā — bringing; mālā — garlands; prasāda-anna — remnants of food; dilā — offered; prasāda-anna — the prasādam; pāñā — getting; prabhu — Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; harṣa — pleased; hailā — became.

**Translation:**

Translation
The priest there presented Him with garlands and prasādam that had been offered to Lord Jagannātha. This pleased Caitanya Mahāprabhu very much.
