# CC Madhya 6.275

**Bengali:**

যদ্যপি ‘মুক্তি’-শব্দের হয় পঞ্চ বৃত্তি ।
রূঢ়িবৃত্ত্যে কহে তবু ‘সাযুজ্যে’ প্রতীতি ॥ ২৭৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yadyapi ‘mukti’-śabdera haya pañca vṛtti
rūḍhi-vṛttye kahe tabu ‘sāyujye’ pratīti

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yadyapi — although; mukti-śabdera — of the word mukti; pañca vṛtti — five meanings; rūḍhi-vṛttye — by the chief or direct meaning; kahe — it says; tabu — still; sāyujye — to become one with the Supreme; pratīti — the conception.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘mukti’ refers to five kinds of liberation. But its direct meaning usually conveys the idea of becoming one with the Lord.
