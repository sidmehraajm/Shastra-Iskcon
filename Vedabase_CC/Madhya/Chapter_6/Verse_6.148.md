# CC Madhya 6.148

**Bengali:**

বেদের নিগূঢ় অর্থ বুঝন না হয় ।
পুরাণ-বাক্যে সেই অর্থ করয় নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vedera nigūḍha artha bujhana nā haya
purāṇa-vākye sei artha karaya niścaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vedera — of the Vedic literature; nigūḍha — confidential; artha — meaning; bujhana — understanding; nā — not; haya — is; purāṇa-vākye — by the words of the Purāṇas; artha — meaning; karaya — makes; niścaya — certain.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The confidential meaning of the Vedas is not easily understood by common men; therefore that meaning is supplemented by the words of the Purāṇas.
