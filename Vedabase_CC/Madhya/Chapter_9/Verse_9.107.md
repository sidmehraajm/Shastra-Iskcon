# CC Madhya 9.107

**Bengali:**

সেই বিপ্র মহাপ্রভুর বড় ভক্ত হৈল ।
চারি মাস প্রভু-সঙ্গ কভু না ছাড়িল ॥ ১০৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei vipra mahāprabhura baḍa bhakta haila
cāri māsa prabhu-saṅga kabhu nā chāḍila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei vipra — that brāhmaṇa; baḍa — big; bhakta — devotee; haila — became; cāri māsa — for four months; prabhu-saṅga — association of the Lord; kabhu — at any time; nā — did not; chāḍila — give up.

**Translation:**

Translation
That brāhmaṇa became a great devotee of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, and for four continuous months he did not give up the Lord’s company.
