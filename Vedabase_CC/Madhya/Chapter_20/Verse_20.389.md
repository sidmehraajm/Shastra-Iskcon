# CC Madhya 20.389

**Bengali:**

সূর্যোদয় হৈতে ষষ্টিপল-ক্রমোদয় ।
সেই এক দণ্ড, অষ্ট দণ্ডে ‘প্রহর’ হয় ॥ ৩৮৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sūryodaya haite ṣaṣṭi-pala-kramodaya
sei eka daṇḍa, aṣṭa daṇḍe ‘prahara’ haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sūrya-udaya haite — beginning from the sunrise; ṣaṣṭi-pala — sixty palas; sei — that; eka daṇḍa — one daṇḍa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The sun rises in steps consisting of sixty palas. Sixty palas equal one daṇḍa, and eight daṇḍas comprise one prahara.
