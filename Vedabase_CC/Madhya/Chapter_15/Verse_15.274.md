# CC Madhya 15.274

**Bengali:**

সহজে নির্মল এই ‘ব্রাহ্মণ’-হৃদয় ।
কৃষ্ণের বসিতে এই যোগ্যস্থান হয় ॥ ২৭৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sahaje nirmala ei ‘brāhmaṇa’-hṛdaya
kṛṣṇera vasite ei yogya-sthāna haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sahaje — by nature; nirmala — without contamination; ei — this; brāhmaṇa-hṛdaya — heart of a brāhmaṇa; vasite — to sit down; ei — this; yogya-sthāna — proper place; haya — is.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The heart of a brāhmaṇa is by nature very clean; therefore it is a proper place for Kṛṣṇa to sit.
