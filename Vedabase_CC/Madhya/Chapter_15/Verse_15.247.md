# CC Madhya 15.247

**Bengali:**

তেঁহো যদি প্রসাদ দিতে হৈলা আন-মন ।
অমোঘ আসি’ অন্ন দেখি’ করয়ে নিন্দন ॥ ২৪৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

teṅho yadi prasāda dite hailā āna-mana
amogha āsi’ anna dekhi’ karaye nindana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
teṅho — he (the Bhaṭṭācārya); yadi — when; prasāda dite — supplying the prasādam; āna-mana — inattentive; amogha — Amogha; āsi’ — coming; anna dekhi’ — seeing the food; karaye nindana — began blaspheming.

**Translation:**

Translation
However, as soon as the Bhaṭṭācārya began distributing prasādam and was a little inattentive, Amogha came in. Seeing the quantity of food, he began to blaspheme.
