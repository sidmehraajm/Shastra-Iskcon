# CC Madhya 10.111

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণরস-তত্ত্ব-বেত্তা, দেহ — প্রেমরূপ ।
সাক্ষাৎ মহাপ্রভুর দ্বিতীয় স্বরূপ ॥ ১১১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa-rasa-tattva-vettā, deha — prema-rūpa
sākṣāt mahāprabhura dvitīya svarūpa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa-rasa — of transcendental mellows in relationship with Kṛṣṇa; tattva — of the truth; vettā — cognizant; deha — body; prema-rūpa — personified prema; mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; dvitīya — second; svarūpa — representation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Svarūpa Dāmodara was the personification of ecstatic love, fully cognizant of the transcendental mellows in relationship with Kṛṣṇa. He directly represented Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu as His second expansion.
