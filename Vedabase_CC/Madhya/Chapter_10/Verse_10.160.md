# CC Madhya 10.160

**Bengali:**

আজি হৈতে না পরিব এই চর্মাম্বর ।
প্রভু বহির্বাস আনাইলা জানিয়া অন্তর ॥ ১৬০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āji haite nā pariba ei carmāmbara
prabhu bahirvāsa ānāilā jāniyā antara

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āji haite — from today; nā pariba — I shall not put on; ei — this; carma-ambara — deerskin garment; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bahir-vāsa — the cloth of a sannyāsī; jāniyā — knowing; antara — his contemplation.

**Translation:**

Translation
“From today on I shall not wear this deerskin.” As soon as Brahmānanda Bhāratī decided this, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, understanding his mind, immediately sent for the robes of a sannyāsī.
