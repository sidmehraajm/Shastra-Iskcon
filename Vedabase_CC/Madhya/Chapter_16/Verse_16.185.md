# CC Madhya 16.185

**Bengali:**

ইঁহার যে এই গতি, ইথে কি বিস্ময় ?
তোমার দর্শন-প্ৰভাব এইমত হয় ॥’ ১৮৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

iṅhāra ye ei gati, ithe ki vismaya?
tomāra darśana-prabhāva ei-mata haya’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
iṅhāra — of this Muslim governor; ye — which; ei — this; gati — result; ithe — in this; ki — what; vismaya — the wonder; tomāra — of You; darśana-prabhāva — influence of seeing; ei-mata haya — is like this.

**Translation:**

Translation
“It is no wonder that this Muslim governor has attained such results. Simply by seeing You, all this is possible.
