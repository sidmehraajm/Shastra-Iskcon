# CC Madhya 16.176

**Bengali:**

এত বলি’ বিশ্বাসেরে কহিল বচন ।
“ভাগ্য তাঁর — আসি’ করুক প্রভু দরশন ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta bali’ viśvāsere kahila vacana
“bhāgya tāṅra — āsi’ karuka prabhu daraśana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bali’ — saying this; viśvāsere — unto the secretary of the Muslim governor; kahila vacana — spoke the following words; bhāgya — great fortune; tāṅra — his; āsi’ — coming; karuka — let him do; prabhu daraśana — visiting Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
After thinking this, the mahā-pātra immediately informed the Muslim secretary, “It is a great fortune for your governor. Let him come visit Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
