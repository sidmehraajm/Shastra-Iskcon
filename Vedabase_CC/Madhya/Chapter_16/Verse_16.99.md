# CC Madhya 16.99

**Bengali:**

প্রসাদ ভোজন করি’ তথায় রহিলা ।
প্রাতঃকালে চলি’ প্রভু ‘ভুবনেশ্বর’ আইলা ॥ ৯৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prasāda bhojana kari’ tathāya rahilā
prātaḥ-kāle cali’ prabhu ‘bhuvaneśvara’ āilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prasāda bhojana kari’ — after taking the prasādam; prātaḥ-kāle — early in the morning; cali’ — walking; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhuvaneśvara āilā — reached the place known as Bhuvaneśvara.

**Translation:**

Translation
After taking prasādam, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu remained there for the night. Early in the morning He began walking, and finally He reached Bhuvaneśvara.
