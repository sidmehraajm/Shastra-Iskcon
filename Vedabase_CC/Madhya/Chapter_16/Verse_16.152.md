# CC Madhya 16.152

**Bengali:**

প্রতিগ্রামে রাজ-আজ্ঞায় রাজভৃত্যগণ ।
নব্য গৃহে নানা-দ্রব্যে করয়ে সেবন ॥ ১৫২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prati-grāme rāja-ājñāya rāja-bhṛtya-gaṇa
navya gṛhe nānā-dravye karaye sevana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prati-grāme — in each village; rāja-ājñāya — by the order of the King; rāja-bhṛtya-gaṇa — the government servants; navya gṛhe — in newly constructed houses; nānā-dravye — with all kinds of food grains; karaye sevana — rendered service.

**Translation:**

Translation
In each and every village, in compliance with the King’s order, government officers constructed new houses and filled each of them with stocks of grain. Thus they served the Lord.
