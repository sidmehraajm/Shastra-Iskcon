# CC Ādi 13.4

**Bengali:**

জয় দামোদর–স্বরপ জয় মুরারি গুপ্ত ।
এই সব চন্দ্রোদয়ে তমঃ কৈল লুপ্ত ॥ ৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

jaya dāmodara-svarūpa jaya murāri gupta
ei saba candrodaye tamaḥ kaila lupta

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jaya — all glories; dāmodara-svarūpa — Svarūpa Dāmodara; jaya — all glories; murāri gupta — Murāri Gupta; ei saba — of all these; candra-udaye — such moons having arisen; tamaḥ — darkness; kaila — made; lupta — dissipated.

**Translation:**

Translation
All glories to Svarūpa Dāmodara and Murāri Gupta! All these brilliant moons have together dissipated the darkness of this material world.
