# CC Ādi 13.9

**Bengali:**

চৌদ্দশত সাত শকে জন্মের প্রমাণ ।
চৌদ্দশত পঞ্চান্নে হইল অন্তর্ধান ॥ ৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

caudda-śata sāta śake janmera pramāṇa
caudda-śata pañcānne ha-ila antardhāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
caudda-śata-sata — 1407; śake — in the Śaka Era; janmera — of birth; pramāṇa — evidence; caudda-śata pañcānne — in the year 1455; ha-ila — became; antardhāna — disappearance.

**Translation:**

Translation
In the year 1407 of the Śaka Era (A.D. 1486), Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu appeared, and in the year 1455 (A.D. 1534) He disappeared from this world.
