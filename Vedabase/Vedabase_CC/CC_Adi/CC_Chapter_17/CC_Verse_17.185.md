# CC Ādi 17.185

**Bengali:**

ঐছে যদি পুনঃ কর, তবে না সহিমু ।
সবংশে তোমারে মারি যবন নাশিমু ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

aiche yadi punaḥ kara, tabe nā sahimu
savaṁśe tomāre māri yavana nāśimu

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aiche — similarly; yadi — if; punaḥ — again; kara — you do; tabe — then; nā</em> <em>sahimu — I shall not tolerate; sa-vaṁśe — along with your family; tomāre — you; māri — killing; yavana — the meat-eaters; nāśimu — I shall vanquish.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘But if you perform such activities again, I shall not be tolerant. At that time I shall kill you, your entire family and all the meat-eaters.’
