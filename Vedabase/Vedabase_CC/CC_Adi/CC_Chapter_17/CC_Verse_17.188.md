# CC Ādi 17.188

**Bengali:**

কাজী কহে—ইহা আমি কারে না কহিল ।
সেই দিন আমার এক পিয়াদা আইল ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kājī kahe, — ihā āmi kāre nā kahila
sei dina āmāra eka piyādā āila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kājī kahe — the Kazi said; ihā — this; āmi — I; kāre — to others; nā</em> <em>kahila — did not tell; sei dina — on that day; āmāra — my; eka — one; piyādā — orderly; āila — came to see me.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Kazi continued, “I did not speak to anyone about this incident, but on that very day one of my orderlies came to see me.
