# CC Ādi 17.207

**Bengali:**

উচ্চ করি’ গায় গীত, দেয় করতালি ।
মৃদঙ্গ–করতাল–শব্দে কর্ণে লাগে তালি ॥ ২০৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ucca kari’ gāya gīta, deya karatāli
mṛdaṅga-karatāla-śabde karṇe lāge tāli

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ucca — loud; kari’ — making; gāya — sings; gīta — songs; deya — practices; karatāli — clapping; mṛdaṅga — mṛdaṅga drum; karatāla — hand cymbals; śabde — by sounds; karṇe — in the ear; lāge — there is; tāli — blocking.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘Now He loudly sings all kinds of songs, claps, and plays drums and hand cymbals, making a tumultuous sound that deafens our ears.
