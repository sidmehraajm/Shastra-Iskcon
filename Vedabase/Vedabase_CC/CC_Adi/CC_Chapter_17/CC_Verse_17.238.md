# CC Ādi 17.238

**Bengali:**

তাহি মধ্যে ছয়ঋতু লীলার বর্ণন ।
মধুপান, রাসোৎসব, জলকেলি কথন ॥ ২৩৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tāhi madhye chaya-ṛtu līlāra varṇana
madhu-pāna, rāsotsava, jala-keli kathana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tāhi madhye — during that; chaya-ṛtu — the six seasons; līlāra — of the pastimes; varṇana — description; madhu-pāna — drinking of the honey; rāsa-utsava — dancing the rāsa-līlā; kathana — narrations.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita narrated all the pastimes enacted during the six changing seasons. He described the drinking of honey, the celebration of the rāsa dance, the swimming in the Yamunā and other such incidents.
