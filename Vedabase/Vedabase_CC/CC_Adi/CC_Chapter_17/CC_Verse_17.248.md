# CC Ādi 17.248

**Bengali:**

এক পড়ুয়া আইল প্রভুকে দেখিতে ।
‘গোপী’ ‘গোপী’ নাম শুনি’ লাগিল বলিতে ॥ ২৪৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka paḍuyā āila prabhuke dekhite
‘gopī’ ‘gopī’ nāma śuni’ lāgila balite

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka paḍuyā — one student; āila — came there; prabhuke — the Lord; dekhite — to see; gopī gopī — gopī gopī; śuni’ — hearing; lāgila — began; balite — to say.

**Translation:**

Translation
A student who came to see the Lord was astonished that the Lord was chanting “Gopī! Gopī!” Thus he spoke as follows.
