# CC Ādi 17.298

**Bengali:**

অদ্বৈত–আচার্য–গোসাঞি ভক্ত–অবতার ।
কৃষ্ণ অবতারিয়া কৈলা ভক্তির প্রচার ॥ ২৯৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

advaita-ācārya-gosāñi bhakta-avatāra
kṛṣṇa avatāriyā kailā bhaktira pracāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
advaita-ācārya — Śrī Advaita Prabhu; gosāñi — spiritual master; bhaktaavatāra — the incarnation of a devotee; kṛṣṇa — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; avatāriyā — descending; kailā — did; bhaktira — of devotional service; pracāra — propagation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Advaita Ācārya Prabhu appeared as an incarnation of a devotee. He is in the category of Kṛṣṇa, but He descended to this earth to propagate devotional service.
