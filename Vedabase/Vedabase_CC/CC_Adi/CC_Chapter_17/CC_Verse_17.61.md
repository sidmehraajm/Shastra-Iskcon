# CC Ādi 17.61

**Bengali:**

ফিরি’ গেল বিপ্র ঘরে মনে দুঃখ পাঞা ।
আর দিন প্রভুকে কহে গঙ্গায় লাগ পাঞা ॥ ৬১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

phiri’ gela vipra ghare mane duḥkha pāñā
āra dina prabhuke kahe gaṅgāya lāga pāñā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
phiri’ gela — went back; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; mane — within his mind; duḥkha — unhappiness; pāñā — getting; āra dina — the next day; prabhuke — unto the Lord; kahe — says; gaṅgāya — on the bank of the Ganges; lāga — touch; pāñā — getting.

**Translation:**

Translation
He returned home with an unhappy mind, but on the next day he met Lord Caitanya on the bank of the Ganges and spoke to Him.
