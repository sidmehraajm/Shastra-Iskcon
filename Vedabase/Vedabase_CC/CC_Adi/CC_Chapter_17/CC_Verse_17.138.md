# CC Ādi 17.138

**Bengali:**

বৃন্দাবনদাস ইহা ‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে’ ।
বিতারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন, প্রভু–কৃপাবলে ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vṛndāvana-dāsa ihā ‘caitanya-maṅgale’
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena, prabhu-kṛpā-bale

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vṛndāvana-dāsa — Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura; ihā — this; caitanyamaṅgale — in his book named Caitanya-maṅgala; varṇiyāchena — has described; prabhu — of the Lord; kṛpā-bale — by the strength of mercy.

**Translation:**

Translation
By the grace of the Lord, Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura has elaborately described this incident in his Caitanya-maṅgala [now Caitanya-bhāgavata].
