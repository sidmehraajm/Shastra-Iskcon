# CC Ādi 17.247

**Bengali:**

একদিন গোপীভাবে গৃহেতে বসিয়া ।
‘গোপী’ ‘গোপী’ নাম লয় বিষণ্ণ হঞা ॥ ২৪৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka-dina gopī-bhāve gṛhete vasiyā
‘gopī’ ‘gopī’ nāma laya viṣaṇṇa hañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka-dina — one day; gopī-bhāve — in the ecstasy of the gopīs; vasiyā — sitting; gopī gopī — gopī gopī; laya — chants; viṣaṇṇa — morose; hañā — becoming.

**Translation:**

Translation
One day the Lord, in the ecstasy of the gopīs, was sitting in His house. Very morose in separation, He was calling, “Gopī! Gopī!”
