# CC Ādi 17.60

**Bengali:**

আর এক বিপ্র আইল কীর্তন দেখিতে ।
দ্বারে কপাট,—না পাইল ভিতরে যাইতে ॥ ৬০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āra eka vipra āila kīrtana dekhite
dvāre kapāṭa, — nā pāila bhitare yāite

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āra — another; eka — one; vipra — brāhmaṇa; kīrtana — chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; dvāre — in the gateway; kapāṭa — the door (being closed); nā pāila — did not get; bhitare — inside; yāite — to go.

**Translation:**

Translation
Another brāhmaṇa came to see the kīrtana performance, but the door was closed, and he could not enter the hall.
