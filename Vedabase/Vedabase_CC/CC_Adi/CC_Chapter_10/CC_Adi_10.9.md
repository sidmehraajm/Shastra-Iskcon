# CC Ādi 10.9

**Bengali:**

শ্রীপতি, শ্রীনিধি—তাঁর দুই সহোদর ।
চারি ভাইর দাস–দাসী, গৃহ–পরিকর ॥ ৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śrīpati, śrīnidhi — tāṅra dui sahodara
cāri bhāira dāsa-dāsī, gṛha-parikara

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śrīpati — Śrīpati; śrīnidhi — Śrīnidhi; tāṅra — their; dui — two; sahodara — own brothers; cāri — four; bhāira — brothers; dāsa-dāsī — family members, menservants and maidservants; gṛha-parikara — all counted in one family.

**Translation:**

Translation
Their two brothers were named Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi. These four brothers and their servants and maidservants are considered one big branch.
