# CC Ādi 10.141

**Bengali:**

অঙ্গসেবা গোবিন্দেরে দিলেন ঈশ্বর ।
জগন্নাথ দেখিতে চলেন আগে কাশীশ্বর ॥ ১৪১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

aṅga-sevā govindere dilena īśvara
jagannātha dekhite calena āge kāśīśvara

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aṅga-sevā — taking care of the body; govindere — unto Govinda; dilena — He gave; īśvara — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; jagannātha — the Jagannātha Deity; dekhite — while going to visit; calena — goes; āge — in front; kāśīśvara — Kāśīśvara.

**Translation:**

Translation
Govinda cared for the body of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, whereas Kāśīśvara went in front of the Lord when He went to see Jagannātha in the temple.
