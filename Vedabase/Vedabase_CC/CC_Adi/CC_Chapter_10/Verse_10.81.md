# CC Ādi 10.81

**Bengali:**

বাণীনাথ বসু আদি যত গ্রামী জন ।
সবেই চৈতন্যভৃত্য,—চৈতন্য–প্রাণধন ॥ ৮১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vāṇīnātha vasu ādi yata grāmī jana
sabei caitanya-bhṛtya, — caitanya-prāṇadhana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vāṇīnātha vasu — Vāṇīnātha Vasu; ādi — heading the list; yata — all; grāmī — of the village; jana — inhabitants; sabei — all of them; caitanya-bhṛtya — servants of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; caitanya-prāṇa-dhana — their life and soul was Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the inhabitants of Kulīna-grāma village, headed by Vāṇīnātha Vasu, were servants of Lord Caitanya, who was their only life and wealth.
