# CC Ādi 10.5

**Bengali:**

যত যত মহান্ত কৈলা তাঁ–সবার গণন ।
কেহ করিবারে নারে জ্যেষ্ঠ–লঘু–ক্রম ॥ ৫॥

**Transliteration:**

yata yata mahānta kailā tāṅ-sabāra gaṇana
keha karibāre nāre jyeṣṭha-laghu-krama

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yata yata — as many as there are; mahānta — great devotees; kailā — made; tāṅ-sabāra — of all of them; gaṇana — counting; keha — all of them; karibāre</em> <em>nāre — can not do; jyeṣṭha — elder; laghu — younger; krama — chronology.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the great personalities in the line of Lord Caitanya enumerated these devotees, but they could not distinguish between the greater and the lesser.
