# CC Ādi 10.47

**Bengali:**

তাঁর লীলা বর্ণিয়াছেন বৃন্দাবনদাস ।
যেবা অবশিষ্ট, আগে করিব প্রকাশ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tāṅra līlā varṇiyāchena vṛndāvana-dāsa
yebā avaśiṣṭa, āge kariba prakāśa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tāṅra — his; līlā — pastimes; varṇiyāchena — described; vṛndāvana-dāsa — Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura; yebā — whatever; avaśiṣṭa — remained undescribed; āge — later in the book; kariba — I shall make; prakāśa — manifest.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura has vividly described the pastimes of Haridāsa Ṭhākura in his Caitanya-bhāgavata. Whatever has remained undescribed I shall try to explain later in this book.
