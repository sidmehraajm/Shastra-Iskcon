# CC Ādi 2.38

**Bengali:**

‘নার’–শব্দে কহে সর্বজীবের নিচয় ।
‘অয়ন’–শব্দেতে কহে তাহার আশ্রয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘nāra’-śabde kahe sarva jīvera nicaya
‘ayana’-śabdete kahe tāhāra āśraya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nāra-śabde — by the word nāra; sarva jīvera — of all living entities; nicaya — the assemblage; ayana-śabdete — by the word ayana; kahe — one means; tāhāra — of them; āśraya — the refuge.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘nāra’ refers to the aggregate of all the living beings, and the word ‘ayana’ refers to the refuge of them all.
