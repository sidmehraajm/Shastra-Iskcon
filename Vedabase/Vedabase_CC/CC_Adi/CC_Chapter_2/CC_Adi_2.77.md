# CC Ādi 2.77

**Bengali:**

যৈছে কহি,—এই বিপ্র পরম পণ্ডিত ।
বিপ্র—অনুবাদ, ইহার বিধেয়—পাণ্ডিত্য ॥ ৭৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yaiche kahi, — ei vipra parama paṇḍita
vipra — anuvāda, ihāra vidheya — pāṇḍitya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yaiche — just as; kahi — I say; ei — this; vipra — brāhmaṇa; paṇḍita — learned man; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; ihāra — of this; vidheya — predicate; pāṇḍitya — erudition.

**Translation:**

Translation
“For example, we may say, ‘This vipra is a greatly learned man.’ In this sentence, the vipra is the subject, and the predicate is his erudition.
