# CC Ādi 5.216

**Bengali:**

নিত্যানন্দ–দয়া মোরে তাঁরে দেখাইল ।
শ্রীরাধা–মদনমোহনে প্রভু করি’ দিল ॥ ২১৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nityānanda-dayā more tāṅre dekhāila
śrī-rādhā-madana-mohane prabhu kari’ dila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nityānanda-dayā — the mercy of Lord Nityānanda; more — unto me; tāṅre — Madana-mohana; dekhāila — showed; śrī-rādhā-madana-mohane — Rādhā-Madana-mohana; prabhu kari’ dila — gave as my Lord and master.

**Translation:**

Translation
The mercy of Lord Nityānanda showed me Śrī Madana-mohana and gave me Śrī Madana-mohana as my Lord and master.
