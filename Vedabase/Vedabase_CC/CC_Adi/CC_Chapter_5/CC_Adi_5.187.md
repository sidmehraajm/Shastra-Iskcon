# CC Ādi 5.187

**Bengali:**

চন্দনলেপিত–অঙ্গ, তিলক সুঠাম ।
মত্তগজ জিনি’ মদ–মন্থর পয়ান ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

candana-lepita-aṅga, tilaka suṭhāma
matta-gaja jini’ mada-manthara payāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
candana — with sandalwood pulp; lepita — smeared; aṅga — body; tilaka suṭhāma — nicely decorated with tilaka; jini’ — surpassing; mada-manthara — maddened by drinking; payāna — movement.

**Translation:**

Translation
His body was anointed with sandalwood pulp, and He was nicely decorated with tilaka. His movements surpassed those of a maddened elephant.
