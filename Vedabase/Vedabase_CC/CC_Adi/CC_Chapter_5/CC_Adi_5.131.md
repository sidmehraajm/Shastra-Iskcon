# CC Ādi 5.131

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণ যবে অবতরে সর্বাংশ–আশ্রয় ।
সর্বাংশ আসি’ তবে কৃষ্ণেতে মিলয় ॥ ১৩১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa yabe avatare sarvāṁśa-āśraya
sarvāṁśa āsi’ tabe kṛṣṇete milaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; yabe — when; avatare — descends; sarva-aṁśa-āśraya — the shelter of all other viṣṇu-tattvas; āsi’ — coming; tabe — at that time; kṛṣṇete — in Kṛṣṇa; milaya — join.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the Supreme Personality of Godhead Kṛṣṇa appears, He is the shelter of all plenary parts. Thus at that time all His plenary portions join in Him.
