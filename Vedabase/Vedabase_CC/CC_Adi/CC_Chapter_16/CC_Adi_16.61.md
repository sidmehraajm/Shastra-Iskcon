# CC Ādi 16.61

**Bengali:**

‘অবিমৃষ্ট–বিধেয়াংশ’—এই দোষের নাম ।
আর এক দোষ আছে, শুন সাবধান ॥ ৬১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa’ — ei doṣera nāma
āra eka doṣa āche, śuna sāvadhāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa — avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa; doṣera — of the fault; nāma — the name; āra — another; eka — one; doṣa — fault; āche — there is; śuna — hear; sāvadhāna — carefully.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Not only is there the fault avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa, but there is also another fault, which I shall point out to you. Kindly hear Me with great attention.
