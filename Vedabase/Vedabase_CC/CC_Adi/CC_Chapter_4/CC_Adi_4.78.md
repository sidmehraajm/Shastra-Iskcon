# CC Ādi 4.78

**Bengali:**

লক্ষ্মীগণ তাঁর বৈভব–বিলাসাংশরূপ ।
মহিষীগণ বৈভব–প্রকাশস্বরূপ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

lakṣmī-gaṇa tāṅra vaibhava-vilāsāṁśa-rūpa
mahiṣī-gaṇa vaibhava-prakāśa-svarūpa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
lakṣmī-gaṇa — the goddesses of fortune; tāṅra — Her; vaibhava-vilāsa — as vaibhava-vilāsa; rūpa — having the form; mahiṣī-gaṇa — the queens; vaibhava-prakāśa — of vaibhava-prakāśa.

**Translation:**

Translation
The goddesses of fortune are Her plenary portions, and they display the forms of vaibhava-vilāsa. The queens are of the nature of Her vaibhava-prakāśa.
