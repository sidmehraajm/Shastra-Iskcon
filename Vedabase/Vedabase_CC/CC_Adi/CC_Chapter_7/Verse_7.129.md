# CC Ādi 7.129

**Bengali:**

সর্বাশ্রয় ঈশ্বরের করি প্রণব উদ্দেশ ।
‘তত্ত্বমসি’–বাক্য হয় বেদের একদেশ ॥ ১২৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sarvāśraya īśvarera praṇava uddeśa
‘tat tvam asi’ — vākya haya vedera ekadeśa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sarva-āśraya — the reservoir of everything; īśvarera — of the Supreme Personality of Godhead; praṇava — oṁkāra; tat tvam asi — the Vedic mantra tat tvam asi (“you are the same”); vākya — statement; haya — becomes; vedera — of the Vedic literature; eka-deśa — partial understanding.

**Translation:**

Translation
“It is the purpose of the Supreme Personality of Godhead to present praṇava [oṁkāra] as the reservoir of all Vedic knowledge. The words ‘tat tvam asi’ are only a partial explanation of the Vedic knowledge.

**Purport:**

Purport
Tat tvam asi means “you are the same spiritual identity.”
