# CC Ādi 3.74

**Bengali:**

নিত্যানন্দ গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ হলধর ।
অদ্বৈত আচার্য গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ॥ ৭৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nityānanda gosāñi sākṣāt haladhara
advaita ācārya gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nityānanda gosāñi — Lord Nityānanda Gosāñi; sākṣāt — directly; hala-dhara — Lord Balarāma, the holder of the plow; advaita ācārya</em> <em>gosāñi — Śrī Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi; sākṣāt — directly; īśvara — the Personality of Godhead.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Nityānanda Gosāñi is directly Haladhara  , and Advaita Ācārya is the Personality of Godhead Himself.
