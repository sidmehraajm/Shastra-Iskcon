# CC Ādi 3.55

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণবর্ণ–শব্দের অর্থ দুই ত’ প্রমাণ ।
কৃষ্ণ বিনু তাঁর মুখে নাহি আইসে আন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa-varṇa-śabdera artha dui ta pramāṇa
kṛṣṇa vinu tāṅra mukhe nāhi āise āna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa-varṇa-śabdera — of the word kṛṣṇa-varṇa; dui — two; ta — certainly; pramāṇa — examples; kṛṣṇa — Kṛṣṇa; vinu — except for; tāṅra — of Him; mukhe — in the mouth; nāhi āise — does not come; āna — anything else.

**Translation:**

Translation
These are two meanings of the word “kṛṣṇa-varṇa.” Indeed, nothing else but Kṛṣṇa issues from His mouth.
