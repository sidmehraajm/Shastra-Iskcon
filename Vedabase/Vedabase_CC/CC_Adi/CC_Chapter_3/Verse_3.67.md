# CC Ādi 3.67

**Bengali:**

অঙ্গোপাঙ্গ অস্ত্র করে স্বকার্যসাধন ।
‘অঙ্গ’–শব্দের অর্থ আর শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āṅgopāṅga astra kare sva-kārya-sādhana
‘aṅga’-śabdera artha āra śuna diyā mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āṅga-upāṅga — plenary parts and associates; astra — weapons; kare — do; sva-kārya — of their own business; sādhana — as the accomplishment; aṅga-śabdera — of the word aṅga; āra — another; śuna — please hear; diyā — giving; mana — the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
His plenary parts and associates perform the work of weapons as their own specific duties. Please hear from me another meaning of the word “aṅga.”
