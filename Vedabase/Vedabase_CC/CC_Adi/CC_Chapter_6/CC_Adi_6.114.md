# CC Ādi 6.114

**Bengali:**

সংকীর্তন প্রচারিয়া সব জগৎ তারিল ।
অদ্বৈত–প্রসাদে লোক প্রেমধন পাইল ॥ ১১৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

saṅkīrtana pracāriyā saba jagat tārila
advaita-prasāde loka prema-dhana pāila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saṅkīrtana pracāriyā — by preaching the cult of saṅkīrtana; jagat — the universe; tārila — delivered; advaita-prasāde — by the mercy of Advaita Ācārya; loka — all people; prema-dhana pāila — received the treasure of loving God.

**Translation:**

Translation
He liberated the universe by preaching saṅkīrtana. Thus the people of the world received the treasure of love of Godhead through the mercy of Śrī Advaita.
