# CC Ādi 6.22

**Bengali:**

সেই নারায়ণের মুখ্য অঙ্গ,—অদ্বৈত ।
‘অঙ্গ’–শব্দে অংশ করি’ কহে ভাগবত ॥ ২২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei nārāyaṇera mukhya aṅga, — advaita
‘aṅga’-śabde aṁśa kari’ kahe bhāgavata

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei — that; nārāyaṇera — of Lord Nārāyaṇa; mukhya aṅga — the primary part; advaita — Advaita Ācārya; aṅga-śabde — by the word aṅga; kahe — says; bhāgavata — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Advaita is the principal limb [aṅga] of Nārāyaṇa. Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam speaks of “limb” [aṅga] as “a plenary portion” [aṁśa] of the Lord.
