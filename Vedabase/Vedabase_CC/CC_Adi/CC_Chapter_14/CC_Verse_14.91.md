# CC Ādi 14.91

**Bengali:**

এত শুনি’ দ্বিজ গেলা হঞা আনন্দিত ।
মিশ্র জাগিয়া হইলা পরম বিস্মিত ॥ ৯১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta śuni’ dvija gelā hañā ānandita
miśra jāgiyā ha-ilā parama vismita

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta śuni’ — after hearing so much; dvija — the brāhmaṇa; hañā — becoming; ānandita — very pleased; miśra — Jagannātha Miśra; jāgiyā — being awakened; ha-ilā — became; parama — highly; vismita — astonished.

**Translation:**

Translation
Being very pleased, the brāhmaṇa left after talking with Jagannātha Miśra, and when Jagannātha Miśra awakened from his dream, he was very much astonished.
