# CC Ādi 8.47

**Bengali:**

বিস্তার দেখিয়া কিছু সঙ্কোচ হৈল মন ।
সূত্রধৃত কোন লীলা না কৈল বর্ণন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vistāra dekhiyā kichu saṅkoca haila mana
sūtra-dhṛta kona līlā nā kaila varṇana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vistāra — expansive; dekhiyā — seeing; kichu — some; saṅkoca — with hesitation; haila — became; mana — mind; sūtra-dhṛta — taking the synopsis; kona — some; līlā — pastimes; nā — did not; kaila — make; varṇana — description.

**Translation:**

Translation
He saw them to be so extensive that he later felt that some had not been properly described.
