# CC Ādi 8.76

**Bengali:**

সব বৈষ্ণবগণ হরিধ্বনি দিল ।
গোসাঞিদাস আনি’ মালা মোর গলে দিল ॥ ৭৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

saba vaiṣṇava-gaṇa hari-dhvani dila
gosāñi-dāsa āni’ mālā mora gale dila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saba — all; vaiṣṇava — devotees; gaṇa — group; hari-dhvani — chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa; dila — made; gosāñi-dāsa — Gosāñi dāsa; āni’ — bringing; mālā — garland; mora — my; gale — on the neck; dila — gave it.

**Translation:**

Translation
As soon as this happened, the Vaiṣṇavas standing there all loudly chanted, “Haribol!” and the priest, Gosāñi dāsa, brought me the garland and put it around my neck.
