# CC Ādi 8.34

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণলীলা ভাগবতে কহে বেদব্যাস ।
চৈতন্য–লীলার ব্যাস—বৃন্দাবন–দাস ॥ ৩৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa-līlā bhāgavate kahe veda-vyāsa
caitanya-līlāra vyāsa — vṛndāvana-dāsa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa-līlā — the pastimes of Lord Kṛṣṇa; bhāgavate — in the book Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; veda-vyāsa — Vyāsadeva, the editor of the Vedic literatures; caitanya-līlāra — of the pastimes of Lord Caitanya; vyāsa — compiler; vṛndāvana-dāsa — is Vṛndāvana dāsa.

**Translation:**

Translation
As Vyāsadeva has compiled all the pastimes of Lord Kṛṣṇa in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana dāsa has depicted the pastimes of Lord Caitanya.
