# CC Madhya 3.100

**Bengali:**

শতেক সন্ন্যাসী যদি করাহ ভোজন ।
তবে এই অপরাধ হইবে খণ্ডন ॥ ১০০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śateka sannyāsī yadi karāha bhojana
tabe ei aparādha ha-ibe khaṇḍana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śateka sannyāsī — one hundred sannyāsīs; karāha — You make; bhojana — the eating; tabe — then; ei — this; aparādha — offense; ha-ibe — there will be; khaṇḍana — nullification.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Nityānanda Prabhu continued, “If You invite at least one hundred sannyāsīs to Your home and feed them sumptuously, Your offense will be nullified.”
