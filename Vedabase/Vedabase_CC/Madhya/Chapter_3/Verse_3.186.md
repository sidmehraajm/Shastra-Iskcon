# CC Madhya 3.186

**Bengali:**

শুনি’ ভক্তগণ তাঁরে করিল স্তবন ।
বেদ-আজ্ঞা যৈছে, মাতা, তোমার বচন ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śuni’ bhakta-gaṇa tāṅre karila stavana
veda-ājñā yaiche, mātā, tomāra vacana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuni’ — hearing this; bhakta-gaṇa — all the devotees; tāṅre — to her; karila — did; stavana — praying; veda-ājñā — an injunction of the Vedas; mātā — my dear mother; tomāra vacana — your word.

**Translation:**

Translation
After hearing Śacīmātā, all the devotees offered her prayers and assured her that her order, like a Vedic injunction, could not be violated.
