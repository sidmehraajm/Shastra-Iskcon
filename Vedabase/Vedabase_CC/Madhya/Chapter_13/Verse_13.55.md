# CC Madhya 13.55

**Bengali:**

কীর্তন দেখিয়া জগন্নাথ হরষিত ।
সংকীর্তন দেখে রথ করিয়া স্থগিত ॥ ৫৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kīrtana dekhiyā jagannātha haraṣita
saṅkīrtana dekhe ratha kariyā sthagita

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kīrtana dekhiyā — by seeing the performance of saṅkīrtana; haraṣita — very pleased; saṅkīrtana — performance of saṅkīrtana; ratha — the car; kariyā sthagita — stopping.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Jagannātha was very much pleased by the saṅkīrtana, and He brought His car to a standstill just to see the performance.
