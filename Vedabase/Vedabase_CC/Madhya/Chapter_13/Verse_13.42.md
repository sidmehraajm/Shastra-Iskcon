# CC Madhya 13.42

**Bengali:**

গোবিন্দ-ঘোষ — প্রধান কৈল আর সম্প্রদায় ।
হরিদাস, বিষ্ণুদাস, রাঘব, যাহাঁ গায় ॥ ৪২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

govinda-ghoṣa — pradhāna kaila āra sampradāya
haridāsa, viṣṇudāsa, rāghava, yāhāṅ gāya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
govinda-ghoṣa — Govinda Ghoṣa; pradhāna — the chief; kaila — formed; āra — another; sampradāya — group; haridāsa — the younger Haridāsa; viṣṇudāsa — Viṣṇudāsa; rāghava — Rāghava; yāhāṅ — where; gāya — sing.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord formed another group, appointing Govinda Ghoṣa as leader. In this group the younger Haridāsa, Viṣṇudāsa and Rāghava were the responding singers.
