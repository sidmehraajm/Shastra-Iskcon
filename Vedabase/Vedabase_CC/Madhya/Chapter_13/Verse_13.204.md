# CC Madhya 13.204

**Bengali:**

যত ভক্ত কীর্তনীয়া আসিয়া আরামে ।
প্রতিবৃক্ষতলে সবে করেন বিশ্রামে ॥ ২০৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yata bhakta kīrtanīyā āsiyā ārāme
prati-vṛkṣa-tale sabe karena viśrāme

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yata bhakta — all the devotees; kīrtanīyā — who were performing saṅkīrtana; ārāme — in the resting place; prati-vṛkṣa-tale — under each and every tree; sabe — all of them; karena — take; viśrāme — rest.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the devotees who had been performing saṅkīrtana came there and took rest under each and every tree.
