# CC Madhya 16.272

**Bengali:**

বাদিয়ার বাজি পাতি’ চলিলাঙ তথারে ।
বহু-সঙ্গে বৃন্দাবন গমন না করে ॥ ২৭২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bādiyāra bāji pāti’ calilāṅa tathāre
bahu-saṅge vṛndāvana gamana nā kare

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bādiyāra — of a gypsy; bāji — the magic; pāti’ — demonstrating; calilāṅa — I went; tathāre — there; bahu-saṅge — with many men; vṛndāvana — to Vṛndāvana dhāma; nā kare — no one does.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I then understood that I was going to Vṛndāvana like a magician with his show, and this is certainly not good. No one should go to Vṛndāvana with so many men.
