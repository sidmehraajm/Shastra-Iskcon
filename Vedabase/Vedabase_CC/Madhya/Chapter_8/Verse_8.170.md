# CC Madhya 8.170

**Bengali:**

সৌন্দর্য — কুঙ্কুম, সখী-প্রণয় চন্দন ।
স্মিতকান্তি — কর্পূর, তিনে — অঙ্গে বিলেপন ॥ ১৭০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

saundarya — kuṅkuma, sakhī-praṇaya — candana
smita-kānti — karpūra, tine — aṅge vilepana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saundarya — Her personal beauty; kuṅkuma — a red powder known as kuṅkuma; candana — the sandalwood pulp; smita-kānti — the sweetness of Her smile; karpūra — camphor; tine — by these three things; aṅge — on the body; vilepana — smearing.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī’s personal beauty is the reddish powder known as kuṅkuma, Her affection for Her associates is sandalwood pulp, and the sweetness of Her smile is camphor. All these, combined together, are smeared over Her body.
