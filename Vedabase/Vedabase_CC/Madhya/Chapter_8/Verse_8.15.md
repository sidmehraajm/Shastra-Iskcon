# CC Madhya 8.15

**Bengali:**

তাঁর সঙ্গে বহু আইলা বৈদিক ব্রাহ্মণ ।
বিধিমতে কৈল তেঁহো স্নানাদি-তর্পণ ॥ ১৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tāṅra saṅge bahu āilā vaidika brāhmaṇa
vidhi-mate kaila teṅho snānādi-tarpaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tāṅra saṅge — with him; bahu — many; āilā — came; vaidika — following the Vedic principles; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; kaila — did; teṅho — he, Śrīla Rāmānanda Rāya; snāna-ādi-tarpaṇa — bathing and offering oblations, etc.

**Translation:**

Translation
Many brāhmaṇas following the Vedic principles accompanied Rāmānanda Rāya. According to the Vedic rituals, Rāmānanda Rāya took his bath and offered oblations to his forefathers.
