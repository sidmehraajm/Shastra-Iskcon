# CC Madhya 19.44

**Bengali:**

দাক্ষিণাত্য-বিপ্র-সনে আছে পরিচয় ।
সেই বিপ্র নিমন্ত্রিয়া নিল নিজালয় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

dākṣiṇātya-vipra-sane āche paricaya
sei vipra nimantriyā nila nijālaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dākṣiṇātya — Deccan; vipra-sane — with a brāhmaṇa; paricaya — acquaintance; sei — that; nila — brought; nija-ālaya — to his own place.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu had made an acquaintance with a brāhmaṇa from Deccan [in South India], and that brāhmaṇa invited Him for meals and took Him to his place.
