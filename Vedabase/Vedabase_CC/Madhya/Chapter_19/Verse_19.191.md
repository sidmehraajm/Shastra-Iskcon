# CC Madhya 19.191

**Bengali:**

মধুর-রসে ভক্তমুখ্য — ব্রজে গোপীগণ ।
মহিষীগণ, লক্ষ্মীগণ, অসংখ্য গণন ॥ ১৯১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

madhura-rase bhakta-mukhya — vraje gopī-gaṇa
mahiṣī-gaṇa, lakṣmī-gaṇa, asaṅkhya gaṇana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
madhura-rase — in the mellow of conjugal love; bhakta-mukhya — the chief devotees; vraje — in Vṛndāvana; gopī-gaṇa — the gopīs; lakṣmī-gaṇa — the goddesses of fortune in Vaikuṇṭha; asaṅkhya gaṇana — of innumerable reckoning.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The chief devotees in conjugal love are the gopīs in Vṛndāvana, the queens in Dvārakā and the goddesses of fortune in Vaikuṇṭha. These devotees are innumerable.
