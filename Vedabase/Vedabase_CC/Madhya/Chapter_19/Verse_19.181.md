# CC Madhya 19.181

**Bengali:**

সাত্ত্বিক-ব্যভিচারি-ভাবের মিলনে ।
কৃষ্ণভক্তি-রস হয় অমৃত আস্বাদনে ॥ ১৮১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sāttvika-vyabhicāri-bhāvera milane
kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa haya amṛta āsvādane

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sāttvika-vyabhicāri-bhāvera — of sāttvika and vyabhicārī with sthāyibhāva; kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa — the transcendental mellows of devotional service to the Lord; haya — become; amṛta — nectarean; āsvādane — in tasting.

**Translation:**

Translation
“When the higher standard of ecstatic love is mixed with the symptoms of sāttvika and vyabhicārī, the devotee relishes the transcendental bliss of loving Kṛṣṇa in a variety of nectarean tastes.
