# CC Madhya 19.221

**Bengali:**

শান্তের গুণ দাস্যে আছে, অধিক — ‘সেবন’ ।
অতএব দাস্যরসের এই ‘দুই’ গুণ ॥ ২২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śāntera guṇa dāsye āche, adhika — ‘sevana’
ataeva dāsya-rasera ei ‘dui’ guṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śāntera — of the platform of śānta-rasa; dāsye — on the platform of servitude; āche — are; adhika — additional; sevana — serving; ataeva — therefore; dāsya-rasera — of the platform of dāsya-rasa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The qualities of śānta-rasa are also present in dāsya-rasa, but service is added. Thus the dāsya-rasa platform contains the qualities of both śānta-rasa and dāsya-rasa.
