# CC Madhya 14.123

**Bengali:**

বৃন্দাবন-লীলায় কৃষ্ণের সহায় গোপীগণ ।
গোপীগণ বিনা কৃষ্ণের হরিতে নারে মন ॥ ১২৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vṛndāvana-līlāya kṛṣṇera sahāya gopī-gaṇa
gopī-gaṇa vinā kṛṣṇera harite nāre mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vṛndāvana-līlāya — in the pastimes of Vṛndāvana; kṛṣṇera — of Lord Kṛṣṇa; sahāya — assistants; gopī-gaṇa — all the gopīs; harite — to attract; nāre — no one is able; mana — the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In the pastimes of Vṛndāvana, the only assistants are the gopīs. But for the gopīs, no one can attract the mind of Kṛṣṇa.”
