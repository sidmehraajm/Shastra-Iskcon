# CC Madhya 14.10

**Bengali:**

“তব কথামৃতং” শ্লোক রাজা যে পড়িল ।
উঠি’ প্রেমাবেশে প্রভু আলিঙ্গন কৈল ॥ ১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

“tava kathāmṛtaṁ” śloka rājā ye paḍila
uṭhi’ premāveśe prabhu āliṅgana kaila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tava kathāmṛtam — beginning with the words tava kathāmṛtam; rājā — the King; ye paḍila — as he recited; uṭhi’ — getting up; prema-āveśe — in ecstatic love; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; āliṅgana kaila — embraced.

**Translation:**

Translation
As soon as the King recited the verse beginning with the words “tava kathāmṛtam,” the Lord arose in ecstatic love and embraced him.
