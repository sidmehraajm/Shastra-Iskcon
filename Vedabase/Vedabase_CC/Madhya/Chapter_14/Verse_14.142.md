# CC Madhya 14.142

**Bengali:**

সম্যক্ গোপিকার মান না যায় কথন ।
এক-দুই-ভেদে করি দিগ্-দরশন ॥ ১৪২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

samyak gopikāra māna nā yāya kathana
eka-dui-bhede kari dig-daraśana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
samyak — fully; gopikāra — of the gopīs; nā — not; yāya — is possible; kathana — to speak; eka-dui — one, two; bhede — in different types; kari — I make; dik-daraśana — indication.

**Translation:**

Translation
“It is not possible to give a complete statement about the different types of jealous anger manifest by the gopīs, but a few principles may serve as an indication.
