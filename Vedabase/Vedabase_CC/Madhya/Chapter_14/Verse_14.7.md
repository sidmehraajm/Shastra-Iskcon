# CC Madhya 14.7

**Bengali:**

আঁখি মুদি’ প্রভু প্রেমে ভূমিতে শয়ান ।
নৃপতি নৈপুণ্যে করে পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥ ৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āṅkhi mudi’ prabhu preme bhūmite śayāna
nṛpati naipuṇye kare pāda-saṁvāhana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āṅkhi mudi’ — with closed eyes; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; preme — in ecstatic love; bhūmite — on the ground; śayāna — lying down; nṛpati — the King; naipuṇye — very expertly; kare — performs; pāda-saṁvāhana — massaging the legs.

**Translation:**

Translation
As Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu lay on the raised platform with His eyes closed in ecstatic love and emotion, the King very expertly began to massage His legs.
