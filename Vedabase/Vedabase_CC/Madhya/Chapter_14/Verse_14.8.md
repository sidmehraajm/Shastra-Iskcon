# CC Madhya 14.8

**Bengali:**

রাসলীলার শ্লোক পড়ি’ করেন স্তবন ।
“জয়তি তেঽধিকং” অধ্যায় করেন পঠন ॥ ৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

rāsa-līlāra śloka paḍi’ karena stavana
“jayati te ’dhikaṁ” adhyāya karena paṭhana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rāsa-līlāra — of the rāsa-līlā dance; śloka — verses; paḍi’ — reciting; karena — offers; stavana — prayers; jayati te ’dhikam — beginning with the words jayati te ’dhikam; karena — does; paṭhana — recitation.

**Translation:**

Translation
The King began to recite verses about the rāsa-līlā from Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. He recited the chapter beginning with the words “jayati te ’dhikam.”

**Purport:**

Purport
These verses from Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Canto Ten, chapter thirty-one, constitute what is known as the Gopī-gīta.
