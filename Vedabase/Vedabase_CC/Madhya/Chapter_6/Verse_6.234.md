# CC Madhya 6.234

**Bengali:**

আজি কৃষ্ণপ্রাপ্তি-যোগ্য হৈল তোমার মন ।
বেদ-ধর্ম লঙ্ঘি’ কৈলে প্রসাদ ভক্ষণ ॥” ২৩৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āji kṛṣṇa-prāpti-yogya haila tomāra mana
veda-dharma laṅghi’ kaile prasāda bhakṣaṇa”

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āji — today; kṛṣṇa-prāpti — for attainment of the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa; yogya — fit; haila — has become; tomāra — your; mana — mind; veda — of the four Vedas; laṅghi’ — surpassing; kaile — you have done; prasāda — the remnants of food offered to Kṛṣṇa; bhakṣaṇa — eating.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Today your mind has become fit to take shelter of the lotus feet of Kṛṣṇa because, surpassing the Vedic regulative principles, you have eaten the remnants of food offered to the Lord.
