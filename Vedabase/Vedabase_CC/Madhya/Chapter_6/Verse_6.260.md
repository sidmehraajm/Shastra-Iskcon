# CC Madhya 6.260

**Bengali:**

ভাগবতের ‘ব্রহ্মস্তবে’র শ্লোক পড়িলা ।
শ্লোক-শেষে দুই অক্ষর-পাঠ ফিরাইলা ॥ ২৬০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavatera ‘brahma-stave’ra śloka paḍilā
śloka-śeṣe dui akṣara-pāṭha phirāilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavatera — from Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; śloka — a verse; paḍilā — recited; śloka-śeṣe — at the end of the verse; dui akṣara — of two syllables; pāṭha — the reading; phirāilā — changed.

**Translation:**

Translation
He began to quote one of Lord Brahmā’s prayers from Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, but he changed two syllables at the end of the verse.
