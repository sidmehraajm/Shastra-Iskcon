# CC Madhya 6.74

**Bengali:**

ভট্টাচার্য কহে, — ‘ইঁহার প্রৌঢ় যৌবন ।
কেমতে সন্ন্যাস-ধর্ম হইবে রক্ষণ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — ‘iṅhāra prauḍha yauvana
kemate sannyāsa-dharma ha-ibe rakṣaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhaṭṭācārya kahe — Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya replied; iṅhāra — His; prauḍha — full; yauvana — youth; kemate — how; sannyāsa-dharma — principles of a sannyāsī; rakṣaṇa — protection.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Bhaṭṭācārya inquired, “Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu is in His full-fledged youthful life. How can He keep the principles of sannyāsa?
