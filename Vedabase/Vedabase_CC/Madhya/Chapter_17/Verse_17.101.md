# CC Madhya 17.101

**Bengali:**

মহারাষ্ট্রীয় বিপ্র আইসে প্রভু দেখিবারে ।
প্রভুর রূপ-প্রেম দেখি’ হয় চমৎকারে ॥ ১০১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mahārāṣṭrīya vipra āise prabhu dekhibāre
prabhura rūpa-prema dekhi’ haya camatkāre

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahārāṣṭrīya — belonging to the Maharashtra state; vipra — one brāhmaṇa; prabhu dekhibāre — to see Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; rūpa-prema — beauty and ecstatic love; dekhi’ — seeing; haya camatkāre — becomes astonished.

**Translation:**

Translation
At Vārāṇasī there was a Maharashtriyan brāhmaṇa who used to come daily to see Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. This brāhmaṇa was simply astonished to see the Lord’s personal beauty and ecstatic love for Kṛṣṇa.
