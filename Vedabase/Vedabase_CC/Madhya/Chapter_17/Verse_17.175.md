# CC Madhya 17.175

**Bengali:**

তবে বিপ্র প্রভুরে লঞা আইলা নিজ-ঘরে ।
আপন-ইচ্ছায় প্রভুর নানা সেবা করে ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe vipra prabhure lañā āilā nija-ghare
āpana-icchāya prabhura nānā sevā kare

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — thereafter; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; lañā — taking; āilā — came back; nija-ghare — to his home; āpana-icchāya — by his own will; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nānā — various; sevā — services; kare — rendered.

**Translation:**

Translation
The brāhmaṇa then took Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu to his home and, out of his own free will, began to serve the Lord in various ways.
