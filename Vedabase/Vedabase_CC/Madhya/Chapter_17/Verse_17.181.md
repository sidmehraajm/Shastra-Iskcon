# CC Madhya 17.181

**Bengali:**

মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে যদি ‘ভিক্ষা’ মাগিল ।
দৈন্য করি’ সেই বিপ্র কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ১৮১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mahāprabhu tāṅre yadi ‘bhikṣā’ māgila
dainya kari’ sei vipra kahite lāgila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; tāṅre — from him; yadi — when; bhikṣā māgila — requested lunch; dainya kari’ — out of humility; sei vipra — that brāhmaṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
Therefore Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu willingly requested food from the brāhmaṇa, and the brāhmaṇa, feeling natural humility, began to speak as follows.
