# CC Madhya 25.58

**Bengali:**

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-বাণী — অমৃতের ধার ।
তিঁহো যে কহয়ে বস্তু, সেই ‘তত্ত্ব’ — সার ॥” ৫৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-vāṇī — amṛtera dhāra
tiṅho ye kahaye vastu, sei ‘tattva’ — sāra”

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-vāṇī — the message of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; amṛtera dhāra — a continuous shower of nectar; tiṅho — the Lord; ye kahaye vastu — whatever He says to be the ultimate truth, the summum bonum.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The words of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu are a shower of nectar. Whatever He concludes to be the ultimate truth is indeed the summum bonum of all spiritual knowledge.”
