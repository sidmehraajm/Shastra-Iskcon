# CC Madhya 25.30

**Bengali:**

ভক্তি বিনা মুক্তি নহে, ভাগবতে কয় ।
কলিকালে নামাভাসে সুখে মুক্তি হয় ॥ ৩০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhakti vinā mukti nahe, bhāgavate kaya
kali-kāle nāmābhāse sukhe mukti haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhakti vinā — without devotional service; mukti nahe — there is no question of liberation; bhāgavate kaya — it is said in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; nāma-ābhāse — even by a slight appreciation of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; mukti haya — one can get liberation.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In this Age of Kali, one cannot attain liberation without taking to the devotional service of the Lord. In this age, even if one chants the holy name of Kṛṣṇa imperfectly, he still attains liberation very easily.
