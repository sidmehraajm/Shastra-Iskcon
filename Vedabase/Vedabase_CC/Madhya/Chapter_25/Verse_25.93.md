# CC Madhya 25.93

**Bengali:**

যেই সূত্রকর্তা, সে যদি করয়ে ব্যাখ্যান ।
তবে সূত্রের মূল অর্থ লোকের হয় জ্ঞান ॥ ৯৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yei sūtra-kartā, se yadi karaye vyākhyāna
tabe sūtrera mūla artha lokera haya jñāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yei sūtra-kartā — the person who has made the Vedānta-sūtra; yadi — if; karaye vyākhyāna — explains the meaning; tabe — then; sūtrera — of the aphorisms of the Vedānta-sūtra; artha — meaning; lokera — of the people in general; haya jñāna — comes within knowledge.

**Translation:**

Translation
“If the Vedānta-sūtra is explained by Vyāsadeva himself, who has written it, its original meaning can be understood by the people in general.
