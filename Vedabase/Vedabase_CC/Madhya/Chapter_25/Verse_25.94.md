# CC Madhya 25.94

**Bengali:**

প্রণবের যেই অর্থ, গায়ত্রীতে সেই হয় ।
সেই অর্থ চতুঃশ্লোকীতে বিবরিয়া কয় ॥ ৯৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

praṇavera yei artha, gāyatrīte sei haya
sei artha catuḥ-ślokīte vivariyā kaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
praṇavera — of the sound vibration, oṁkāra; artha — meaning; gāyatrīte — in the Gāyatrī mantra; haya — there is; sei artha — that same meaning; catuḥ-ślokīte — in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam summarized in four ślokas; kaya — has said.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The meaning of the sound vibration oṁkāra is present in the Gāyatrī mantra. The same is elaborately explained in the four ślokas of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam known as the catuḥ-ślokī.
