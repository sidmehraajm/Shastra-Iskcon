# CC Madhya 18.164

**Bengali:**

প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ম্লেচ্ছ করয়ে বিচার ।
এই যতি-পাশ ছিল সুবর্ণ অপার ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhure dekhiyā mleccha karaye vicāra
ei yati-pāśa chila suvarṇa apāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhure — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; dekhiyā — seeing; mleccha — the Muslims; karaye vicāra — considered; ei yati-pāśa — within the possession of this sannyāsī; suvarṇa apāra — a large quantity of gold.

**Translation:**

Translation
Seeing the Lord unconscious, the soldiers thought, “This sannyāsī must have possessed a large quantity of gold.
