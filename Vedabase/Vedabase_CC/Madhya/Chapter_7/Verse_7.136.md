# CC Madhya 7.136

**Bengali:**

‘বাসুদেব’-নাম এক দ্বিজ মহাশয় ।
সর্বাঙ্গে গলিত কুষ্ঠ, তাতে কীড়াময় ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘vāsudeva’-nāma eka dvija mahāśaya
sarvāṅge galita kuṣṭha, tāte kīḍā-maya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vāsudeva-nāma — of the name Vāsudeva; eka dvija — one brāhmaṇa; sarva-aṅge — all over his body; galita — acute; kuṣṭha — leprosy; tāte — in that; kīḍā-maya — full of living worms.

**Translation:**

Translation
There was also a brāhmaṇa named Vāsudeva, who was a great person but was suffering from leprosy. Indeed, his body was filled with living worms.
