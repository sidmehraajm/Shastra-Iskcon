# CC Madhya 10.105

**Bengali:**

‘চৈতন্যানন্দ’ গুরু তাঁর আজ্ঞা দিলেন তাঁরে ।
বেদান্ত পড়িয়া পড়াও সমস্ত লোকেরে ॥ ১০৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘caitanyānanda’ guru tāṅra ājñā dilena tāṅre
vedānta paḍiyā paḍāo samasta lokere

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
caitanya-ānanda — of the name Caitanyānanda Bhāratī; guru — spiritual master; tāṅra — his; ājñā — order; dilena — gave; tāṅre — to him; vedānta paḍiyā — reading the Vedānta-sūtra; samasta — all; lokere — people.

**Translation:**

Translation
At the conclusion of his sannyāsa, his spiritual master, Caitanyānanda Bhāratī, ordered him, “Read the Vedānta-sūtra and teach it to all others.”
