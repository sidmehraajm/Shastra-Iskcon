# CC Madhya 20.179

**Bengali:**

গোবিন্দের মাধুরী দেখি’ বাসুদেবের ক্ষোভ ।
সে মাধুরী আস্বাদিতে উপজয় লোভ ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

govindera mādhurī dekhi’ vāsudevera kṣobha
se mādhurī āsvādite upajaya lobha

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
govindera — of Lord Govinda; mādhurī — the sweetness; dekhi’ — seeing; vāsudevera — of Vāsudeva; kṣobha — agitation; mādhurī — sweetness; āsvādite — to taste; upajaya — awakens; lobha — greed.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Indeed, Vāsudeva is agitated just to see the sweetness of Govinda, and a transcendental greed awakens in Him to enjoy that sweetness.
