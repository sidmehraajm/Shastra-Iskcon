# CC Madhya 12.188

**Bengali:**

অদ্বৈত-নিত্যানন্দ বসিয়াছেন এক ঠাঞি ।
দুইজনে ক্রীড়া-কলহ লাগিল তথাই ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

advaita-nityānanda vasiyāchena eka ṭhāñi
dui-jane krīḍā-kalaha lāgila tathāi

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
advaita-nityānanda — Advaita Ācārya and Nityānanda Prabhu; vasiyāchena — sat; eka ṭhāñi — in one place; dui-jane — those two persons; krīḍā-kalaha — mock fighting; lāgila — began; tathāi — there.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Advaita Ācārya and Nityānanda Prabhu sat side by side, and when prasādam was being distributed They engaged in a type of mock fighting.
