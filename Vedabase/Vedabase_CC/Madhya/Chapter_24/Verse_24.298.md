# CC Madhya 24.298

**Bengali:**

আটান্ন চ-কারের সব লোপ হয় ।
এক আত্মারাম-শব্দে আটান্ন অর্থ কয় ॥ ২৯৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āṭānna ca-kārera saba lopa haya
eka ātmārāma-śabde āṭānna artha kaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āṭānna — fifty-eight; ca-kārera — of the additions of the word ca; eka — one; āṭānna artha kaya — fifty-eight imports are automatically derived.

**Translation:**

Translation
“When all the ca-kāras, or additions of the word ‘ca,’ are taken away, fifty-eight different meanings can still be understood by the one word ‘ātmārāma.’
