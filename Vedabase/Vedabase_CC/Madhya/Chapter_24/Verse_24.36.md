# CC Madhya 24.36

**Bengali:**

‘ইত্থম্ভূত’-শব্দের অর্থ — পূর্ণানন্দময় ।
যাঁর আগে ব্রহ্মানন্দ তৃণপ্রায় হয় ॥ ৩৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘ittham-bhūta’-śabdera artha — pūrṇānandamaya
yāṅra āge brahmānanda tṛṇa-prāya haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ittham-bhūta-śabdera — of the word ittham-bhūta; pūrṇa-ānanda-maya — full of transcendental bliss; yāṅra āge — in front of which; brahma-ānanda — the transcendental bliss derived from impersonalism; tṛṇa-prāya — just like straw; haya — is.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘ittham-bhūta’ is transcendentally exalted because it means ‘full of transcendental bliss.’ Before this transcendental bliss, the bliss derived from merging into the existence of the Absolute [brahmānanda] becomes like a piece of straw in comparison.
