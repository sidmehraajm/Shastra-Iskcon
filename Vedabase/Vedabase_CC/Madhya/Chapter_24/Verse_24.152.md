# CC Madhya 24.152

**Bengali:**

তবে যে চ-কার, সেই ‘সমুচ্চয়’ কয় ।
“আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়শ্চ” কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ১৫২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe ye ca-kāra, sei ‘samuccaya’ kaya
“ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca” kṛṣṇere bhajaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — then; ye — that; ca-kāra — syllable ca; samuccaya — aggregation; kaya — is said; ātmārāmāḥ ca — all those who enjoy in the self; munayaḥ ca — all saintly persons; kṛṣṇere bhajaya — worship Kṛṣṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“By the aggregate use of the word ‘ca,’ it is indicated that all the ātmārāmas and saints serve and worship Kṛṣṇa.
