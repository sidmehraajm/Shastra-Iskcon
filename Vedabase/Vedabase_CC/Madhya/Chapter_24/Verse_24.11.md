# CC Madhya 24.11

**Bengali:**

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ব্রহ্ম, দেহ, মন, যত্ন, ধৃতি ।
বুদ্ধি, স্বভাব, — এই সাত অর্থ-প্রাপ্তি ॥ ১১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘ātmā’-śabde brahma, deha, mana, yatna, dhṛti
buddhi, svabhāva, — ei sāta artha-prāpti

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ātmā-śabde — by the word ātmā; deha — the body; mana — the mind; yatna — endeavor; dhṛti — firmness; buddhi — intelligence; svabhāva — nature; ei sāta — these seven; artha-prāpti — attainment of meanings.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The seven different meanings of the word ‘ātmā’ are the Absolute Truth, the body, the mind, endeavor, firmness, intelligence and nature.
