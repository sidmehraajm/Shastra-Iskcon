# CC Madhya 24.324

**Bengali:**

পুনঃ সনাতন কহে যুড়ি’ দুই করে ।
“প্রভু আজ্ঞা দিলা ‘বৈষ্ণবস্মৃতি’ করিবারে ॥ ৩২৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

punaḥ sanātana kahe yuḍi’ dui kare
“prabhu ājñā dilā ‘vaiṣṇava-smṛti’ karibāre

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
punaḥ — again; sanātana — Sanātana Gosvāmī; kahe — says; yuḍi’ dui kare — folding his two hands; prabhu ājñā dilā — Your Lordship has ordered me; vaiṣṇava-smṛti karibāre — to write a directory of Vaiṣṇava activities.

**Translation:**

Translation
Folding his hands, Sanātana Gosvāmī said, “My Lord, You ordered me to write a directory about the activities of Vaiṣṇavas.
