# CC Madhya 5.17

**Bengali:**

ছোটবিপ্ৰ করে সদা তাঁহার সেবন ।
তাঁহার সেবায় বিপ্রের তুষ্ট হৈল মন ॥ ১৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

choṭa-vipra kare sadā tāṅhāra sevana
tāṅhāra sevāya viprera tuṣṭa haila mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
choṭa-vipra — the younger brāhmaṇa; sadā — always; tāṅhāra — his (the old brāhmaṇa’s); sevana — service; tāṅhāra — his; sevāya — by the service; viprera — of the old brāhmaṇa; haila — became; mana — the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
Indeed, the young brāhmaṇa always rendered service to the older one, and the old man, being very satisfied with his service, was pleased with him.
