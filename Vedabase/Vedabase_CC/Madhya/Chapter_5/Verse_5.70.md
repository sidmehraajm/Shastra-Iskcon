# CC Madhya 5.70

**Bengali:**

কন্যা দিতে নারিবে, হবে অসত্য-বচন ।
পুনরপি কহে বিপ্র করিয়া যতন ॥ ৭০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kanyā dite nāribe, habe asatya-vacana
punarapi kahe vipra kariyā yatana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kanyā — daughter; dite — to give; nāribe — you will not be able; habe — it will become; asatya-vacana — a false statement; punarapi — again; kahe — he says; vipra — the brāhmaṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘My dear sir, you will not be able to fulfill your promise. Your promise will be broken.’ Yet, again and again the brāhmaṇa emphasized his promise.
