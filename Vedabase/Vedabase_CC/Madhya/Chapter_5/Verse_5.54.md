# CC Madhya 5.54

**Bengali:**

সব লোক বড়বিপ্রে ডাকিয়া আনিল ।
তবে সেই লঘুবিপ্র কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ৫৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

saba loka baḍa-vipre ḍākiyā ānila
tabe sei laghu-vipra kahite lāgila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saba loka — all the village inhabitants; baḍa-vipre — to the senior brāhmaṇa; ānila — brought; tabe — then; sei laghu-vipra — the junior brāhmaṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the people of the village then called for the elderly brāhmaṇa and brought him to their meeting place. The young brāhmaṇa then began to speak before them as follows.
