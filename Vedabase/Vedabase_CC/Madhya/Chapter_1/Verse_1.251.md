# CC Madhya 1.251

**Bengali:**

নিরন্তর নৃত্যগীত কীর্তন-বিলাস ।
আচণ্ডালে প্রেমভক্তি করিলা প্রকাশ ॥ ২৫১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nirantara nṛtya-gīta kīrtana-vilāsa
ācaṇḍāle prema-bhakti karilā prakāśa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nirantara — without stopping; nṛtya-gīta — chanting and dancing; kīrtana — of saṅkīrtana; ā-caṇḍāle — to everyone, even to the lowest person; prema-bhakti — love of Godhead; karilā — did; prakāśa — manifestation.

**Translation:**

Translation
At Jagannātha Purī, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu performed chanting and dancing unceasingly. Thus He enjoyed the pastime of saṅkīrtana. He manifested His causeless mercy, pure love of God, to everyone, including the lowest man.
