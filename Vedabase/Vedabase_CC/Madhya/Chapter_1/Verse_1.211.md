# CC Madhya 1.211

**Bengali:**

পরব্যসনিনী নারী ব্যগ্রাপি গৃহকর্মসু ।
তদেবাস্বাদয়ত্যন্তর্নবসঙ্গরসায়নম্ ॥ ২১১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

para-vyasaninī nārī
vyagrāpi gṛha-karmasu
tad evāsvādayaty antar
nava-saṅga-rasāyanam

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
para-vyasaninī — attached to another man; nārī — a woman; vyagrā</em> <em>api — although zealous; gṛha-karmasu — in household affairs; tat eva — that only; āsvādayati — tastes; antaḥ — within herself; nava-saṅga — of new association; rasa-ayanam — mellow.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘If a woman is attached to a man other than her husband, she will appear very busy in carrying out her household affairs, but within her heart she is always relishing feelings of association with her paramour.’
