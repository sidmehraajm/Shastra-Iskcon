# CC Madhya 15.37

**Bengali:**

একদিন মহাপ্রভু নিত্যানন্দে লঞা ।
দুই ভাই যুক্তি কৈল নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ৩৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka-dina mahāprabhu nityānande lañā
dui bhāi yukti kaila nibhṛte vasiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka-dina — one day; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nityānande</em> <em>lañā — taking Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; dui bhāi — two brothers; yukti kaila — consulted; nibhṛte vasiyā — sitting in a solitary place.

**Translation:**

Translation
One day the two brothers, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda Prabhu, consulted with each other while sitting together in a solitary place.
