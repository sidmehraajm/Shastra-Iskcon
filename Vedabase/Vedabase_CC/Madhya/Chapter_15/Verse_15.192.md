# CC Madhya 15.192

**Bengali:**

প্রভু ক্রমে ক্রমে পাঁচ-দিন ঘাটাইল ।
পাঁচ-দিন তাঁর ভিক্ষা নিয়ম করিল ॥ ১৯২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhu krame krame pāṅca-dina ghāṭāila
pāṅca-dina tāṅra bhikṣā niyama karila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; krame krame — gradually; pāṅca-dina — to five days; ghāṭāila — reduced; pāñca-dina — for five days; tāṅra — his; bhikṣā — invitation for lunch; niyama karila — accepted regularly.

**Translation:**

Translation
In this way, by and by, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu reduced the duration to five days. Thus for five days He regularly accepted the Bhaṭṭācārya’s invitation to lunch.
