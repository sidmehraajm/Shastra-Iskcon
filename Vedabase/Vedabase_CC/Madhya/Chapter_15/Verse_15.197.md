# CC Madhya 15.197

**Bengali:**

বহুত সন্ন্যাসী যদি আইসে এক ঠাঞি ।
সম্মান করিতে নারি, অপরাধ পাই ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bahuta sannyāsī yadi āise eka ṭhāñi
sammāna karite nāri, aparādha pāi

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bahuta sannyāsī — many sannyāsīs; āise — come; eka ṭhāñi — together; sammāna karite nāri — I cannot receive them properly; aparādha pāi — I shall be an offender.

**Translation:**

Translation
“If all the sannyāsīs came together, it would not be possible for me to pay them proper respects. Therefore I would be an offender.
