# CC Madhya 15.10

**Bengali:**

পূজা-পাত্রে পুষ্প-তুলসী শেষ যে আছিল ।
সেই সব লঞা প্রভু আচার্যে পূজিল ॥ ১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

pūjā-pātre puṣpa-tulasī śeṣa ye āchila
sei saba lañā prabhu ācārye pūjila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pūjā-pātre — on the dish that holds flowers and tulasī; ye āchila — whatever there were; sei saba — all of them; lañā — taking; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ācārye pūjila — worshiped Advaita Ācārya.

**Translation:**

Translation
After being worshiped by Advaita Ācārya, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu would take the dish containing flowers and tulasī and, with whatever paraphernalia remained, worship Advaita Ācārya.
