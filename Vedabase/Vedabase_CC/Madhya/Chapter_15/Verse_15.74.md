# CC Madhya 15.74

**Bengali:**

ভোগের সময় পুনঃ ছুলি’ সংস্করি’ ।
কৃষ্ণে সমর্পণ করে মুখ ছিদ্র করি’ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhogera samaya punaḥ chuli’ saṁskari’
kṛṣṇe samarpaṇa kare mukha chidra kari’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhogera samaya — at the time of offering bhoga; chuli’ — clipping; saṁskari’ — cleansing; kṛṣṇe — unto Lord Kṛṣṇa; samarpaṇa — offering; kare — makes; mukha — at the top; chidra kari’ — making a hole.

**Translation:**

Translation
“At the time of offering bhoga, the coconuts are again clipped and cleansed. After holes are made in them at the top, they are offered to Lord Kṛṣṇa.
