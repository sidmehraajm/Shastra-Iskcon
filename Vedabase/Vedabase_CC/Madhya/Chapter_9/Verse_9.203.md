# CC Madhya 9.203

**Bengali:**

‘মায়াসীতা’রাবণ নিল, শুনিলা আখ্যানে ।
শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু হইল আনন্দিত মনে ॥ ২০৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘māyā-sītā’ rāvaṇa nila, śunilā ākhyāne
śuni’ mahāprabhu haila ānandita mane

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
māyā-sītā — false, illusory Sītā; rāvaṇa — the demon Rāvaṇa; nila — took; śunilā — heard; ākhyāne — in the narration of the Kūrma Purāṇa; mahāprabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; haila — became; ānandita — very happy; mane — within the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
Upon hearing from the Kūrma Purāṇa how Rāvaṇa had kidnapped a false form of mother Sītā, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu became very satisfied.
