# CC Madhya 9.101

**Bengali:**

যাবৎ পড়োঁ, তাবৎ পাঙ তাঁর দরশন ।
এই লাগি’ গীতা-পাঠ না ছাড়ে মোর মন ॥ ১০১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yāvat paḍoṅ, tāvat pāṅa tāṅra daraśana
ei lāgi’ gītā-pāṭha nā chāḍe mora mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yāvat — as long as; paḍoṅ — I read; tāvat — so long; pāṅa — I get; tāṅra — His; daraśana — audience; ei lāgi’ — for this reason; gītā-pāṭha — reading the Bhagavad-gītā; mora mana — my mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
“As long as I read the Bhagavad-gītā, I simply see the Lord’s beautiful features. It is for this reason that I am reading the Bhagavad-gītā, and my mind cannot be distracted from this.”
