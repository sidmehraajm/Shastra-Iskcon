# CC Madhya 4.83

**Bengali:**

পুরী-গোসাঞি আজ্ঞা দিল সকল ব্রাহ্মণে ।
আ-বাল-বৃদ্ধ গ্রামের লোক করাহ ভোজনে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

purī-gosāñi ājñā dila sakala brāhmaṇe
ā-bāla-vṛddha grāmera loka karāha bhojane

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
purī-gosāñi — Mādhavendra Purī; ājñā — order; dila — gave; sakala brāhmaṇe — to all the brāhmaṇas; grāmera — of the village; loka — the people; karāha — make; bhojane — take prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
After the Lord was laid down to rest on the bed, Mādhavendra Purī gathered all the brāhmaṇas who had prepared the prasādam and said to them, “Now feed everyone sumptuously, from the children on up to the aged!”
