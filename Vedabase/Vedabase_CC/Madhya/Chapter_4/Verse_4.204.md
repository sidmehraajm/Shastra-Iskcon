# CC Madhya 4.204

**Bengali:**

লোকের সংঘট্ট দেখি’ প্ৰভুর বাহ্য হৈল ।
ঠাকুরের ভোগ সরি’ আরতি বাজিল ॥ ২০৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

lokera saṅghaṭṭa dekhi’ prabhura bāhya haila
ṭhākurera bhoga sari’ ārati bājila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
lokera — of people; saṅghaṭṭa — a crowd; dekhi’ — seeing; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bāhya — external consciousness; haila — appeared; ṭhākurera — of the Deity; bhoga — offering; sari’ — finishing; ārati — performance of ārati.

**Translation:**

Translation
When many people crowded around Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, He regained His external senses. In the meantime, the offering to the Deity had been finished, and there was a resounding ārati performance.
