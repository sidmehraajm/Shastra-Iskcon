# CC Madhya 4.7

**Bengali:**

চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে যাহা করিল বর্ণন ।
সূত্ররূপে সেই লীলা করিয়ে সূচন ॥ ৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

caitanya-maṅgale yāhā karila varṇana
sūtra-rūpe sei līlā kariye sūcana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
caitanya-maṅgale — in the book named Caitanya-maṅgala; karila varṇana — has described; sūtra-rūpe — in the form of a synopsis; sei līlā — those pastimes; kariye sūcana — I shall present.

**Translation:**

Translation
I am therefore presenting only a synopsis of those events already described elaborately by Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura in his Caitanya-maṅgala [now known as Caitanya-bhāgavata].
