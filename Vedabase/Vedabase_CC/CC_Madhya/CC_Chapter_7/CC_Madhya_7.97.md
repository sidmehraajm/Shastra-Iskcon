# CC Madhya 7.97

**Bengali:**

এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ পথে চলিলা গৌরহরি ।
লোক দেখি’ পথে কহে, — বল ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ei śloka paḍi’ pathe calilā gaurahari
loka dekhi’ pathe kahe, — bala ‘hari’ ‘hari’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei śloka paḍi’ — reciting this verse kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa!; calilā — went; gaurahari — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; loka dekhi’ — seeing other people; pathe — on the way; kahe — He says; bala — say; hari hari — the holy name of Lord Hari.

**Translation:**

Translation
Chanting this verse, Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, known as Gaurahari, went on His way. As soon as He saw someone, He would request him to chant “Hari! Hari!”
