# CC Madhya 15.232

**Bengali:**

ভট্টাচার্য বলে, — প্রভু না করহ বিস্ময় ।
যেই খাবে, তাঁহার শক্ত্যে ভোগ সিদ্ধ হয় ॥ ২৩২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhaṭṭācārya bale — prabhu nā karaha vismaya
yei khābe, tāṅhāra śaktye bhoga siddha haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhaṭṭācārya bale — the Bhaṭṭācārya said; prabhu — my Lord; nā karaha vismaya — do not become astonished; yei khābe — whoever shall eat; tāṅhāra śaktye — by His grace; bhoga — the food; siddha haya — has been prepared.

**Translation:**

Translation
Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya said, “It is not so wonderful, my Lord. Everything has been made possible by the energy and mercy of Him who will eat the food.
