# CC Madhya 15.79

**Bengali:**

এক দিন ফল দশ সংস্কার করিয়া ।
ভোগ লাগাইতে সেবক আইল লঞা ॥ ৭৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka dina phala daśa saṁskāra kariyā
bhoga lāgāite sevaka āila lañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka dina — one day; phala — fruits; daśa — ten; saṁskāra kariyā — after cleansing; bhoga lāgāite — to offer bhoga; āila — came; lañā — taking.

**Translation:**

Translation
“One day it so happened that about ten coconuts were properly clipped and brought by a servant to offer to the Deity.
