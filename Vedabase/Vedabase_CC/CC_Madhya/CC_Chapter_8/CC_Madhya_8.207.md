# CC Madhya 8.207

**Bengali:**

সখীর স্বভাব এক অকথ্য-কথন ।
কৃষ্ণ-সহ নিজলীলায় নাহি সখীর মন ॥ ২০৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sakhīra svabhāva eka akathya-kathana
kṛṣṇa-saha nija-līlāya nāhi sakhīra mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sakhīra — of the gopīs; eka — one; akathya — inexplicable; kathana — narration; kṛṣṇa-saha — with Kṛṣṇa; nija-līlāya — in His personal pastimes; nāhi — not; sakhīra — of the gopīs.

**Translation:**

Translation
“There is an inexplicable fact about the natural inclinations of the gopīs. The gopīs never want to enjoy themselves with Kṛṣṇa personally.
