# CC Madhya 8.116

**Bengali:**

শতকোটি-গোপীতে নহে কাম-নির্বাপণ ।
তাহাতেই অনুমানি শ্রীরাধিকার গুণ ॥ ১১৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śata-koṭi-gopīte nahe kāma-nirvāpaṇa
tāhātei anumāni śrī-rādhikāra guṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śata-koṭi — hundreds of thousands; gopīte — in the midst of gopīs; kāma-nirvāpaṇa — satisfaction of lust; tāhātei — by that way; anumāni — we can imagine; śrī-rādhikāra guṇa — the transcendental quality of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Since Kṛṣṇa’s lusty desires were not satisfied even in the midst of hundreds of thousands of gopīs and He was thus searching after Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī, we can easily imagine how transcendentally qualified She is.”
