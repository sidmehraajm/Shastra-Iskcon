# CC Madhya 8.218

**Bengali:**

নিজেন্দ্রিয়সুখবাঞ্ছা নাহি গোপিকার ।
কৃষ্ণে সুখ দিতে করে সঙ্গম-বিহার ॥ ২১৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nijendriya-sukha-vāñchā nāhi gopikāra
kṛṣṇe sukha dite kare saṅgama-vihāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nija-indriya-sukha — for personal sense gratification; vāñchā — the desire; nāhi — there is not; gopikāra — of the gopīs; sukha — happiness; dite — to give; kare — do; saṅgama-vihāra — mingling and enjoying with Kṛṣṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Among the gopīs, there is not a pinch of desire for sense gratification. Their only desire is to give pleasure to Kṛṣṇa, and this is why they mingle with Him and enjoy with Him.
