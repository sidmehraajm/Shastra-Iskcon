# CC Madhya 24.162

**Bengali:**

চ-শব্দে ‘অপি’র অর্থ ইহাঁও কহয় ।
‘মুনি’, ‘নির্গ্রন্থ’-শব্দের পূর্ববৎ অর্থ হয় ॥ ১৬২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ca-śabde ‘api’ra artha ihāṅo kahaya
‘muni’, ‘nirgrantha’-śabdera pūrvavat artha haya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ca-śabde — by the word ca; ihāṅo — here also; kahaya — is applicable; pūrva-vat — as mentioned above; artha haya — there are the meanings.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The meanings of the words ‘ca’ and ‘api’ can be applied here. The meanings of the words ‘muni’ and ‘nirgrantha’ are the same as before.
