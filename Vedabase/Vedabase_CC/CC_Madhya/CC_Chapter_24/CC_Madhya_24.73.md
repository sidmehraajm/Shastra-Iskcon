# CC Madhya 24.73

**Bengali:**

সেই ব্রহ্ম-শব্দে কহে স্বয়ং-ভগবান্‌ ।
অদ্বিতীয়-জ্ঞান, যাঁহা বিনা নাহি আন ॥ ৭৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei brahma-śabde kahe svayaṁ-bhagavān
advitīya-jñāna, yāṅhā vinā nāhi āna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei — that; brahma-śabde — by the word brahma; svayam-bhagavān — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; advitīya-jñāna — the supreme one, without duality; yāṅhā — which; vinā — without; nāhi āna — there is nothing else.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The proper meaning of the word ‘brahma’ is the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is one without a second and without whom nothing else exists.
