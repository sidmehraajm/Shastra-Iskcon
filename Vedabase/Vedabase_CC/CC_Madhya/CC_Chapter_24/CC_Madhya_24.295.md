# CC Madhya 24.295

**Bengali:**

ইতরেতর ‘চ’ দিয়া সমাস করিয়ে ।
‘আটান্ন’বার আত্মারাম নাম লইয়ে ॥ ২৯৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

itaretara ‘ca’ diyā samāsa kariye
‘āṭānna’-bāra ātmārāma nāma la-iye

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
itaretara — in different varieties; ca — the word ca; samāsa kariye — make a compound word; āṭānna-bāra — fifty-eight times; ātmārāma — the ātmārāma devotees; nāma la-iye — I take their different names.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In this way, as I add the word ‘ca’ to one word after another, I make a compound. Thus the different names of ātmārāmas can be taken fifty-eight times.
