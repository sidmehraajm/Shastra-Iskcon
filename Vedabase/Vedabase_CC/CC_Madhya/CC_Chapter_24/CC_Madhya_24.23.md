# CC Madhya 24.23

**Bengali:**

মায়া-শক্ত্যে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডাদি-পরিপাটী-সৃজন ।
‘উরুক্রম’-শব্দের এই অর্থ নিরূপণ ॥ ২৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

māyā-śaktye brahmāṇḍādi-paripāṭī-sṛjana
‘urukrama’-śabdera ei artha nirūpaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
māyā-śaktye — by His external potency; brahmāṇḍa-ādi — of material universes and so on; paripāṭī — an orderly arrangement; sṛjana — creating; urukrama-śabdera — of the word urukrama; artha — of the meaning; nirūpaṇa — ascertainment.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘urukrama’ indicates the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who, by His external potency, has perfectly created innumerable universes.
