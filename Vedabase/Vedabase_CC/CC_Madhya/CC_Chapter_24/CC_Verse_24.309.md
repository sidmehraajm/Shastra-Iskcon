# CC Madhya 24.309

**Bengali:**

“ক্ষেত্রজ্ঞ আত্মা পুরুষঃ
প্রধানং প্রকৃতিঃ স্ত্রিয়াম্‌ ॥” ৩০৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

“kṣetrajña ātmā puruṣaḥ
pradhānaṁ prakṛtiḥ striyām”

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṣetra-jñaḥ — the word kṣetrajña; puruṣaḥ — the enjoyer; pradhānam — the chief; prakṛtiḥ — the material nature; striyām — in the feminine gender.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘The word ‘kṣetrajña’ refers to the living entity, the enjoyer, the chief and material nature.’

**Purport:**

Purport
This is a quotation from the Svarga-varga (7) of the Amara-kośa dictionary.
