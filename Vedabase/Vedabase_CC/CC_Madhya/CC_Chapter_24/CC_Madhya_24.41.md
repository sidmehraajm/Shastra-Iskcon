# CC Madhya 24.41

**Bengali:**

‘গুণ’ শব্দের অর্থ — কৃষ্ণের গুণ অনন্ত ।
সচ্চিদ্‌রূপ-গুণ সর্ব পূর্ণানন্দ ॥ ৪১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘guṇa’ śabdera artha — kṛṣṇera guṇa ananta
sac-cid-rūpa-guṇa sarva pūrṇānanda

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
guṇa śabdera artha — the meaning of the word guṇa; sat-cit-rūpa-guṇa — such qualities are spiritual and eternal; sarva pūrṇa-ānanda — full of all transcendental bliss.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘guṇa’ means ‘quality.’ The qualities of Kṛṣṇa are transcendentally situated and are unlimited in quantity. All of the spiritual qualities are full of transcendental bliss.
