# CC Madhya 24.19

**Bengali:**

‘উরুক্রম’-শব্দে কহে, বড় যাঁর ক্রম ।
‘ক্রম’-শব্দে কহে এই পাদবিক্ষেপণ ॥ ১৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘urukrama’-śabde kahe, baḍa yāṅra krama
‘krama’-śabde kahe ei pāda-vikṣepaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
urukrama-śabde — by the word urukrama; baḍa — great; yāṅra — whose; krama — step; krama-śabde — by the word krama; ei — this; pāda-vikṣepaṇa — throwing forth of the foot.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The word ‘urukrama’ refers to one whose krama [step] is great. The word ‘krama’ means ‘throwing the foot forward,’ that is, ‘stepping.’
