# CC Madhya 24.167

**Bengali:**

এহো কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট মহামুনি হঞা ।
অহৈতুকী ভক্তি করে নির্গ্রন্থ হঞা ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eho kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa mahā-muni hañā
ahaitukī bhakti kare nirgrantha hañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eho — such yogīs; mahā-muni hañā — becoming great saintly persons; ahaitukī bhakti kare — they perform causeless devotional service; nirgrantha hañā — becoming indifferent to the mystic yoga process.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Being attracted by the transcendental qualities of Kṛṣṇa, such yogīs become great saints. At that time, not being hampered by the yogic process, they engage in unalloyed devotional service.
