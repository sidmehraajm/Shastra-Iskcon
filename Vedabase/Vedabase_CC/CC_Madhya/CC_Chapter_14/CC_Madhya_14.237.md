# CC Madhya 14.237

**Bengali:**

নিত্যানন্দ বিনা প্রভুকে ধরে কোন্ জন ।
প্রভুর আবেশ না যায়, না রহে কীর্তন ॥ ২৩৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nityānanda vinā prabhuke dhare kon jana
prabhura āveśa nā yāya, nā rahe kīrtana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nityānanda vinā — except for Nityānanda Prabhu; prabhuke — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; dhare — can catch; kon jana — what person; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; āveśa — the ecstasy; nā yāya — does not go away; nā rahe — could not be continued.

**Translation:**

Translation
Only Nityānanda Prabhu could catch Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, but the ecstatic mood of the Lord would not stop. At the same time, kīrtana could not be continued.
