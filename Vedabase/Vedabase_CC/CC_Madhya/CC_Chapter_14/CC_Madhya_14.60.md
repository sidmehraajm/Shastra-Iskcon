# CC Madhya 14.60

**Bengali:**

দেখিয়া প্রতাপরুদ্র পাত্র-মিত্র-সঙ্গে ।
প্রভুর মহিমা দেখি’ প্রেমে ফুলে অঙ্গে ॥ ৬০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

dekhiyā pratāparudra pātra-mitra-saṅge
prabhura mahimā dekhi’ preme phule aṅge

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dekhiyā — seeing; pratāparudra — King Pratāparudra; pātra-mitra-saṅge — with his ministers and friends; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; mahimā — the greatness; dekhi’ — by seeing; preme — in love; phule — eruptions; aṅge — on the body.

**Translation:**

Translation
Seeing the greatness of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Pratāparudra Mahārāja and his ministers and friends were so moved by ecstatic love that the hair on their bodies stood on end.
