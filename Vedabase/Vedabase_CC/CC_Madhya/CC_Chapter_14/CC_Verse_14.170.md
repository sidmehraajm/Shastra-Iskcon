# CC Madhya 14.170

**Bengali:**

কিলকিঞ্চিতাদি-ভাবের শুন বিবরণ ।
যে ভাব-ভূষায় রাধা হরে কৃষ্ণ-মন ॥ ১৭০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kila-kiñcitādi-bhāvera śuna vivaraṇa
ye bhāva-bhūṣāya rādhā hare kṛṣṇa-mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kila-kiñcita-ādi — beginning with the ecstasy named kila-kiñcita; śuna — hear; vivaraṇa — the description; ye bhāva-bhūṣāya — with these ecstatic ornaments; rādhā — Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; hare — enchants; kṛṣṇa-mana — the mind of Kṛṣṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Now hear a description of different ecstasies, beginning with kila-kiñcita. With these ecstatic ornaments, Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī enchants the mind of Kṛṣṇa.
