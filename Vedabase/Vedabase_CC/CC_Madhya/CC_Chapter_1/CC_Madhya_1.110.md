# CC Madhya 1.110

**Bengali:**

চাতুর্মাস্য তাঁহা প্রভু শ্রীবৈষ্ণবের সনে ।
গোঙাইল নৃত্য-গীত-কৃষ্ণসংকীর্তনে ॥ ১১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

cāturmāsya tāṅhā prabhu śrī-vaiṣṇavera sane
goṅāila nṛtya-gīta-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtane

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
cāturmāsya — observance of the four months of the rainy season; tāṅhā — there; prabhu — the Lord; śrī-vaiṣṇavera sane — with the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas; goṅāila — passed; nṛtya — dancing; gīta — singing; kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtane — in chanting the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu passed the Cāturmāsya months with the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas, singing, chanting the holy name and dancing.
