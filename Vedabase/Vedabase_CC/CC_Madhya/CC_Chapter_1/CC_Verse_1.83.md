# CC Madhya 1.83

**Bengali:**

ভাগবতের শ্লোক-গূঢ়ার্থ বিশদ করিঞা ।
রূপ-গোসাঞি শ্লোক কৈল লোক বুঝাইঞা ॥ ৮৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavatera śloka-gūḍhārtha viśada kariñā
rūpa-gosāñi śloka kaila loka bujhāiñā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; gūḍha-artha — confidential meaning; viśada — elaborate description; kariñā — doing; rūpa-gosāñi — Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī; śloka — the verse; kaila — compiled; loka — the people in general; bujhāiñā — making understand.

**Translation:**

Translation
In one verse, Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī has explained the confidential meaning of the verse of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam for the understanding of the general populace.
