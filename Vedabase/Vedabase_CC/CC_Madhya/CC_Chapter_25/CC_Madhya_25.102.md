# CC Madhya 25.102

**Bengali:**

ভাগবতের সম্বন্ধ, অভিধেয়, প্রয়োজন ।
চতুঃশ্লোকীতে প্রকট তার করিয়াছে লক্ষণ ॥ ১০২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavatera sambandha, abhidheya, prayojana
catuḥ-ślokīte prakaṭa tāra kariyāche lakṣaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; abhidheya — activities in that relationship; prayojana — the ultimate goal of life; catuḥ-ślokīte — in the four famous verses of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; tāra — of them; kariyāche — has done; lakṣaṇa — the symptoms.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The essence of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — our relationship with the Supreme Lord, our activities in that connection and the goal of life — is manifest in the four verses of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam known as the catuḥ-ślokī. Everything is explained in those verses.
