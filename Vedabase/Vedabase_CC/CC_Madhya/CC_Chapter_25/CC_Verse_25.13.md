# CC Madhya 25.13

**Bengali:**

ভক্ত-দুঃখ দেখি’ প্রভু মনেতে চিন্তিল ।
সন্ন্যাসীর মন ফিরাইতে মন হইল ॥ ১৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhakta-duḥkha dekhi’ prabhu manete cintila
sannyāsīra mana phirāite mana ha-ila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhakta-duḥkha dekhi’ — seeing the unhappiness of the devotees; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; manete cintila — considered within His mind; sannyāsīra mana — the minds of the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs; mana ha-ila — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu decided.

**Translation:**

Translation
They submitted their request, and Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, seeing His devotees’ unhappiness, decided to turn the minds of the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs.
