# CC Madhya 25.23

**Bengali:**

প্রকাশানন্দের শিষ্য এক তাঁহার সমান ।
সভামধ্যে কহে প্রভুর করিয়া সম্মান ॥ ২৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prakāśānandera śiṣya eka tāṅhāra samāna
sabhā-madhye kahe prabhura kariyā sammāna

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prakāśānandera śiṣya eka — one of the disciples of Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; tāṅhāra samāna — equal in learning with Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; sabhā-madhye — in the assembly of the sannyāsīs; prabhura kariyā sammāna — respecting Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu seriously.

**Translation:**

Translation
One of the disciples of Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī, who was as learned as his guru, began to speak in that assembly, offering all respects to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
