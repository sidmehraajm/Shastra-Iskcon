# CC Madhya 25.44

**Bengali:**

ব্যাসসূত্রের অর্থ আচার্য করিয়াছে আচ্ছাদন ।
এই হয় সত্য শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-বচন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vyāsa-sūtrera artha ācārya kariyāche ācchādana
ei haya satya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-vacana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vyāsa-sūtrera — of the aphorisms of the Vedānta known as Vyāsa-sūtra; ācārya — Śaṅkarācārya; kariyāche ācchādana — has purposely covered; ei — this; haya — is; satya — the truth; śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-vacana — the words and explanation given by Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The conclusion is that the import of the Vedānta-sūtra is covered by the imaginary explanation of Śaṅkarācārya. Whatever Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu has said is perfectly true.
