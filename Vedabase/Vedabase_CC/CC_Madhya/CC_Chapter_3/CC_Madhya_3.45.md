# CC Madhya 3.45

**Bengali:**

সার্দ্রক, বাস্তুক-শাক বিবিধ প্রকার ।
পটোল, কুষ্মাণ্ড-বড়ি, মানকচু আর ॥ ৪৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sārdraka, vāstuka-śāka vividha prakāra
paṭola, kuṣmāṇḍa-baḍi, mānakacu āra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sārdraka — pots with ginger dishes; vāstuka-śāka — spinach; vividha — various; prakāra — kinds; paṭola — a kind of vegetable; kuṣmāṇḍa — squash; baḍi — with split dhal; mānakacu — the root of a vegetable tree called kacu.

**Translation:**

Translation
Among the cooked vegetables were paṭolas, squash, mānakacu and a salad made with pieces of ginger and various types of spinach.
