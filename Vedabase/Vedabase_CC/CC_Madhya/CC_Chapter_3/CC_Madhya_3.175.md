# CC Madhya 3.175

**Bengali:**

যদ্যপি সহসা আমি করিয়াছি সন্ন্যাস ।
তথাপি তোমা-সবা হৈতে নহিব উদাস ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yadyapi sahasā āmi kariyāchi sannyāsa
tathāpi tomā-sabā haite nahiba udāsa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yadyapi — although; sahasā — all of a sudden; āmi — I; kariyāchi sannyāsa — accepted sannyāsa; tomā-sabā — all of you; haite — from; nahiba — I shall never be; udāsa — indifferent.

**Translation:**

Translation
“My dear friends, although I have suddenly accepted this renounced order, I still know that I shall never be indifferent to you.
