# CC Madhya 19.222

**Bengali:**

শান্তের গুণ, দাস্যের সেবন — সখ্যে দুই হয় ।
দাস্যের ‘সম্ভ্রমংগৌরব’-সেবা, সখ্যে ‘বিশ্বাস’-ময় ॥ ২২২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śāntera guṇa, dāsyera sevana — sakhye dui haya
dāsyera ‘sambhrama-gaurava’-sevā, sakhye ‘viśvāsa’-maya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śāntera guṇa — qualities of śānta-rasa; dui — the qualities of two rasas; dāsyera — of the dāsya platform; sambhrama-gaurava — with awe and veneration; sevā — service; sakhye — on the platform of fraternity; viśvāsa-maya — mixed with confidence.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The qualities of śānta-rasa and the service of dāsya-rasa are both present on the platform of sakhya-rasa. On the platform of fraternity, the qualities of dāsya-rasa are mixed with the confidence of fraternity instead of awe and veneration.
