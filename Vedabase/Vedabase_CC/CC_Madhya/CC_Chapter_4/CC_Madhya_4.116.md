# CC Madhya 4.116

**Bengali:**

এই লাগি’ পুছিলেন ব্রাহ্মণের স্থানে ।
ব্রাহ্মণ কহিল সব ভোগ-বিবরণে ॥ ১১৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ei lāgi’ puchilena brāhmaṇera sthāne
brāhmaṇa kahila saba bhoga-vivaraṇe

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei lāgi’ — for this matter; puchilena — he inquired; brāhmaṇera sthāne — from the brāhmaṇa; kahila — informed; saba — everything; bhoga — foods; vivaraṇe — in description.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the brāhmaṇa priest was questioned about this matter, he explained in detail what kinds of foods were offered to the Deity of Gopīnātha.
