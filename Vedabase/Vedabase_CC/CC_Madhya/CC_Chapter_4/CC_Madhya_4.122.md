# CC Madhya 4.122

**Bengali:**

আরতি দেখিয়া পুরী কৈল নমস্কার ।
বাহিরে আইলা, কারে কিছু না কহিল আর ॥ ১২২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ārati dekhiyā purī kaila namaskāra
bāhire āilā, kāre kichu nā kahila āra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ārati dekhiyā — after seeing the ārati; kaila — offered; namaskāra — obeisances; bāhire āilā — he went out; kāre — to anyone; kichu — anything; nā — not; kahila — say; āra — more.

**Translation:**

Translation
After the ārati was finished, Mādhavendra Purī offered his obeisances to the Deity and then left the temple. He did not say anything more to anyone.
