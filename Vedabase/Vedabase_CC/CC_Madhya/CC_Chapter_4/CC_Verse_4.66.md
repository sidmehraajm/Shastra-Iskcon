# CC Madhya 4.66

**Bengali:**

আরাত্রিক করি’ কৈল বহুত স্তবন ।
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কৈল আত্ম-সমর্পণ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ārātrika kari’ kaila bahuta stavana
daṇḍavat kari’ kaila ātma-samarpaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ārātrika — the performance of ārātrika; kaila — chanted; bahuta — varieties; stavana — of prayers; daṇḍavat — obeisances; kari’ — offering; kaila — did; ātma-samarpaṇa — self-surrender.

**Translation:**

Translation
After the last offering of tāmbūla and pan, bhoga-ārātrika was performed. Finally everyone offered various prayers and then obeisances, falling flat before the Deity in full surrender.
