# CC Madhya 4.70

**Bengali:**

বন্য শাক-ফল-মূলে বিবিধ ব্যঞ্জন ।
কেহ বড়া-বড়ি-কড়ি করে বিপ্ৰগণ ॥ ৭০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vanya śāka-phala-mūle vividha vyañjana
keha baḍā-baḍi-kaḍi kare vipra-gaṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vanya śāka — spinach of the forest; phala — fruits; mūle — with roots; vividha — varieties; vyañjana — vegetables; keha — someone; baḍā-baḍi — baḍā and baḍi; kare — made; vipra-gaṇa — all the brāhmaṇas..

**Translation:**

Translation
The vegetable preparations were made from various kinds of spinach, roots and fruits collected from the forest, and someone made baḍā and baḍi by mashing dhal. In this way the brāhmaṇas prepared all kinds of food.
