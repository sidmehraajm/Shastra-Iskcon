# CC Madhya 11.219

**Bengali:**

কীর্তন দেখি’ সবার মনে হৈল চমৎকার ।
কভু নাহি দেখি ঐছে প্রেমের বিকার ॥ ২১৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kīrtana dekhi’ sabāra mane haila camatkāra
kabhu nāhi dekhi aiche premera vikāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kīrtana dekhi’ — seeing the performance of saṅkīrtana; mane — in the mind; haila — there was; camatkāra — astonishment; kabhu — at any time; nāhi — never; dekhi — see; aiche — such; premera — of ecstatic love; vikāra — transformation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Everyone was astonished to see such a performance of saṅkīrtana, and they all agreed that never before had kīrtana been so performed and ecstatic love of God so exhibited.
