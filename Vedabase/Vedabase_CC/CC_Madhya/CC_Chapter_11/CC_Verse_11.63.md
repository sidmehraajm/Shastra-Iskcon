# CC Madhya 11.63

**Bengali:**

গোপীভাবে বিরহে প্রভু ব্যাকুল হঞা ।
আলালনাথে গেলা প্রভু সবারে ছাড়িয়া ॥ ৬৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gopī-bhāve virahe prabhu vyākula hañā
ālālanāthe gelā prabhu sabāre chāḍiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopī-bhāve — in the mood of the gopīs; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; vyākula — agitated; hañā — being; ālālanāthe — to Ālālanātha; gelā — went; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; sabāre — all; chāḍiyā — having given up.

**Translation:**

Translation
Due to separation from Lord Jagannātha, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu felt the same great anxiety the gopīs feel in separation from Kṛṣṇa. In this condition He gave up all association and went to Ālālanātha.
