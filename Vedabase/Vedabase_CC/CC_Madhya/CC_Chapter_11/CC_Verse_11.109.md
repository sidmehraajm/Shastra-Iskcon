# CC Madhya 11.109

**Bengali:**

মহাপ্রভুর আলয়ে করিল গমন ।
এত মহাপ্রসাদ চাহি’ — কহ কি কারণ ॥ ১০৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mahāprabhura ālaye karila gamana
eta mahā-prasāda cāhi’ — kaha ki kāraṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ālaye — the residential place; karila gamana — he has already gone; eta — so much; kaha — please tell; ki kāraṇa — what is the reason.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Indeed, Vāṇīnātha has already gone to the residence of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and has taken a huge quantity of mahā-prasādam. Please let me know the reason for this.”
