# CC Madhya 13.25

**Bengali:**

সূক্ষ্ম শ্বেতবালু পথে পুলিনের সম ।
দুই দিকে টোটা, সব — যেন বৃন্দাবন ॥ ২৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sūkṣma śveta-bālu pathe pulinera sama
dui dike ṭoṭā, saba — yena vṛndāvana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sūkṣma — fine; śveta-bālu — white sand; pathe — on the path; pulinera</em> <em>sama — just like the bank of the Yamunā; dui dike — on two sides; ṭoṭā — gardens; saba — all; yena — like; vṛndāvana — the holy place Vṛndāvana.

**Translation:**

Translation
The fine, white sand spread all over the path resembled the bank of the Yamunā, and the small gardens on both sides looked just like those in Vṛndāvana.
