# CC Madhya 13.10

**Bengali:**

কটিতটে বদ্ধ, দৃঢ় স্থূল পট্টডোরী ।
দুই দিকে দয়িতাগণ উঠায় তাহা ধরি’ ॥ ১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kaṭi-taṭe baddha, dṛḍha sthūla paṭṭa-ḍorī
dui dike dayitā-gaṇa uṭhāya tāhā dhari’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kaṭi-taṭe — on the waist; baddha — bound; dṛḍha — strong; sthūla — thick; paṭṭa-ḍorī — rope made of silk; dui dike — from two sides; dayitā-gaṇa — the dayitās; tāhā — that rope; dhari’ — catching.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord Jagannātha Deity was bound at the waist by a strong, thick rope made of silk. From two sides the dayitās caught hold of this rope and raised the Deity.
