# CC Madhya 13.32

**Bengali:**

কীর্তনীয়াগণে দিল মাল্য-চন্দন ।
স্বরূপ, শ্রীবাস, — যাহাঁ মুখ্য দুইজন ॥ ৩২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kīrtanīyā-gaṇe dila mālya-candana
svarūpa, śrīvāsa, — yāhāṅ mukhya dui-jana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kīrtanīyā-gaṇe — unto the performers of saṅkīrtana; mālya-candana — garlands and sandalwood pulp; svarūpa — Svarūpa; śrīvāsa — Śrīvāsa; yāhāṅ — where; mukhya — principal; dui-jana — two persons.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord also gave garlands and sandalwood pulp to the performers of saṅkīrtana. The two chief performers were Svarūpa Dāmodara and Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura.
