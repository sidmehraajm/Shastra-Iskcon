# CC Madhya 5.48

**Bengali:**

এইমত বিপ্র চিত্তে চিন্তিতে লাগিল ।
আর দিন লঘুবিপ্র তাঁর ঘরে আইল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ei-mata vipra citte cintite lāgila
āra dina laghu-vipra tāṅra ghare āila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei-mata — in this way; vipra — the elderly brāhmaṇa; cintite — to think; lāgila — began; āra dina — the next day; laghu-vipra — the young brāhmaṇa; ghare — to the home; āila — came.

**Translation:**

Translation
The next day, the elderly brāhmaṇa was thinking deeply about this matter when the young brāhmaṇa came to his house.
