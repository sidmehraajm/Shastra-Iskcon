# CC Madhya 5.10

**Bengali:**

পূর্বে বিদ্যানগরের দুই ত’ ব্রাহ্মণ ।
তীর্থ করিবারে দুঁহে করিলা গমন ॥ ১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

pūrve vidyānagarera dui ta’ brāhmaṇa
tīrtha karibāre duṅhe karilā gamana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pūrve — previously; vidyānagarera — of the town known as Vidyānagara; dui — two; ta’ — certainly; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; duṅhe — both of them; karilā — began; gamana — journey.

**Translation:**

Translation
Formerly at Vidyānagara in South India there were two brāhmaṇas who made a long tour to see different places of pilgrimage.
