# CC Madhya 5.46

**Bengali:**

এত শুনি’ বিপ্রের চিন্তিত হৈল মন ।
একান্ত-ভাবে চিন্তে বিপ্র গোপাল-চরণ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta śuni’ viprera cintita haila mana
ekānta-bhāve cinte vipra gopāla-caraṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta śuni’ — hearing this; viprera — of the old brāhmaṇa; haila — became; mana — the mind; ekānta-bhāve — with single-minded attention; cinte — thinks; vipra — the brāhmaṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the elderly brāhmaṇa heard this, his mind became very much agitated. Feeling helpless, he simply turned his attention to the lotus feet of Gopāla.
