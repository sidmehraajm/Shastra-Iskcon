# CC Madhya 5.84

**Bengali:**

স্ববাক্য ছাড়িতে ইঁহার নাহি কভু মন ।
স্বজন-মৃত্যু-ভয়ে কহে অসত্য-বচন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sva-vākya chāḍite iṅhāra nāhi kabhu mana
svajana-mṛtyu-bhaye kahe asatya-vacana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sva-vākya — his own promise; chāḍite — to give up; iṅhāra — of this brāhmaṇa; kabhu — at any time; mana — the mind; sva-jana — of his own kinsmen; mṛtyu-bhaye — fearing the suicide; kahe — says; asatya-vacana — untruthful words.

**Translation:**

Translation
“He had no desire to break his promise, but fearing that his kinsmen would commit suicide, he deviated from the truth.
