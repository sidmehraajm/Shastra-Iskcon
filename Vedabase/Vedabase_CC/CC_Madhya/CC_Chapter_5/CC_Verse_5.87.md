# CC Madhya 5.87

**Bengali:**

তবে সেই ছোটবিপ্র গেলা বৃন্দাবন ।
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কহে সব বিবরণ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe sei choṭa-vipra gelā vṛndāvana
daṇḍavat kari’ kahe saba vivaraṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — after this; sei — that; choṭa-vipra — young brāhmaṇa; vṛndāvana — to Vṛndāvana; daṇḍavat kari’ — after offering respects; kahe — tells; saba — all; vivaraṇa — the description.

**Translation:**

Translation
After the meeting, the young brāhmaṇa started for Vṛndāvana. Upon arriving there, he first offered his respectful obeisances to the Deity and then narrated everything in full detail.
