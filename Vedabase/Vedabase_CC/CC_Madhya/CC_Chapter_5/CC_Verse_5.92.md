# CC Madhya 5.92

**Bengali:**

আবির্ভাব হঞা আমি তাঁহা সাক্ষী দিব ।
তবে দুই বিপ্রের সত্য প্রতিজ্ঞা রাখিব ॥ ৯২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āvirbhāva hañā āmi tāhāṅ sākṣī diba
tabe dui viprera satya pratijñā rākhiba

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āvirbhāva — appearance; hañā — making; āmi — I; tāhāṅ — there; sākṣī — witness; diba — shall give; tabe — at that time; dui — two; viprera — of the brāhmaṇas; pratijñā — promise; rākhiba — I shall keep.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I shall certainly appear there, and at that time I shall protect the honor of both you brāhmaṇas by bearing witness to the promise.”
