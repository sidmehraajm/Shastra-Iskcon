# CC Madhya 5.108

**Bengali:**

তবে সেই বিপ্র যাই নগরে কহিল ।
শুনিঞা সকল লোক চমৎকার হৈল ॥ ১০৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe sei vipra yāi nagare kahila
śuniñā sakala loka camatkāra haila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — thereafter; sei — that; vipra — brāhmaṇa; nagare — to the town; kahila — said; śuniñā — hearing; sakala — all; loka — the people; camatkāra — struck with wonder; haila — were.

**Translation:**

Translation
The young brāhmaṇa then went to the town and informed all the people about Gopāla’s arrival. Hearing this, the people were struck with wonder.
