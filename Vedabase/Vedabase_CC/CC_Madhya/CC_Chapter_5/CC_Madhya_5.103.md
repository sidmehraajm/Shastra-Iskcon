# CC Madhya 5.103

**Bengali:**

এইমতে চলি’ বিপ্র নিজে-দেশে আইলা ।
গ্রামের নিকট আসি’ মনেতে চিন্তিলা ॥ ১০৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ei-mate cali’ vipra nija-deśe āilā
grāmera nikaṭa āsi’ manete cintilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei-mate — in this way; cali’ — walking; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; deśe — to the country; āilā — returned; grāmera — to the village; nikaṭa — near; āsi’ — coming; manete — within his mind; cintilā — thought.

**Translation:**

Translation
The young brāhmaṇa walked and walked in this way until he eventually arrived in his own country. When he neared his own village, he began to think as follows.
