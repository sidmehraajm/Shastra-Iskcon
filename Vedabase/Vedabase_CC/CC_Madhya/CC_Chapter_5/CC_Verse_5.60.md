# CC Madhya 5.60

**Bengali:**

আর কেহ সঙ্গে নাহি, এই সঙ্গে একল ।
ধুতুরা খাওয়াঞা বাপে করিল পাগল ॥ ৬০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āra keha saṅge nāhi, ei saṅge ekala
dhuturā khāoyāñā bāpe karila pāgala

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āra — anyone; keha — else; saṅge — in company; nāhi — there was not; ei — this brāhmaṇa; ekala — alone; dhuturā — an intoxicant; khāoyāñā — making him eat; bāpe — my father; karila — made; pāgala — mad.

**Translation:**

Translation
“There was no one besides this man with my father. Giving him an intoxicant known as dhuturā to eat, this rogue made my father mad.
