# CC Madhya 9.149

**Bengali:**

‘চতুর্ভুজ-মূর্তি’ দেখায় গোপীগণের আগে ।
সেই ‘কৃষ্ণে’ গোপিকার নহে অনুরাগে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘catur-bhuja-mūrti’ dekhāya gopī-gaṇera āge
sei ‘kṛṣṇe’ gopikāra nahe anurāge

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
catur-bhuja-mūrti — four-handed form; dekhāya — exhibits; gopī-gaṇera — of the gopīs; sei kṛṣṇe — unto that Kṛṣṇa; gopikāra — of the gopīs; anurāge — attraction.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Although Kṛṣṇa assumed the four-armed form of Nārāyaṇa, He could not attract the serious attention of the gopīs in ecstatic love.
