# CC Madhya 9.37

**Bengali:**

সেই কৃষ্ণ তুমি সাক্ষাৎ — ইহা নির্ধারিল ।
এত কহি’ বিপ্র প্রভুর চরণে পড়িল ॥ ৩৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei kṛṣṇa tumi sākṣāt — ihā nirdhārila
eta kahi’ vipra prabhura caraṇe paḍila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei — that; kṛṣṇa — the Personality of Godhead, Kṛṣṇa; tumi — You; sākṣāt — directly; ihā — this; nirdhārila — concluded; eta kahi’ — saying this; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; caraṇe — at the lotus feet; paḍila — fell down.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Sir, You are that Lord Kṛṣṇa Himself. This is my conclusion.” Saying this, the brāhmaṇa fell down at the lotus feet of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
