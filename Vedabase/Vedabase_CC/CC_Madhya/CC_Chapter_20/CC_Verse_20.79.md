# CC Madhya 20.79

**Bengali:**

মহারাষ্ট্রীয় দ্বিজে প্রভু মিলাইলা সনাতনে ।
সেই বিপ্র তাঁরে কৈল মহা-নিমন্ত্রণে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mahārāṣṭrīya dvije prabhu milāilā sanātane
sei vipra tāṅre kaila mahā-nimantraṇe

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahārāṣṭrīya — from Maharashtra; dvije — the brāhmaṇa; milāilā — introduced; sanātane — unto Sanātana Gosvāmī; sei — that; kaila — did; mahā — full; nimantraṇe — invitation.

**Translation:**

Translation
When Caitanya Mahāprabhu introduced the Maharashtrian brāhmaṇa to Sanātana, the brāhmaṇa immediately invited Sanātana Gosvāmī for full meals.
