# CC Madhya 20.358

**Bengali:**

ভাগবতারম্ভে ব্যাস মঙ্গলাচরণে ।
‘পরমেশ্বর’ নিরূপিল এই দুই লক্ষণে ॥ ৩৫৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavatārambhe vyāsa maṅgalācaraṇe
‘parameśvara’ nirūpila ei dui lakṣaṇe

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavata-ārambhe — in the beginning of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; maṅgala-ācaraṇe — in the auspicious invocation; parama-īśvara — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; nirūpila — has described; ei dui lakṣaṇe — by these two characteristics, namely svarūpa (personal) and taṭastha (marginal) symptoms.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In the auspicious invocation at the beginning of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, Śrīla Vyāsadeva has described the Supreme Personality of Godhead by these symptoms.
