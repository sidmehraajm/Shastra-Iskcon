# CC Madhya 23.58

**Bengali:**

অধিরূঢ়-মহাভাব — দুই ত’ প্রকার ।
সম্ভোগে ‘মাদন’, বিরহে ‘মোহন’ নাম তার ॥ ৫৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

adhirūḍha-mahābhāva — dui ta’ prakāra
sambhoge ‘mādana’, virahe ‘mohana’ nāma tāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva — highly advanced ecstasy; dui ta’ prakāra — two varieties; sambhoge — in actually meeting; mādana — mādana; mohana — mohana; tāra — of them.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Highly advanced ecstasy is divided into two categories — mādana and mohana. Meeting together is called mādana, and separation is called mohana.
