# CC Madhya 22.128

**Bengali:**

সাধুসঙ্গ, নামকীর্তন, ভাগবতশ্রবণ ।
মথুরাবাস, শ্রীমূর্তির শ্রদ্ধায় সেবন ॥ ১২৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sādhu-saṅga, nāma-kīrtana, bhāgavata-śravaṇa
mathurā-vāsa, śrī-mūrtira śraddhāya sevana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sādhu-saṅga — association with devotees; nāma-kīrtana — chanting the holy name; bhāgavata-śravaṇa — hearing Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; śrī-mūrtira śraddhāya sevana — worshiping the Deity with faith and veneration.

**Translation:**

Translation
“One should associate with devotees, chant the holy name of the Lord, hear Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, reside at Mathurā and worship the Deity with faith and veneration.
