# CC Madhya 22.93

**Bengali:**

এত সব ছাড়ি’ আর বর্ণাশ্রম-ধর্ম ।
অকিঞ্চন হঞা লয় কৃষ্ণৈক-শরণ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta saba chāḍi’ āra varṇāśrama-dharma
akiñcana hañā laya kṛṣṇaika-śaraṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta saba — all these; chāḍi’ — giving up; āra — and; varṇa-āśrama-dharma — the regulative principle of four varṇas and four āśramas; hañā — becoming; laya — he takes; kṛṣṇa-eka-śaraṇa — exclusive shelter at the lotus feet of the Lord.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Without hesitation, one should take exclusive shelter of Lord Kṛṣṇa with full confidence, giving up bad association and even neglecting the regulative principles of the four varṇas and four āśramas. That is to say, one should abandon all material attachment.
