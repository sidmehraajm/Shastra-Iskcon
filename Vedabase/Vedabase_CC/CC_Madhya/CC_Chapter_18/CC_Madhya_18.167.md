# CC Madhya 18.167

**Bengali:**

কৃষ্ণদাস — রাজপুত, নির্ভয় সে বড় ।
সেই বিপ্র — নির্ভয়, সে — মুখে বড় দড় ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇadāsa — rājaputa, nirbhaya se baḍa
sei vipra — nirbhaya, se — mukhe baḍa daḍa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇadāsa — Kṛṣṇadāsa; rājaputa — belonging to the Rājaputa race; nirbhaya — fearless; se — he; baḍa — very; sei vipra — the Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa; se — he; mukhe — in the mouth; baḍa daḍa — very brave.

**Translation:**

Translation
The devotee Kṛṣṇadāsa, who belonged to the Rājaputa race, was very fearless. The Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa was also fearless, and he spoke very bravely.
