# CC Madhya 18.165

**Bengali:**

এই চারি বাটোয়ার ধুতুরা খাওয়াঞা ।
মারি’ ডারিয়াছে, যতির সব ধন লঞা ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ei cāri bāṭoyāra dhuturā khāoyāñā
māri’ ḍāriyāche, yatira saba dhana lañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei — these; cāri — four; bāṭoyāra — rogues; māri’ ḍāriyāche — killed; yatira — of the sannyāsī; dhana — wealth; lañā — taking away.

**Translation:**

Translation
“These four rogues here must have taken away that sannyāsī’s riches after killing Him by making Him take the poison dhuturā.”
