# CC Madhya 18.215

**Bengali:**

সেই বিপ্রে, কৃষ্ণদাসে, প্রভু বিদায় দিলা ।
যোড়-হাতে দুইজন কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২১৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei vipre, kṛṣṇadāse, prabhu vidāya dilā
yoḍa-hāte dui-jana kahite lāgilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei vipre — to the Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; vidāya dilā — asked to go back; yoḍa-hāte — with folded hands; dui-jana — two persons; kahite lāgilā — began to say.

**Translation:**

Translation
At Soro-kṣetra, the Lord requested the Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa and Rājaputa Kṛṣṇadāsa to return home, but with folded hands they began to speak as follows.
