# CC Madhya 6.271

**Bengali:**

প্রভু কহে, — ‘মুক্তিপদে’র আর অর্থ হয় ।
মুক্তিপদ-শব্দে ‘সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর’ কহয় ॥ ২৭১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe, — ‘mukti-pade’ra āra artha haya
mukti-pada-śabde ‘sākṣāt īśvara’ kahaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — the Lord said; mukti-padera — of the term mukti-pade; artha — meaning; haya — there is; mukti-pada-śabde — by the word mukti-pada; īśvara — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; kahaya — is said.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu replied, “The word ‘mukti-pade’ has another meaning. ‘Mukti-pada’ directly refers to the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
