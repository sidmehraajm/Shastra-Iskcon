# CC Madhya 6.276

**Bengali:**

মুক্তি-শব্দ কহিতে মনে হয় ঘৃণা-ত্রাস ।
ভক্তি-শব্দ কহিতে মনে হয় ত’ উল্লাস ॥ ২৭৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mukti-śabda kahite mane haya ghṛṇā-trāsa
bhakti-śabda kahite mane haya ta’ ullāsa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mukti-śabda — the word mukti; mane — in the mind; haya — there is; ghṛṇā — hatred; trāsa — and fear; bhakti-śabda — the word bhakti; mane — in the mind; haya — there is; ta’ — indeed; ullāsa — transcendental joy.

**Translation:**

Translation
“The very sound of the word ‘mukti’ immediately induces hate and fear, but when we say the word ‘bhakti,’ we naturally feel transcendental bliss within the mind.”
