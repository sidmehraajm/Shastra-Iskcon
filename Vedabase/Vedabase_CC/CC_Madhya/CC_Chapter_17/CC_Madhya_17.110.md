# CC Madhya 17.110

**Bengali:**

‘মহাভাগবত’-লক্ষণ শুনি ভাগবতে ।
সে-সব লক্ষণ প্রকট দেখিয়ে তাঁহাতে ॥ ১১০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘mahā-bhāgavata’-lakṣaṇa śuni bhāgavate
se-saba lakṣaṇa prakaṭa dekhiye tāṅhāte

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-bhāgavata — of a first-class devotee; lakṣaṇa — symptoms; śuni — we hear; bhāgavate — in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; prakaṭa — manifest; dekhiye — I see; tāṅhāte — in Him.

**Translation:**

Translation
“We have heard about the symptoms of a first-class devotee in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, and all those symptoms are manifest in the body of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
