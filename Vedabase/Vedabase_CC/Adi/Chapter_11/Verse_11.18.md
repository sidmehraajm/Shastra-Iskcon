# CC Ādi 11.18

**Bengali:**

শ্ৰীমাধব ঘোষ—মুখ্য কীর্তনীয়াগণে ।
নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু নৃত্য করে যাঁর গানে ॥ ১৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śrī-mādhava ghoṣa — mukhya kīrtanīyā-gaṇe
nityānanda-prabhu nṛtya kare yāṅra gāne

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śrī-mādhava ghoṣa — Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa; mukhya — chief; kīrtanīyā-gaṇe — amongst the performers of saṅkīrtana; nṛtya — dance; kare — does; yāṅra — whose; gāne — in song.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa was a principal performer of kīrtana. While he sang, Nityānanda Prabhu danced.
