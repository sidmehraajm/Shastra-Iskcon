# CC Ādi 13.88

**Bengali:**

নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্তী কহিল গণিয়া ।
এই মাসে পুত্র হবে শুভক্ষণ পাঞা ॥ ৮৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nīlāmbara cakravartī kahila gaṇiyā
ei māse putra habe śubha-kṣaṇa pāñā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nīlāmbara cakravartī — Nīlāmbara Cakravartī; kahila — said; gaṇiyā — by astrological calculation; ei māse — in this month; putra — son; habe — will take birth; śubha-kṣaṇa — auspicious moment; pāñā — taking advantage of.

**Translation:**

Translation
Nīlāmbara Cakravartī [the grandfather of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu] then did an astrological calculation and said that in that very month, taking advantage of an auspicious moment, the child would take birth.
