# CC Ādi 13.28

**Bengali:**

পৌগণ্ড–বয়সে পড়েন, পড়ান শিষ্যগণে ।
সর্বত্র করেন কৃষ্ণনামের ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ২৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

paugaṇḍa-vayase paḍena, paḍāna śiṣyagaṇe
sarvatra karena kṛṣṇa-nāmera vyākhyāne

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
paugaṇḍa-vayase — in the age of paugaṇḍa; paḍāna — teaches; śiṣya-gaṇe — disciples; sarvatra — everywhere; karena — does; kṛṣṇa-nāmera — the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa; vyākhyāne — description.

**Translation:**

Translation
During His paugaṇḍa age He became a serious student and also taught disciples. In this way He used to explain the holy name of Kṛṣṇa everywhere.
