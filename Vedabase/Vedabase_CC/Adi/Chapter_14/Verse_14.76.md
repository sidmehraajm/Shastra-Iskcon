# CC Ādi 14.76

**Bengali:**

কভু পুত্রসঙ্গে শচী করিলা শয়ন ।
দেখে, দিব্যলোক আসি’ ভরিল ভবন ॥ ৭৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kabhu putra-saṅge śacī karilā śayana
dekhe, divyaloka āsi’ bharila bhavana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kabhu — sometimes; putra-saṅge — taking the boy; śacī — mother Śacī; karilā</em> <em>śayana — took rest; dekhe — sees; divya-loka — celestial denizens; āsi’ — coming there; bharila — filled up; bhavana — the whole house.

**Translation:**

Translation
Sometimes, taking her son with her, mother Śacī would lie down on her bed, and she would see that denizens of the celestial world had come there, filling the entire house.
