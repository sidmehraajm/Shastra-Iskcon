# CC Ādi 8.3

**Bengali:**

জয় জয়াদ্বৈত আচার্য কৃপাময় ।
জয় জয় গদাধর পণ্ডিত মহাশয় ॥ ৩॥

**Transliteration:**

jaya jayādvaita ācārya kṛpāmaya
jaya jaya gadādhara paṇḍita mahāśaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jaya jaya — all glories; advaita — unto Advaita Prabhu; ācārya — teacher; kṛpāmaya — very merciful; jaya jaya — all glories to; gadādhara — Gadādhara; paṇḍita — learned scholar; mahāśaya — great personality.

**Translation:**

Translation
Let me offer my respectful obeisances unto Advaita Ācārya, who is very merciful, and also to that great personality Gadādhara Paṇḍita, the learned scholar.
