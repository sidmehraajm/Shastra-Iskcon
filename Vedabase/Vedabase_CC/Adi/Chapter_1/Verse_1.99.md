# CC Ādi 1.99

**Bengali:**

এক ভাগবত বড়—ভাগবত–শাস্ত্র ।
আর ভাগবত—ভক্ত ভক্তি–রস–পাত্র ॥ ৯৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka bhāgavata baḍa — bhāgavata-śāstra
āra bhāgavata — bhakta bhakti-rasa-pātra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka — one; bhāgavata — in relation to the Supreme Lord; baḍa — great; bhāgavata-śāstra — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; bhāgavata — in relation to the Supreme Lord; bhakta — pure devotee; bhakti-rasa — of the mellow of devotion; pātra — the recipient.

**Translation:**

Translation
One of the bhāgavatas is the great scripture Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, and the other is the pure devotee absorbed in the mellows of loving devotion.
