# CC Ādi 4.192

**Bengali:**

গোপী–শোভা দেখি’ কৃষ্ণের শোভা বাড়ে যত ।
কৃষ্ণ–শোভা দেখি’ গোপীর শোভা বাড়ে তত ॥ ১৯২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gopī-śobhā dekhi’ kṛṣṇera śobhā bāḍhe yata
kṛṣṇa-śobhā dekhi’ gopīra śobhā bāḍhe tata

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopī-śobhā — the beauty of the gopīs; kṛṣṇera — of Lord Kṛṣṇa; śobhā — the beauty; bāḍhe — increases; yata — as much as; kṛṣṇa-śobhā — the beauty of Lord Kṛṣṇa; dekhi’ — seeing; gopīra — of the gopīs; bāḍhe — increases; tata — that much.

**Translation:**

Translation
The beauty of Lord Kṛṣṇa increases at the sight of the beauty of the gopīs. And the more the gopīs see Lord Kṛṣṇa’s beauty, the more their beauty increases.
