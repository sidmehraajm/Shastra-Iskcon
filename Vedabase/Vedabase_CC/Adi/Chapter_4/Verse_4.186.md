# CC Ādi 4.186

**Bengali:**

গোপীগণ করে যবে কৃষ্ণ–দরশন ।
সুখবাঞ্ছা নাহি, সুখ হয় কোটিগুণ ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gopī-gaṇa kare yabe kṛṣṇa-daraśana
sukha-vāñchā nāhi, sukha haya koṭi-guṇa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopī-gaṇa — the gopīs; yabe — when; kṛṣṇa-daraśana — seeing Lord Kṛṣṇa; sukha-vāñchā — desire for happiness; nāhi — there is not; sukha — the happiness; haya — there is; koṭi-guṇa — ten million times.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the gopīs see Lord Kṛṣṇa, they derive unbounded bliss, although they have no desire for such pleasure.
