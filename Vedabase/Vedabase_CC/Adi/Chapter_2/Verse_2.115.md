# CC Ādi 2.115

**Bengali:**

কেহো কহে, পরব্যোমে নারায়ণ হরি ।
সকল সম্ভবে কৃষ্ণে, যাতে অবতারী ॥ ১১৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

keho kahe, para-vyome nārāyaṇa hari
sakala sambhave kṛṣṇe, yāte avatārī

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
keho — someone; kahe — says; para-vyome — in the transcendental world; nārāyaṇa — Lord Nārāyaṇa; hari — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; sakala</em> <em>sambhave — all possibilities; kṛṣṇe — in Lord Kṛṣṇa; yāte — since; avatārī — the source of all incarnations.

**Translation:**

Translation
Some call Him Hari, or the Nārāyaṇa of the transcendental world. Everything is possible in Kṛṣṇa, for He is the primeval Lord.
