# CC Ādi 16.74

**Bengali:**

প্রথম–চরণে পঞ্চ ‘ত’–কারের পাঁতি ।
তৃতীয়া–চরণে হয় পঞ্চ ‘রেফ’–স্থিতি ॥৭৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

prathama-caraṇe pañca ‘ta’-kārera pāṅti
tṛtīya-caraṇe haya pañca ‘repha’-sthiti

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prathama-caraṇe — in the first line; pañca — five; ta-karera — of the letter ta; tṛtīya-caraṇe — in the third line; haya — there is; pañca — five; repha — of the letter ra.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In the arrangement of the first line the letter ‘ta’ occurs five times, and the arrangement of the third line repeats the letter ‘ra’ five times.
