# CC Ādi 17.121

**Bengali:**

নগরিয়া লোকে প্রভু যবে আজ্ঞা দিলা ।
ঘরে ঘরে সংকীর্তন করিতে লাগিলা ॥ ১২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

nagariyā loke prabhu yabe ājñā dilā
ghare ghare saṅkīrtana karite lāgilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nagariyā — citizens; loke — all the people; prabhu — the Lord; yabe — when; ājñā — order; dilā — gave; ghare ghare — in each and every home; saṅkīrtana — chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; lāgilā — began.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord ordered all the citizens of Navadvīpa to chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra, and in each and every home they began performing saṅkīrtana regularly.
