# CC Ādi 17.330

**Bengali:**

বৃন্দাবনদাস ইহা ‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে’ ।
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিলা নিত্যানন্দ–আজ্ঞা–বলে ॥ ৩৩০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vṛndāvana-dāsa ihā ‘caitanya-maṅgale’
vistāri’ varṇilā nityānanda-ājñā-bale

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vṛndāvana-dāsa — Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana dāsa; ihā — this; caitanya-maṅgale — in his book Caitanya-maṅgala; varṇilā — described; nityānanda — of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu; ājñā — of the order; bale — on the strength.

**Translation:**

Translation
By the order and strength of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu, Śrīla Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura has elaborately described in his Caitanya-maṅgala all that I have not.
