# CC Ādi 17.199

**Bengali:**

কেহ—হরিদাস, সদা বলে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ।
জানি কার ঘরে ধন করিবেক চুরি ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

keha — haridāsa, sadā bale ‘hari’ ‘hari’
jāni kāra ghare dhana karibeka curi

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
keha — some of them; haridāsa — Haridāsa; sadā — always; bale — says; hari</em> <em>hari — the name of the Lord, “Hari, Hari”; jāni — I understand; kāra — someone’s; ghare — at home; dhana — wealth; karibeka — will do; curi — theft.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘Some of them are called Haridāsa. They always chant “Hari, Hari,” and thus I thought they would steal the riches from someone’s house.

**Purport:**

Purport
Another meaning of “Hari, Hari” is “I am stealing. I am stealing.”
