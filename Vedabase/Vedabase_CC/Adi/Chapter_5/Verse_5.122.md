# CC Ādi 5.122

**Bengali:**

সনকাদি ভাগবত শুনে যাঁর মুখে ।
ভগবানের গুণ কহে, ভাসে প্রেমসুখে ॥ ১২২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sanakādi bhāgavata śune yāṅra mukhe
bhagavānera guṇa kahe, bhāse prema-sukhe

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sanaka-ādi — the great sages headed by Sanaka, Sananda, etc.; bhāgavata — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; yāṅra mukhe — from whose mouth; bhagavānera — of the Personality of Godhead; guṇa — attributes; kahe — say; bhāse — float; prema-sukhe — in the transcendental bliss of love of Godhead.

**Translation:**

Translation
The four Kumāras hear Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam from His lips, and they in turn repeat it in the transcendental bliss of love of Godhead.
