# CC Ādi 5.80

**Bengali:**

সেই পুরুষ সৃষ্টি–স্থিতি–প্রলয়ের কর্তা ।
নানা অবতার করে, জগতের ভর্তা ॥ ৮০ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei puruṣa sṛṣṭi-sthiti-pralayera kartā
nānā avatāra kare, jagatera bhartā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei — that; puruṣa — the Personality of Godhead; sṛṣṭi-sthiti-pralayera — of creation, maintenance and annihilation; kartā — creator; nānā — various; avatāra — incarnations; kare — makes; jagatera — of the material world; bhartā — maintainer.

**Translation:**

Translation
That puruṣa [Kāraṇodakaśāyī Viṣṇu] is the performer of creation, maintenance and destruction. He manifests Himself in many incarnations, for He is the maintainer of the world.
