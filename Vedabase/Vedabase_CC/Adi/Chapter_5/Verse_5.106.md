# CC Ādi 5.106

**Bengali:**

হিরণ্যগর্ভ, অন্তর্যামী, জগৎ–কারণ ।
যাঁর অংশ করি’ করে বিরাট–কল্পন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

hiraṇya-garbha, antaryāmī, jagat-kāraṇa
yāṅra aṁśa kari’ kare virāṭa-kalpana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
hiraṇya-garbha — Hiraṇyagarbha; antaḥ-yāmī — the Supersoul; jagat-kāraṇa — the cause of the material world; yāṅra aṁśa kari’ — taking as His expansion; kare — does; virāṭa-kalpana — conception of the universal form.

**Translation:**

Translation
He is the Supersoul, Hiraṇyagarbha, the cause of the material world. The universal form is conceived as His expansion.
