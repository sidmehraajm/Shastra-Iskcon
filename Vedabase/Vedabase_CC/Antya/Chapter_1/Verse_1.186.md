# CC Antya 1.186

**Bengali:**

padāni tv agatārthāni
tad-artha-gataye narāḥ
yojayanti padair anyaiḥ
sa udghātyaka ucyate

**Transliteration:**

padāni — words; tu — but; agata-arthāni — having an unclear meaning; tat — that; artha-gataye — to understand the meaning; narāḥ — men; yojayanti — join; padaiḥ — with words; anyaiḥ — other; saḥ — that.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
padāni — words; tu — but; agata-arthāni — having an unclear meaning; tat — that; artha-gataye — to understand the meaning; narāḥ — men; yojayanti — join; padaiḥ — with words; anyaiḥ — other; saḥ — that.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘To explain an unclear word, men generally join it with other words. Such an attempt is called udghātyaka.’ ”

**Purport:**

Purport
This verse is quoted from the Sāhitya-darpaṇa (6.289).
