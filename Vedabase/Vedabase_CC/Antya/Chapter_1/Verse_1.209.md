# CC Antya 1.209

**Bengali:**

tabe mahāprabhu saba bhakta lañā gelā
haridāsa-ṭhākura rūpe āliṅgana kailā

**Transliteration:**

tabe — at that time; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; saba — all; bhakta — devotees; lañā — with; gelā — departed from the place; haridāsa-ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; rūpe — Rūpa Gosvāmī; āliṅgana kailā — embraced.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — at that time; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; saba — all; bhakta — devotees; lañā — with; gelā — departed from the place; haridāsa-ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; rūpe — Rūpa Gosvāmī; āliṅgana kailā — embraced.

**Translation:**

Translation
Then, when Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu left with all of His devotees, Haridāsa Ṭhākura also embraced Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī.
