# CC Antya 1.63

**Bengali:**

prasāda khāya, ‘hari’ bale sarva-bhakta-jana
dekhi’ haridāsa-rūpera haraṣita mana

**Transliteration:**

prasāda khāya — eat the prasādam; sarva-bhakta-jana — all the devotees; dekhi’ — seeing this; haridāsa — of Haridāsa Ṭhākura; rūpera — and of Rūpa Gosvāmī; haraṣita — jubilant; mana — the minds.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prasāda khāya — eat the prasādam; sarva-bhakta-jana — all the devotees; dekhi’ — seeing this; haridāsa — of Haridāsa Ṭhākura; rūpera — and of Rūpa Gosvāmī; haraṣita — jubilant; mana — the minds.

**Translation:**

Translation
When Haridāsa Ṭhākura and Rūpa Gosvāmī saw that all the devotees were accepting prasādam and chanting the holy name of Hari, they both were greatly pleased.
