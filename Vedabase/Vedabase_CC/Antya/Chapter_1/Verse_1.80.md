# CC Antya 1.80

**Bengali:**

tāla-patre śloka likhi’ cālete rākhilā
samudra-snāna karibāre rūpa-gosāñi gelā

**Transliteration:**

tāla-patre — on a palm leaf; śloka — the verse; likhi’ — writing; cālete — in the thatched roof; rākhilā — kept it; samudra-snāna — bath in the sea; rūpa-gosāñi — Rūpa Gosvāmī; gelā — departed.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tāla-patre — on a palm leaf; śloka — the verse; likhi’ — writing; cālete — in the thatched roof; rākhilā — kept it; samudra-snāna — bath in the sea; rūpa-gosāñi — Rūpa Gosvāmī; gelā — departed.

**Translation:**

Translation
After writing this verse on a palm leaf, Rūpa Gosvāmī put it somewhere in his thatched roof and went to bathe in the sea.
