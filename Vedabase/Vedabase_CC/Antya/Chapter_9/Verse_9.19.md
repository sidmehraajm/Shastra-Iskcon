# CC Antya 9.19

**Bengali:**

dui-lakṣa kāhana tāra ṭhāñi bākī ha-ila
dui-lakṣa kāhana kauḍi rājā ta’ māgila

**Transliteration:**

dui-lakṣa — 200,000 kāhanas of kauḍis (one kāhana equals 1,280 kauḍis); tāra ṭhāñi — from him; bākī ha-ila — there was a balance due; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas; rājā — the King; ta’ — certainly; māgila — demanded.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dui-lakṣa — 200,000 kāhanas of kauḍis (one kāhana equals 1,280 kauḍis); tāra ṭhāñi — from him; bākī ha-ila — there was a balance due; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas; rājā — the King; ta’ — certainly; māgila — demanded.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Once when he deposited the collection, however, a balance of 200,000 kāhanas of conchshells was due from him. Therefore the King demanded this sum.
