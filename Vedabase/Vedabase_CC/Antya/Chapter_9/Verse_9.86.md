# CC Antya 9.86

**Bengali:**

“gopīnātha-paṭṭanāyake yabe cāṅge caḍāilā
tāra sevaka saba āsi’ prabhure kahilā

**Transliteration:**

gopīnātha-paṭṭanāyake — Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; yabe — when; cāṅge — on the cāṅga; tāra sevaka — his servants; saba — all; āsi’ — coming; prabhure kahilā — informed Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopīnātha-paṭṭanāyake — Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; yabe — when; cāṅge — on the cāṅga; tāra sevaka — his servants; saba — all; āsi’ — coming; prabhure kahilā — informed Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
“When Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka was lifted onto the cāṅga,” he said, “all his servants went to inform Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
