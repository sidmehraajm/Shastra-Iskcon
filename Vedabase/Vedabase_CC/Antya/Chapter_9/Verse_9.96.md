# CC Antya 9.96

**Bengali:**

kon chāra padārtha ei dui-lakṣa kāhana?
prāṇa-rājya karoṅ prabhu-pade nirmañchana”

**Transliteration:**

kon — what; chāra — little; padārtha — matter; ei — this; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas; rājya — kingdom; karoṅ — I do; prabhu-pade — the lotus feet of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nirmañchana — sacrificing.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kon — what; chāra — little; padārtha — matter; ei — this; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas; rājya — kingdom; karoṅ — I do; prabhu-pade — the lotus feet of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nirmañchana — sacrificing.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I do not care about this small sum of 200,000 kāhanas. What to speak of this, I would indeed sacrifice everything at the lotus feet of the Lord, including my life and kingdom.”
