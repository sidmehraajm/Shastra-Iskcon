# CC Antya 10.66

**Bengali:**

sāta-dike sāta-sampradāya gāya, bājāya
madhye mahā-premāveśe nāce gaura-rāya

**Transliteration:**

sāta-dike — in seven directions; sāta-sampradāya — the seven groups; gāya — chant; bājāya — play on the mṛdaṅga; mahā-premāveśe — in great ecstatic love of Kṛṣṇa; nāce — dances; gaura-rāya — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sāta-dike — in seven directions; sāta-sampradāya — the seven groups; gāya — chant; bājāya — play on the mṛdaṅga; mahā-premāveśe — in great ecstatic love of Kṛṣṇa; nāce — dances; gaura-rāya — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
The seven groups began chanting and beating their drums in seven directions, and Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu began dancing in the center in great ecstatic love.
