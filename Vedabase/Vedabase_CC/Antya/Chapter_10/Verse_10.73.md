# CC Antya 10.73

**Bengali:**

prati roma-kūpe haya prasveda, raktodgama
‘jaja’ ‘gaga’ ‘pari’ ‘mumu’ — gadgada vacana

**Transliteration:**

prati roma-kūpe — in every hole of the hair; haya — there was; prasveda — perspiration; rakta-udgama — a profusion of blood; jaja gaga pari mumu — the sounds “jaja gaga pari mumu”; vacana — words.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prati roma-kūpe — in every hole of the hair; haya — there was; prasveda — perspiration; rakta-udgama — a profusion of blood; jaja gaga pari mumu — the sounds “jaja gaga pari mumu”; vacana — words.

**Translation:**

Translation
He bled and perspired from every pore of His body. His voice faltered. Unable to say the line properly, He uttered only “jaja gaga pari mumu.”
