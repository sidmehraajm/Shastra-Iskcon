# CC Antya 10.151

**Bengali:**

eta bali’ dadhi-bhāta karilā bhojana
caitanya-dāsere dilā ucchiṣṭa-bhājana

**Transliteration:**

eta bali’ — saying this; dadhi-bhāta — yogurt with rice; karilā</em> <em>bhojana — ate; caitanya-dāsere — unto Caitanya dāsa; dilā — He offered; ucchiṣṭa-bhājana — the remnants of His food.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms

**Translation:**

Translation
After saying this, the Lord ate the rice mixed with yogurt and offered Caitanya dāsa the remnants of His food.
