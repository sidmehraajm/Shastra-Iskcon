# CC Antya 10.94

**Bengali:**

prabhu kahe, — ‘bhitare tabe āilā kemane?
taiche kene prasāda laite nā kailā gamane?’

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; bhitare — inside; tabe — then; āilā — you came; kemane — how; taiche — in the same way; kene — why; prasāda laite — to take prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; bhitare — inside; tabe — then; āilā — you came; kemane — how; taiche — in the same way; kene — why; prasāda laite — to take prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord asked, “How did you enter the room? Why didn’t you go out to take your lunch in the same way?”
