# CC Antya 10.137

**Bengali:**

bhṛṣṭa phula-baḍī, āra mudga-ḍāli-sūpa
vividha vyañjana rāndhe prabhura ruci-anurūpa

**Transliteration:**

bhṛṣṭa — fried; phula-baḍī — a hot dhal preparation; āra — and; mudga-ḍāli-sūpa — a liquid preparation made from mung dhal; rāndhe — used to cook; prabhura ruci-anurūpa — very tasteful for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhṛṣṭa — fried; phula-baḍī — a hot dhal preparation; āra — and; mudga-ḍāli-sūpa — a liquid preparation made from mung dhal; rāndhe — used to cook; prabhura ruci-anurūpa — very tasteful for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
They also offered phula-baḍī, liquid mung dhal and many vegetables, all cooked according to the Lord’s taste.
