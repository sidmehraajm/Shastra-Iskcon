# CC Antya 10.20

**Bengali:**

sukutā khāile sei āma ha-ibeka nāśa
ei sneha mane bhāvi’ prabhura ullāsa

**Transliteration:**

sukutā khāile — by eating the sukutā; ha-ibeka nāśa — will be vanquished; ei — this; sneha — affection; mane — in the mind; bhāvi’ — thinking of; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ullāsa — delight.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sukutā khāile — by eating the sukutā; ha-ibeka nāśa — will be vanquished; ei — this; sneha — affection; mane — in the mind; bhāvi’ — thinking of; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ullāsa — delight.

**Translation:**

Translation
Because of sincere affection, she thought that eating this sukutā would cure the Lord’s disease. Considering these affectionate thoughts of Damayantī, the Lord was very pleased.
