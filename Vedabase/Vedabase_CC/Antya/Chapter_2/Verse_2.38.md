# CC Antya 2.38

**Bengali:**

eka vatsara teṅho prathama ekeśvara
prabhu dekhibāre āilā utkaṇṭhā-antara

**Transliteration:**

eka vatsara — one year; teṅho — Śrīkānta Sena; prathama — first; ekeśvara — alone; prabhu dekhibāre — to see the Lord; āilā — came; utkaṇṭhā-antara — with great eagerness in the mind.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka vatsara — one year; teṅho — Śrīkānta Sena; prathama — first; ekeśvara — alone; prabhu dekhibāre — to see the Lord; āilā — came; utkaṇṭhā-antara — with great eagerness in the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
One year, Śrīkānta Sena came alone to Jagannātha Purī in great eagerness to see the Lord.
