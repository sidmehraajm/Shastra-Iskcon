# CC Antya 2.102

**Bengali:**

‘choṭa-haridāsa’ nāma prabhura kīrtanīyā
tāhāre kahena ācārya ḍākiyā āniyā

**Transliteration:**

choṭa-haridāsa nāma — a devotee named Choṭa Haridāsa; prabhura kīrtanīyā — a chanter of songs for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; tāhāre — unto him; kahena — says; ācārya — the ācārya.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms

**Translation:**

Translation
A devotee named Choṭa Haridāsa used to sing for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. Bhagavān Ācārya called him to his home and spoke as follows.
