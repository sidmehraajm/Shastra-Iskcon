# CC Antya 2.42

**Bengali:**

śivānande kahiha, — āmi ei pauṣa-māse
ācambite avaśya āmi yāiba tāṅra pāśe

**Transliteration:**

śivānande kahiha — speak to Śivānanda Sena; āmi — I; ei — this; pauṣa-māse — in the month of Pauṣa (December-January); ācambite — all of a sudden; avaśya — certainly; āmi — I; yāiba — shall go; tāṅra pāśe — to his place.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śivānande kahiha — speak to Śivānanda Sena; āmi — I; ei — this; pauṣa-māse — in the month of Pauṣa (December-January); ācambite — all of a sudden; avaśya — certainly; āmi — I; yāiba — shall go; tāṅra pāśe — to his place.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Please inform Śivānanda Sena that in the month of Pauṣa [December-January] I shall certainly go to his home.
