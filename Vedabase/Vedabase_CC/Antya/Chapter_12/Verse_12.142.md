# CC Antya 12.142

**Bengali:**

paṇḍita kahe, — “prabhu yāi’ karuna viśrāma
mui, ebe la-iba prasāda kari’ samādhāna

**Transliteration:**

paṇḍita kahe — Jagadānanda Paṇḍita said; prabhu — my Lord; yāi’ — going; karuna viśrāma — take rest; mui — I; ebe — now; la-iba prasāda — shall take prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
paṇḍita kahe — Jagadānanda Paṇḍita said; prabhu — my Lord; yāi’ — going; karuna viśrāma — take rest; mui — I; ebe — now; la-iba prasāda — shall take prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
Jagadānanda replied, “My Lord, You go take rest. I shall take prasādam after I finish making some arrangements.
