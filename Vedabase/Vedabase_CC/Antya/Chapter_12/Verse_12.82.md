# CC Antya 12.82

**Bengali:**

cale saba bhakta-gaṇa rodana kariyā
mahāprabhu rahilā ghare viṣaṇṇa hañā

**Transliteration:**

cale — proceed; saba — all; bhakta-gaṇa — the devotees; rodana</em> <em>kariyā — crying; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; rahilā — remained; ghare — at His place; viṣaṇṇa hañā — being very morose.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms

**Translation:**

Translation
The devotees of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu began their journey crying, while the Lord remained morosely at His residence.
