# CC Antya 7.75

**Bengali:**

caudda mādala bāje ucca saṅkīrtana
eka eka nartakera preme bhāsila bhuvana

**Transliteration:**

caudda mādala — fourteen mṛdaṅgas; ucca saṅkīrtana — loud congregational chanting; eka eka — of each group; nartakera — of the dancer; preme — in ecstatic love; bhāsila bhuvana — inundated the entire world.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
caudda mādala — fourteen mṛdaṅgas; ucca saṅkīrtana — loud congregational chanting; eka eka — of each group; nartakera — of the dancer; preme — in ecstatic love; bhāsila bhuvana — inundated the entire world.

**Translation:**

Translation
Fourteen mṛdaṅgas resounded with the loud congregational chanting, and in each group was a dancer whose dance of ecstatic love inundated the entire world.
