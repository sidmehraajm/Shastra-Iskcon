# CC Antya 7.81

**Bengali:**

“bhāgavatera ṭīkā kichu kariyāchi likhana
āpane mahāprabhu yadi karena śravaṇa”

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavatera — on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; kichu — some; kariyāchi likhana — I have written; āpane — You; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; yadi — if; karena śravaṇa — would hear.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavatera — on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; kichu — some; kariyāchi likhana — I have written; āpane — You; mahāprabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; yadi — if; karena śravaṇa — would hear.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I have written some commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,” he said. “Would Your Lordship kindly hear it?”
