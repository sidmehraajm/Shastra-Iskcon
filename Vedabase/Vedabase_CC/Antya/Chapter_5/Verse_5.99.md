# CC Antya 5.99

**Bengali:**

svarūpera ṭhāñi ācārya kailā nivedana
eka vipra prabhura nāṭaka kariyāche uttama

**Transliteration:**

svarūpera ṭhāñi — before Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; ācārya — Bhagavān Ācārya; kailā — did; nivedana — submission; eka vipra — one brāhmaṇa; nāṭaka — drama; kariyāche — has composed; uttama — very nice.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
svarūpera ṭhāñi — before Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; ācārya — Bhagavān Ācārya; kailā — did; nivedana — submission; eka vipra — one brāhmaṇa; nāṭaka — drama; kariyāche — has composed; uttama — very nice.

**Translation:**

Translation
Bhagavān Ācārya submitted to Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī, “A good brāhmaṇa has prepared a drama about Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu that appears exceptionally well composed.
