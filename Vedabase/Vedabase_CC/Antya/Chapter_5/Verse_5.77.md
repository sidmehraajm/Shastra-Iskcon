# CC Antya 5.77

**Bengali:**

prabhu kahe, — “rāmānanda vinayera khani
āpanāra kathā para-muṇḍe dena āni’

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe — the Lord replied; rāmānanda — Rāmānanda Rāya; vinayera khani — a mine of humility; āpanāra kathā — his own words; para-muṇḍe — on another’s head; dena — confers; āni’ — bringing.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — the Lord replied; rāmānanda — Rāmānanda Rāya; vinayera khani — a mine of humility; āpanāra kathā — his own words; para-muṇḍe — on another’s head; dena — confers; āni’ — bringing.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said, “Rāmānanda Rāya is a mine of great humility. Therefore he has attributed his own words to another’s intelligence.
