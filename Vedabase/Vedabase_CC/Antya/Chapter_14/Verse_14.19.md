# CC Antya 14.19

**Bengali:**

maṇḍalī-bandhe gopī-gaṇa karena nartana
madhye rādhā-saha nāce vrajendra-nandana

**Transliteration:**

maṇḍalī-bandhe — in a circle; gopī-gaṇa — the gopīs; madhye — in the middle; rādhā-saha — with Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; nāce — dances; vrajendra-nandana — Kṛṣṇa, the son of Mahārāja Nanda.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
maṇḍalī-bandhe — in a circle; gopī-gaṇa — the gopīs; madhye — in the middle; rādhā-saha — with Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; nāce — dances; vrajendra-nandana — Kṛṣṇa, the son of Mahārāja Nanda.

**Translation:**

Translation
The gopīs were dancing in a circle, and in the middle of that circle, Kṛṣṇa, the son of Mahārāja Nanda, danced with Rādhārāṇī.
