# CC Antya 14.52

**Bengali:**

kṛṣṇera viyoge gopīra daśa daśā haya
sei daśa daśā haya prabhura udaya

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇera viyoge — by separation from Kṛṣṇa; gopīra — of the gopīs; haya — there are; sei — those; daśa daśā — ten kinds of transcendental transformations; haya — are; prabhura udaya — visible in the body of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇera viyoge — by separation from Kṛṣṇa; gopīra — of the gopīs; haya — there are; sei — those; daśa daśā — ten kinds of transcendental transformations; haya — are; prabhura udaya — visible in the body of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the gopīs felt separation from Kṛṣṇa, they experienced ten kinds of bodily transformations. These same symptoms appeared in the body of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
