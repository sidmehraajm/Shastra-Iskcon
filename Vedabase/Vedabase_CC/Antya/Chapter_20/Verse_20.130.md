# CC Antya 20.130

**Bengali:**

mahā-prasādera tāhāṅ mahimā varṇilā
kṛṣṇādharāmṛtera phala-śloka āsvādilā

**Transliteration:**

mahā-prasādera — of the remnants of the food of the Lord, mahā-prasādam; mahimā — the glories; varṇilā — described; kṛṣṇa-adhara-amṛtera — of the nectar from the lips of Kṛṣṇa; phala-śloka — the verse mentioning the result; āsvādilā — tasted.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-prasādera — of the remnants of the food of the Lord, mahā-prasādam; mahimā — the glories; varṇilā — described; kṛṣṇa-adhara-amṛtera — of the nectar from the lips of Kṛṣṇa; phala-śloka — the verse mentioning the result; āsvādilā — tasted.

**Translation:**

Translation
Also in that chapter, the glories of mahā-prasādam are explained, and a verse is tasted describing the effect of nectar from the lips of Kṛṣṇa.
