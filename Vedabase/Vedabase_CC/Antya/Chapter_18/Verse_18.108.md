# CC Antya 18.108

**Bengali:**

keha kare vījana, keha pāda-samvāhana,
keha karāya tāmbūla bhakṣaṇa
rādhā-kṛṣṇa nidrā gelā, sakhī-gaṇa śayana kailā,
dekhi’ āmāra sukhī haila mana

**Transliteration:**

keha — someone; kare — does; vījana — fanning; keha — someone; pāda-samvāhana — massaging of the feet; keha — someone; karāya — made Them do; tāmbūla bhakṣaṇa — eating a preparation of betel leaves; rādhā-kṛṣṇa — Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; nidrā gelā — went to sleep; sakhī-gaṇa — all the gopīs; dekhi’ — seeing; āmāra — My; sukhī — happy; haila — became; mana — mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Some of the gopīs fanned Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, others massaged Their feet, and some fed Them betel leaves to chew. When Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa fell asleep, all the gopīs also lay down. When I saw this, My mind was very happy.
