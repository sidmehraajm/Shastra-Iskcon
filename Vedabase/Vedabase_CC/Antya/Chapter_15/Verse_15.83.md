# CC Antya 15.83

**Bengali:**

svarūpa-gosāñi tabe madhura kariyā
gīta-govindera pada gāya prabhure śunāñā

**Transliteration:**

svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; tabe — thereafter; madhura kariyā — very sweetly; gīta-govindera — of the book Gīta-govinda; gāya — sings; prabhure — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; śunāñā — making to hear.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; tabe — thereafter; madhura kariyā — very sweetly; gīta-govindera — of the book Gīta-govinda; gāya — sings; prabhure — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; śunāñā — making to hear.

**Translation:**

Translation
Thus for the pleasure of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī began very sweetly singing the following verse from the Gīta-govinda.
