# CC Antya 11.16

**Bengali:**

eka-dina govinda mahā-prasāda lañā
haridāse dite gelā ānandita hañā

**Transliteration:**

eka-dina — one day; govinda — the personal servant of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; mahā-prasāda lañā — taking mahā-prasādam; gelā — went; ānandita hañā — in great jubilation.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka-dina — one day; govinda — the personal servant of Lord Caitanya Mahāprabhu; mahā-prasāda lañā — taking mahā-prasādam; gelā — went; ānandita hañā — in great jubilation.

**Translation:**

Translation
One day Govinda, the personal servant of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, went in great jubilation to deliver the remnants of Lord Jagannātha’s food to Haridāsa Ṭhākura.
