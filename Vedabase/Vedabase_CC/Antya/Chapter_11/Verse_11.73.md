# CC Antya 11.73

**Bengali:**

siṁha-dvāre āsi’ prabhu pasārira ṭhāṅi
āṅcala pātiyā prasāda māgilā tathāi

**Transliteration:**

siṁha-dvāre āsi’ — coming in front of the Siṁha-dvāra; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; pasārira ṭhāṅi — from all the shopkeepers; āṅcala pātiyā — spreading His cloth; prasāda — Jagannātha’s prasādam; tathāi — there.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
siṁha-dvāre āsi’ — coming in front of the Siṁha-dvāra; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; pasārira ṭhāṅi — from all the shopkeepers; āṅcala pātiyā — spreading His cloth; prasāda — Jagannātha’s prasādam; tathāi — there.

**Translation:**

Translation
Approaching the Siṁha-dvāra gate, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu spread His cloth and began to beg prasādam from all the shopkeepers there.
