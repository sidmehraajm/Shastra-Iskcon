# CC Antya 11.18

**Bengali:**

govinda kahe, — ‘uṭha āsi’ karaha bhojana’
haridāsa kahe, — āji karimu laṅghana

**Transliteration:**

govinda kahe — Govinda said; uṭha — please get up; āsi’ — coming; karaha bhojana — take your prasādam; āji — today; karimu laṅghana — I shall observe fasting.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
govinda kahe — Govinda said; uṭha — please get up; āsi’ — coming; karaha bhojana — take your prasādam; āji — today; karimu laṅghana — I shall observe fasting.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Please rise and take your mahā-prasādam,” Govinda said.

**Purport:**

Purport
Haridāsa Ṭhākura replied, “Today I shall observe fasting.
