# CC Antya 6.326

**Bengali:**

āpana-uddhāra ei raghunātha-dāsa
‘gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe’ kariyāchena prakāśa

**Transliteration:**

āpana-uddhāra — his personal deliverance; ei raghunātha-dāsa — this Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — in his poem known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āpana-uddhāra — his personal deliverance; ei raghunātha-dāsa — this Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — in his poem known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Translation:**

Translation
In his poem known as the Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, Raghunātha dāsa has described his personal deliverance.
