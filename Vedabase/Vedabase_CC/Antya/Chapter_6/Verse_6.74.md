# CC Antya 6.74

**Bengali:**

prabhu kahe, — “e-dravya dine kariye bhojana
rātrye tomāra ghare prasāda karimu bhakṣaṇa

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe — Lord Nityānanda Prabhu said; e-dravya — this food; dine — during the daytime; kariye bhojana — let Me eat; rātrye — at night; tomāra ghare — in your house; prasāda — the prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — Lord Nityānanda Prabhu said; e-dravya — this food; dine — during the daytime; kariye bhojana — let Me eat; rātrye — at night; tomāra ghare — in your house; prasāda — the prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
Lord Nityānanda replied, “Let Me eat all this food here during the day, and I shall eat at your home at night.
