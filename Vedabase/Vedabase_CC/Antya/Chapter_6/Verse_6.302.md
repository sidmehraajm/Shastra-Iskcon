# CC Antya 6.302

**Bengali:**

jala-tulasīra sevāya tāṅra yata sukhodaya
ṣoḍaśopacāra-pūjāya tata sukha naya

**Transliteration:**

jala-tulasīra sevāya — by worshiping with water and tulasī; yata — as much as; sukha-udaya — rise of transcendental happiness; ṣoḍaśa-upacāra-pūjāya — by worshiping with sixteen kinds of paraphernalia; tata — so much; sukha — happiness; naya — is not.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jala-tulasīra sevāya — by worshiping with water and tulasī; yata — as much as; sukha-udaya — rise of transcendental happiness; ṣoḍaśa-upacāra-pūjāya — by worshiping with sixteen kinds of paraphernalia; tata — so much; sukha — happiness; naya — is not.

**Translation:**

Translation
The amount of transcendental bliss that Raghunātha dāsa enjoyed simply by offering water and tulasī is impossible to achieve even if one worships the Deity with sixteen kinds of paraphernalia.
