# CC Antya 6.305

**Bengali:**

tabe aṣṭa-kauḍira khājā kare samarpaṇa
svarūpa-ājñāya govinda tāhā kare samādhāna

**Transliteration:**

tabe — then; aṣṭa-kauḍira — costing eight kauḍis; svarūpa-ājñāya — by the order of Svarūpa Dāmodara; govinda — the personal servant of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; tāhā — that; kare samādhāna — arranges.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — then; aṣṭa-kauḍira — costing eight kauḍis; svarūpa-ājñāya — by the order of Svarūpa Dāmodara; govinda — the personal servant of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; tāhā — that; kare samādhāna — arranges.

**Translation:**

Translation
Raghunātha dāsa then began offering the costly sweetmeats known as khājā, which Govinda, following the order of Svarūpa Dāmodara, would supply.
