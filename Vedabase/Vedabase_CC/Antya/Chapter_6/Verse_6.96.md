# CC Antya 6.96

**Bengali:**

puṣpa-mālā vipra āni’ prabhu-gale dila
candana āniyā prabhura sarvāṅge lepila

**Transliteration:**

puṣpa-mālā — a flower garland; vipra — a brāhmaṇa; prabhu-gale — on the neck of Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; dila — placed; candana āniyā — bringing sandalwood pulp; prabhura — of Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; sarvāṅge lepila — smeared all over the body.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
puṣpa-mālā — a flower garland; vipra — a brāhmaṇa; prabhu-gale — on the neck of Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; dila — placed; candana āniyā — bringing sandalwood pulp; prabhura — of Lord Nityānanda Prabhu; sarvāṅge lepila — smeared all over the body.

**Translation:**

Translation
Then a brāhmaṇa brought a flower garland, placed the garland on Nityānanda Prabhu’s neck and smeared sandalwood pulp all over His body.
