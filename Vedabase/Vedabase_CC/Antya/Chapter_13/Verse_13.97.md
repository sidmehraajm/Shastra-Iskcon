# CC Antya 13.97

**Bengali:**

rāmadāsa kahe, — “āmi śūdra adhama!
‘brāhmaṇera sevā’, — ei mora nija-dharma

**Transliteration:**

rāmadāsa kahe — Rāmadāsa said; āmi — I; śūdra — a śūdra; brāhmaṇera sevā — to serve a brāhmaṇa; mora nija-dharma — my own religious duty.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rāmadāsa kahe — Rāmadāsa said; āmi — I; śūdra — a śūdra; brāhmaṇera sevā — to serve a brāhmaṇa; mora nija-dharma — my own religious duty.

**Translation:**

Translation
Rāmadāsa replied, “I am a śūdra, a fallen soul. To serve a brāhmaṇa is my duty and religious principle.
