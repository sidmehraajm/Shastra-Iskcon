# CC Antya 4.40

**Bengali:**

‘raghunāthera pāda-padme veciyāchoṅ māthā
kāḍite nā pāroṅ māthā, pāṅa baḍa vyathā

**Transliteration:**

raghunāthera — of Lord Rāmacandra; pāda-padme — at the lotus feet; veciyāchoṅ</em> <em>māthā — I have sold my head; kāḍite — to take away; nā pāroṅ — I am unable; māthā — the head; pāṅa — I get; baḍa vyathā — too much pain.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
raghunāthera — of Lord Rāmacandra; pāda-padme — at the lotus feet; veciyāchoṅ</em> <em>māthā — I have sold my head; kāḍite — to take away; nā pāroṅ — I am unable; māthā — the head; pāṅa — I get; baḍa vyathā — too much pain.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘I have sold my head at the lotus feet of Lord Rāmacandra. I cannot take it away. That would be too painful for me.
