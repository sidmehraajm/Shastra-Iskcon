# CC Antya 4.215

**Bengali:**

gauḍe ye artha chila, tāhā ānāilā
kuṭumba-brāhmaṇa-devālaye bāṅṭi’ dilā

**Transliteration:**

gauḍe — in Bengal; ye — whatever; artha — money; chila — there was; tāhā — that; ānāilā — collected; kuṭumba — to relatives; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; bāṅṭi’ dilā — divided and distributed.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gauḍe — in Bengal; ye — whatever; artha — money; chila — there was; tāhā — that; ānāilā — collected; kuṭumba — to relatives; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; bāṅṭi’ dilā — divided and distributed.

**Translation:**

Translation
He collected whatever money he had accumulated in Bengal and divided it among his relatives, the brāhmaṇas and the temples.
