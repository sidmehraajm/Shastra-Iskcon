# CC Antya 4.53

**Bengali:**

divya prasāda pāya nitya jagannātha-mandire
tāhā āni’ nitya avaśya dena doṅhākāre

**Transliteration:**

divya — first class; prasāda — prasādam; nitya — daily; jagannātha-mandire — at the temple of Lord Jagannātha; tāhā āni’ — bringing that; nitya — daily; avaśya — certainly; dena — delivers; doṅhākāre — to both of them.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
divya — first class; prasāda — prasādam; nitya — daily; jagannātha-mandire — at the temple of Lord Jagannātha; tāhā āni’ — bringing that; nitya — daily; avaśya — certainly; dena — delivers; doṅhākāre — to both of them.

**Translation:**

Translation
The offerings of prasādam in the temple of Lord Jagannātha were of the highest quality. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu would bring this prasādam and deliver it to the two devotees.
