# CC Antya 3.219

**Bengali:**

mahā-mahā-vipra ethā kulīna-samāja
nīce ādara kara, nā vāsaha bhaya lāja!!

**Transliteration:**

mahā-mahā-vipra — great, great brāhmaṇas; kulīna-samāja — aristocratic society; nīce — to a low-class person; ādara kara — You show honor; nā vāsaha — You do not care for; bhaya lāja — fear or shame.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-mahā-vipra — great, great brāhmaṇas; kulīna-samāja — aristocratic society; nīce — to a low-class person; ādara kara — You show honor; nā vāsaha — You do not care for; bhaya lāja — fear or shame.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Sir, You are living within a society of great, great brāhmaṇas and aristocrats, but without fear or shame You adore a lower-class man like me.
