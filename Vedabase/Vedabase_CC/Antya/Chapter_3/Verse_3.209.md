# CC Antya 3.209

**Bengali:**

tina dina bhitare sei viprera ‘kuṣṭha’ haila
ati ucca nāsā tāra galiyā paḍila

**Transliteration:**

tina dina — three days; bhitare — within; sei — that; viprera — of the brāhmaṇa; haila — appeared; ati — very; ucca — raised; nāsā — nose; tāra — his; galiyā — melting; paḍila — fell.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tina dina — three days; bhitare — within; sei — that; viprera — of the brāhmaṇa; haila — appeared; ati — very; ucca — raised; nāsā — nose; tāra — his; galiyā — melting; paḍila — fell.

**Translation:**

Translation
Within three days that brāhmaṇa was attacked by leprosy, and as a result his highly raised nose melted away and fell off.
