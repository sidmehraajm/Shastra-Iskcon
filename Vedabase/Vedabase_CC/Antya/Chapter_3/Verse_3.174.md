# CC Antya 3.174

**Bengali:**

aneka paṇḍita sabhāya, brāhmaṇa, sajjana
dui bhāi mahā-paṇḍita — hiraṇya, govardhana

**Transliteration:**

aneka paṇḍita — many learned scholars; sabhāya — in that assembly; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; dui bhāi — the two brothers; mahā-paṇḍita — very learned scholars; hiraṇya — Hiraṇya; govardhana — Govardhana.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aneka paṇḍita — many learned scholars; sabhāya — in that assembly; brāhmaṇa — brāhmaṇas; dui bhāi — the two brothers; mahā-paṇḍita — very learned scholars; hiraṇya — Hiraṇya; govardhana — Govardhana.

**Translation:**

Translation
In that assembly were many learned scholars, brāhmaṇas and respectable gentlemen. The two brothers Hiraṇya and Govardhana were also greatly learned.
