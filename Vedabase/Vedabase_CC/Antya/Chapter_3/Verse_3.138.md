# CC Antya 3.138

**Bengali:**

eta bali’ tāre ‘nāma’ upadeśa kari’
uṭhiyā calilā ṭhākura bali’ ‘hari’ ‘hari’

**Transliteration:**

eta bali’ — saying this; tāre — her; nāma upadeśa kari’ — instructing about the process of chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; calilā — left; ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; bali’ — chanting; hari hari — the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra..

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bali’ — saying this; tāre — her; nāma upadeśa kari’ — instructing about the process of chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; calilā — left; ṭhākura — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; bali’ — chanting; hari hari — the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra..

**Translation:**

Translation
After thus instructing the prostitute about the process of chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra, Haridāsa Ṭhākura stood up and left, continuously chanting “Hari, Hari.”
