# CC Antya 3.203

**Bengali:**

śuni’ haridāsa tabe uṭhiyā calilā
majumadāra sei vipre tyāga karilā

**Transliteration:**

śuni’ — hearing; haridāsa — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; tabe — then; uṭhiyā calilā — got up and began to go away; majumadāra — Hiraṇya and Govardhana Majumadāra; sei vipre — this brāhmaṇa.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuni’ — hearing; haridāsa — Haridāsa Ṭhākura; tabe — then; uṭhiyā calilā — got up and began to go away; majumadāra — Hiraṇya and Govardhana Majumadāra; sei vipre — this brāhmaṇa.

**Translation:**

Translation
Then Haridāsa Ṭhākura got up to leave, and the Majumadāras, the masters of Gopāla Cakravartī, immediately kicked him out and dismissed him from their service.
