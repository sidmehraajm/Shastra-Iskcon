# CC Antya 16.48

**Bengali:**

sarvajña-śiromaṇi caitanya īśvara
vaiṣṇave tāṅhāra viśvāsa, jānena antara

**Transliteration:**

sarva-jña — omniscient; śiromaṇi — topmost; caitanya — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; īśvara — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; vaiṣṇave — in the Vaiṣṇavas; tāṅhāra viśvāsa — his faith; jānena — He knows; antara — the heart.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sarva-jña — omniscient; śiromaṇi — topmost; caitanya — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; īśvara — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; vaiṣṇave — in the Vaiṣṇavas; tāṅhāra viśvāsa — his faith; jānena — He knows; antara — the heart.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu is the most exalted, omniscient Supreme Personality of Godhead, and therefore He knew that Kālidāsa, in the core of his heart, had full faith in Vaiṣṇavas.
