# CC Antya 16.114

**Bengali:**

aneka ‘sukṛte’ ihā hañāche samprāpti
sabe ei āsvāda kara kari’ mahā-bhakti”

**Transliteration:**

aneka — many; sukṛte — by pious activities; ihā — this; hañāche samprāpti — has become available; sabe — all of you; ei — this prasādam; kari’ mahā-bhakti — with great devotion.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aneka — many; sukṛte — by pious activities; ihā — this; hañāche samprāpti — has become available; sabe — all of you; ei — this prasādam; kari’ mahā-bhakti — with great devotion.

**Translation:**

Translation
“This prasādam has been made available only as a result of many pious activities. Now taste it with great faith and devotion.”
