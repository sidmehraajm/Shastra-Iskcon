# CC Antya 5.61

**Bengali:**

ānera ki kathā, tumi — prabhura upadeṣṭā!
āmi ta’ bhikṣuka vipra, tumi — mora poṣṭā

**Transliteration:**

ānera ki kathā — what to speak of others; tumi — you; prabhura upadeṣṭā — an instructor of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; āmi — I; ta’ — certainly; bhikṣuka — beggar; vipra — brāhmaṇa; mora — my; poṣṭā — maintainer.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ānera ki kathā — what to speak of others; tumi — you; prabhura upadeṣṭā — an instructor of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; āmi — I; ta’ — certainly; bhikṣuka — beggar; vipra — brāhmaṇa; mora — my; poṣṭā — maintainer.

**Translation:**

Translation
“You are an instructor even for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, what to speak of others. I am but a beggar brāhmaṇa, and you are my maintainer.
