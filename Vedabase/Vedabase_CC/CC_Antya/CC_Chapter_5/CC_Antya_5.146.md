# CC Antya 5.146

**Bengali:**

ei-mata śiśupāla karila nindana
sei-vākye sarasvatī karena stavana

**Transliteration:**

ei-mata — in this way; śiśupāla — Śiśupāla; karila nindana — blasphemed; sei-vākye — by those words; sarasvatī — the goddess of learning; karena stavana — offers prayers.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei-mata — in this way; śiśupāla — Śiśupāla; karila nindana — blasphemed; sei-vākye — by those words; sarasvatī — the goddess of learning; karena stavana — offers prayers.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Śiśupāla also blasphemed Kṛṣṇa in this way, but the goddess of learning, Sarasvatī, offered her prayers to Kṛṣṇa even by his words.
