# CC Antya 5.91

**Bengali:**

baṅga-deśī eka vipra prabhura carite
nāṭaka kari’ lañā āila prabhuke śunāite

**Transliteration:**

baṅga-deśī — from Bengal; eka vipra — one brāhmaṇa; nāṭaka kari’ — writing a drama; lañā — taking; āila — came; prabhuke śunāite — to induce Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu to hear.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
baṅga-deśī — from Bengal; eka vipra — one brāhmaṇa; nāṭaka kari’ — writing a drama; lañā — taking; āila — came; prabhuke śunāite — to induce Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu to hear.

**Translation:**

Translation
A brāhmaṇa from Bengal wrote a drama about the characteristics of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and came with his manuscript to induce the Lord to hear it.
