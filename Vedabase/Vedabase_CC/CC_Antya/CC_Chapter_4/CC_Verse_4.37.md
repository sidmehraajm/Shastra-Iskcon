# CC Antya 4.37

**Bengali:**

“tomā-duṅhāra ājñā āmi kemane laṅghimu?
dīkṣā-mantra deha’ kṛṣṇa-bhajana karimu”

**Transliteration:**

tomā — of you; duṅhāra — of both; ājñā — the order; āmi — I; kemane — how; laṅghimu — shall disobey; dīkṣā — initiation; mantra — mantra; kṛṣṇa-bhajana — devotional service to Kṛṣṇa; karimu — I shall perform.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tomā — of you; duṅhāra — of both; ājñā — the order; āmi — I; kemane — how; laṅghimu — shall disobey; dīkṣā — initiation; mantra — mantra; kṛṣṇa-bhajana — devotional service to Kṛṣṇa; karimu — I shall perform.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Vallabha replied, ‘My dear brothers, how can I disobey your orders? Initiate me into the Kṛṣṇa mantra so that I may perform devotional service to Lord Kṛṣṇa.’
