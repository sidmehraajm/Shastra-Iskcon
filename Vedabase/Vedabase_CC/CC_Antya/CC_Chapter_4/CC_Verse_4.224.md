# CC Antya 4.224

**Bengali:**

‘ujjvala-nīlamaṇi’-nāma grantha kaila āra
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa tāhāṅ pāiye pāra

**Transliteration:**

ujjvala-nīlamaṇi — Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi; grantha — scripture; kaila — compiled; āra — also; rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa — the transcendental mellows of the pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; tāhāṅ — there; pāiye — we get; pāra — the extreme limit.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ujjvala-nīlamaṇi — Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi; grantha — scripture; kaila — compiled; āra — also; rādhā-kṛṣṇa-līlā-rasa — the transcendental mellows of the pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; tāhāṅ — there; pāiye — we get; pāra — the extreme limit.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī also compiled the book named Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, from which one can understand, to the fullest limits, the loving affairs of Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.
