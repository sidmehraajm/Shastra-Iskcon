# CC Antya 4.33

**Bengali:**

āmā-sabā-saṅge kṛṣṇa-kathā, bhāgavata śune
tāhāra parīkṣā kailuṅ āmi-dui-jane

**Transliteration:**

āmā-sabā — all of us; saṅge — with; kṛṣṇa-kathā — talks about Lord Kṛṣṇa; bhāgavata śune — hears Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; parīkṣā — examination; kailuṅ — did; āmi-dui-jane — both of us.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āmā-sabā — all of us; saṅge — with; kṛṣṇa-kathā — talks about Lord Kṛṣṇa; bhāgavata śune — hears Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; parīkṣā — examination; kailuṅ — did; āmi-dui-jane — both of us.

**Translation:**

Translation
“He heard Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam and talks about Lord Kṛṣṇa with us, and both of us examined him.
