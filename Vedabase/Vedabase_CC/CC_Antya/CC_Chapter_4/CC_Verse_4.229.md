# CC Antya 4.229

**Bengali:**

‘bhāgavata-sandarbha’-nāma kaila grantha-sāra
bhāgavata-siddhāntera tāhāṅ pāiye pāra

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavata-sandarbha — the Bhāgavata-sandarbha, which is also known as the Ṣaṭ-sandarbha; kaila — made; grantha-sāra — the essence of all scriptures; bhāgavata-siddhāntera — of conclusive information about the Supreme Personality of Godhead and His service; tāhāṅ — there; pāiye — we get; pāra — the limit.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavata-sandarbha — the Bhāgavata-sandarbha, which is also known as the Ṣaṭ-sandarbha; kaila — made; grantha-sāra — the essence of all scriptures; bhāgavata-siddhāntera — of conclusive information about the Supreme Personality of Godhead and His service; tāhāṅ — there; pāiye — we get; pāra — the limit.

**Translation:**

Translation
In particular, Śrīla Jīva Gosvāmī compiled the book named Bhāgavata-sandarbha, or Ṣaṭ-sandarbha, which is the essence of all scriptures. From this book one can obtain a conclusive understanding of devotional service and the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
