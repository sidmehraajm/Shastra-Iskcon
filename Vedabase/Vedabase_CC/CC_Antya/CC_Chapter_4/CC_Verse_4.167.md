# CC Antya 4.167

**Bengali:**

kāhāṅ tumi — prāmāṇika, śāstre pravīṇa!
kāhāṅ jagā — kālikāra baṭuyā navīna!

**Transliteration:**

kāhāṅ — where; tumi — you; prāmāṇika — authority; śāstre pravīṇa — experienced in the learning of the śāstras; jagā — Jagā; kālikāra — recent; baṭuyā — youth; navīna — new.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kāhāṅ — where; tumi — you; prāmāṇika — authority; śāstre pravīṇa — experienced in the learning of the śāstras; jagā — Jagā; kālikāra — recent; baṭuyā — youth; navīna — new.

**Translation:**

Translation
“You are an experienced authority in the śāstras, whereas Jagā is just a young boy.
