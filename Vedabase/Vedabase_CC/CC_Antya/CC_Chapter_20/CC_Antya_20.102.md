# CC Antya 20.102

**Bengali:**

ebe antya-līlā-gaṇera kari anuvāda
‘anuvāda’ kaile pāi līlāra ‘āsvāda’

**Transliteration:**

ebe — now; antya-līlā-gaṇera kari anuvāda — I beg to repeat all the facts of this Antya-līlā; pāi — I get; līlāra — of the pastime; āsvāda — taste.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ebe — now; antya-līlā-gaṇera kari anuvāda — I beg to repeat all the facts of this Antya-līlā; pāi — I get; līlāra — of the pastime; āsvāda — taste.

**Translation:**

Translation
Now let me repeat all the pastimes of the Antya-līlā, for if I do so I shall taste the pastimes again.
