# CC Antya 14.108

**Bengali:**

veṇu-nāda śuni’ āilā rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī
saba sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge kariyā sājani

**Transliteration:**

veṇu-nāda — the vibration of the flute; śuni’ — hearing; āilā — came; rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī — Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; saba — all; sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge — accompanied by gopīs.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
veṇu-nāda — the vibration of the flute; śuni’ — hearing; āilā — came; rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī — Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; saba — all; sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge — accompanied by gopīs.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Hearing the vibration of Kṛṣṇa’s flute, Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī and all Her gopī friends came there to meet Him. They were all very nicely dressed.
