# CC Antya 14.12

**Bengali:**

kṛṣṇa mathurāya gele, gopīra ye daśā haila
kṛṣṇa-vicchede prabhura se daśā upajila

**Transliteration:**

kṛṣṇa mathurāya gele — when Lord Kṛṣṇa departed for Mathurā; gopīra — of the gopīs; haila — was; kṛṣṇa-vicchede — by separation from Kṛṣṇa; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; se daśā — that situation; upajila — happened.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kṛṣṇa mathurāya gele — when Lord Kṛṣṇa departed for Mathurā; gopīra — of the gopīs; haila — was; kṛṣṇa-vicchede — by separation from Kṛṣṇa; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; se daśā — that situation; upajila — happened.

**Translation:**

Translation
When Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu felt separation from Kṛṣṇa, His condition exactly corresponded to that of the gopīs in Vṛndāvana after Kṛṣṇa’s departure for Mathurā.
