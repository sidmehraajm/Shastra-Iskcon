# CC Antya 17.70

**Bengali:**

ei ta’ kahiluṅ ‘kūrmākṛti’-anubhāva
unmāda-ceṣṭita tāte unmāda-pralāpa

**Transliteration:**

ei ta’ kahiluṅ — thus I have described; kūrma-ākṛti — of becoming like a tortoise; anubhāva — the ecstatic symptom; unmāda-ceṣṭita — enacted in madness; tāte — in that; unmāda-pralāpa — talking like a madman.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei ta’ kahiluṅ — thus I have described; kūrma-ākṛti — of becoming like a tortoise; anubhāva — the ecstatic symptom; unmāda-ceṣṭita — enacted in madness; tāte — in that; unmāda-pralāpa — talking like a madman.

**Translation:**

Translation
Thus I have described Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s ecstatic transformation of becoming like a tortoise. In that ecstasy, He talked and acted like a madman.
