# CC Antya 17.13

**Bengali:**

ethā govinda mahāprabhura śabda nā pāñā
svarūpere bolāila kapāṭa khuliyā

**Transliteration:**

ethā — here; govinda — Govinda; mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; śabda — sound; nā pāñā — not getting; svarūpere — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; bolāila — called for; kapāṭa — the doors; khuliyā — opening.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ethā — here; govinda — Govinda; mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; śabda — sound; nā pāñā — not getting; svarūpere — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; bolāila — called for; kapāṭa — the doors; khuliyā — opening.

**Translation:**

Translation
Meanwhile, not hearing any sounds from Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Govinda immediately sent for Svarūpa Dāmodara and opened the doors.
