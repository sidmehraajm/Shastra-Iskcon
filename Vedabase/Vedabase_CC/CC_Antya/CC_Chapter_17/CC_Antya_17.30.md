# CC Antya 17.30

**Bengali:**

svarūpa-gosāñi prabhura bhāva jāniyā
bhāgavatera śloka paḍe madhura kariyā

**Transliteration:**

svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosāñi; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhāva — the emotion; jāniyā — understanding; bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; paḍe — recites; madhura kariyā — in a sweet voice.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosāñi; prabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhāva — the emotion; jāniyā — understanding; bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; paḍe — recites; madhura kariyā — in a sweet voice.

**Translation:**

Translation
Understanding the ecstatic emotions of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, Svarūpa Dāmodara, in a sweet voice, recited the following verse from Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.
