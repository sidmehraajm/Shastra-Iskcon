# CC Antya 3.112

**Bengali:**

“ṭhākura, tumi — parama-sundara, prathama yauvana
tomā dekhi’ kon nārī dharite pāre mana?

**Transliteration:**

ṭhākura — O great devotee ācārya; parama-sundara — very beautifully constructed; prathama yauvana — the beginning of youth; tomā dekhi’ — seeing you; kon nārī — what woman; dharite pāre — can control; mana — her mind.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ṭhākura — O great devotee ācārya; parama-sundara — very beautifully constructed; prathama yauvana — the beginning of youth; tomā dekhi’ — seeing you; kon nārī — what woman; dharite pāre — can control; mana — her mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
“My dear Ṭhākura, O great preacher, great devotee, you are so beautifully built, and your youth is just beginning. Who is the woman who could control her mind after seeing you?
