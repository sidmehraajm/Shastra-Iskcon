# CC Antya 3.252-253

**Bengali:**

mahā-bhāgavata tumi, — tomāra darśane
tomāra kṛṣṇa-nāma-kīrtana-śravaṇe
citta śuddha haila, cāhe kṛṣṇa-nāma laite
kṛṣṇa-nāma upadeśi’ kṛpā kara mote

**Transliteration:**

mahā-bhāgavata — the foremost devotee; tumi — you; tomāra darśane — by seeing you; tomāra — your; kṛṣṇa-nāma — of the holy name of Kṛṣṇa; kīrtana — chanting; śravaṇe — by hearing; citta — consciousness; śuddha haila — became purified; cāhe — wants; kṛṣṇa-nāma laite — to chant the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa; kṛṣṇa-nāma upadeśi’ — instructing about chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; mote — unto me.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-bhāgavata — the foremost devotee; tumi — you; tomāra darśane — by seeing you; tomāra — your; kṛṣṇa-nāma — of the holy name of Kṛṣṇa; kīrtana — chanting; śravaṇe — by hearing; citta — consciousness; śuddha haila — became purified; cāhe — wants; kṛṣṇa-nāma laite — to chant the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa; kṛṣṇa-nāma upadeśi’ — instructing about chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; mote — unto me.

**Translation:**

Translation
“My dear sir, you are the foremost devotee. Simply seeing you and hearing you chant the holy name of Kṛṣṇa has purified my consciousness. Now I want to chant the holy name of the Lord. Please be kind to me by instructing me about the ecstasy of chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra.
