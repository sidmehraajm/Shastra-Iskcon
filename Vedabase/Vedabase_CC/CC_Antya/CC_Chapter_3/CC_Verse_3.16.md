# CC Antya 3.16

**Bengali:**

yadyapi brāhmaṇī sei tapasvinī satī
tathāpi tāhāra doṣa — sundarī yuvatī

**Transliteration:**

yadyapi — although; brāhmaṇī — wife of a brāhmaṇa; tapasvinī — austere; satī — chaste; tathāpi — still; tāhāra — her; doṣa — fault; sundarī — very beautiful; yuvatī — young girl.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yadyapi — although; brāhmaṇī — wife of a brāhmaṇa; tapasvinī — austere; satī — chaste; tathāpi — still; tāhāra — her; doṣa — fault; sundarī — very beautiful; yuvatī — young girl.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Although the boy’s mother is completely austere and chaste, she has one natural fault — she is a very beautiful young girl.
