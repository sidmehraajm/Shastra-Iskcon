# CC Antya 3.76

**Bengali:**

ucca saṅkīrtana tāte karilā pracāra
sthira-cara jīvera saba khaṇḍāilā saṁsāra”

**Transliteration:**

ucca saṅkīrtana — loud chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; karilā pracāra — You have spread; sthira-cara — nonmoving and moving; jīvera — of the living entities; saba — all; khaṇḍāilā — You finished; saṁsāra — the bondage to material existence.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ucca saṅkīrtana — loud chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; karilā pracāra — You have spread; sthira-cara — nonmoving and moving; jīvera — of the living entities; saba — all; khaṇḍāilā — You finished; saṁsāra — the bondage to material existence.

**Translation:**

Translation
“You have preached the loud chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra and in this way freed all moving and nonmoving living entities from material bondage.”
