# CC Antya 3.177

**Bengali:**

keha bale, — ‘nāma haite haya pāpa-kṣaya’
keha bale, — ‘nāma haite jīvera mokṣa haya’

**Transliteration:**

keha bale — some of them said; nāma haite — by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; pāpa-kṣaya — disappearance of all reactions to sinful activities; keha bale — some of them said; nāma haite — by chanting the holy name; jīvera — of the living entities; mokṣa haya — there is liberation.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
keha bale — some of them said; nāma haite — by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; pāpa-kṣaya — disappearance of all reactions to sinful activities; keha bale — some of them said; nāma haite — by chanting the holy name; jīvera — of the living entities; mokṣa haya — there is liberation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Some of them said, “By chanting the holy name of the Lord, one is freed from the reactions of all sinful life.”

**Purport:**

Purport
Others said, “Simply by chanting the holy name of the Lord, a living being is liberated from material bondage.”
