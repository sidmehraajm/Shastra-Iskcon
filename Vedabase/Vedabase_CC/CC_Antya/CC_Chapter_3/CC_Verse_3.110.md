# CC Antya 3.110

**Bengali:**

tulasī namaskari’ haridāsera dvāre yāñā
gosāñire namaskari’ rahilā dāṇḍāñā

**Transliteration:**

tulasī namaskari’ — after offering obeisances to the tulasī plant; haridāsera — of Ṭhākura Haridāsa; dvāre — at the door; yāñā — going; gosāñire — unto the ācārya; rahilā dāṇḍāñā — remained standing.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tulasī namaskari’ — after offering obeisances to the tulasī plant; haridāsera — of Ṭhākura Haridāsa; dvāre — at the door; yāñā — going; gosāñire — unto the ācārya; rahilā dāṇḍāñā — remained standing.

**Translation:**

Translation
After offering obeisances to the tulasī plant, she went to the door of Haridāsa Ṭhākura, offered him obeisances and stood there.
