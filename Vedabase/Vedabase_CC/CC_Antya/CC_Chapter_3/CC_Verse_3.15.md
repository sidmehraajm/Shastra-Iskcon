# CC Antya 3.15

**Bengali:**

paṇḍita hañā mane kene vicāra nā kara?
rāṇḍī brāhmaṇīra bālake prīti kene kara?

**Transliteration:**

paṇḍita hañā — being a learned teacher; mane — in the mind; kene — why; vicāra nā kara — do You not consider; rāṇḍī brāhmaṇīra — of a widowed wife of a brāhmaṇa; prīti — affection; kene kara — why do You show.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
paṇḍita hañā — being a learned teacher; mane — in the mind; kene — why; vicāra nā kara — do You not consider; rāṇḍī brāhmaṇīra — of a widowed wife of a brāhmaṇa; prīti — affection; kene kara — why do You show.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Dear Lord, You are a learned teacher. Why then don’t You consider that this boy is the son of a widowed brāhmaṇī? Why are You so affectionate to him?
