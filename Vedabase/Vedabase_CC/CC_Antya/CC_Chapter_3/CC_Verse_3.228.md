# CC Antya 3.228

**Bengali:**

tarka nā kariha, tarkāgocara tāṅra rīti
viśvāsa kariyā śuna kariyā pratīti

**Transliteration:**

tarka nā kariha — do not argue; tarka-agocara — beyond argument; tāṅra — his; rīti — behavior; viśvāsa kariyā — believing; śuna — listen; kariyā pratīti — having confidence.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tarka nā kariha — do not argue; tarka-agocara — beyond argument; tāṅra — his; rīti — behavior; viśvāsa kariyā — believing; śuna — listen; kariyā pratīti — having confidence.

**Translation:**

Translation
Hear about such incidents without putting forth dry arguments, for these incidents are beyond our material reasoning. One must believe in them with faith.
