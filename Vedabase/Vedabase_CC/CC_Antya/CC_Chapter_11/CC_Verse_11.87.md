# CC Antya 11.87

**Bengali:**

purī-bhāratīra saṅge prabhu bhikṣā kailā
sakala vaiṣṇava tabe bhojana karilā

**Transliteration:**

purī-bhāratīra saṅge — with Paramānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhikṣā kailā — honored the prasādam; tabe — then; bhojana karilā — began to eat.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
purī-bhāratīra saṅge — with Paramānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhikṣā kailā — honored the prasādam; tabe — then; bhojana karilā — began to eat.

**Translation:**

Translation
With Paramānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sat down and accepted the prasādam. When He began to eat, so did all the Vaiṣṇavas.
