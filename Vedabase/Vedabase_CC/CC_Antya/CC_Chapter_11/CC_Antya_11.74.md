# CC Antya 11.74

**Bengali:**

‘haridāsa-ṭhākurera mahotsavera tare
prasāda māgiye bhikṣā deha’ ta’ āmāre’

**Transliteration:**

haridāsa-ṭhākurera — of Haridāsa Ṭhākura; mahotsavera tare — for holding a festival; prasāda māgiye — I am begging prasādam; ta’ — certainly; āmāre — unto Me.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
haridāsa-ṭhākurera — of Haridāsa Ṭhākura; mahotsavera tare — for holding a festival; prasāda māgiye — I am begging prasādam; ta’ — certainly; āmāre — unto Me.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I am begging prasādam for a festival honoring the passing away of Haridāsa Ṭhākura,” the Lord said. “Please give Me alms.”
