# CC Antya 11.78

**Bengali:**

svarūpa-gosāñi kahilena saba pasārire
eka eka dravyera eka eka puñjā deha’ more

**Transliteration:**

svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; kahilena — said; saba pasārire — to all the shopkeepers; eka eka dravyera — of each particular type of prasādam; deha’ more — deliver to me.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
svarūpa-gosāñi — Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī; kahilena — said; saba pasārire — to all the shopkeepers; eka eka dravyera — of each particular type of prasādam; deha’ more — deliver to me.

**Translation:**

Translation
Svarūpa Dāmodara said to all the shopkeepers, “Deliver to me four palmfuls of prasādam from each and every item.”
