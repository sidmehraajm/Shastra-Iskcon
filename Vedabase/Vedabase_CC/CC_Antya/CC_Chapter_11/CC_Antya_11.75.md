# CC Antya 11.75

**Bengali:**

śuniyā pasāri saba cāṅgaḍā uṭhāñā
prasāda dite āse tārā ānandita hañā

**Transliteration:**

śuniyā — hearing; pasāri — the shopkeepers; saba — all; cāṅgaḍā uṭhāñā — taking big baskets; prasāda dite — to deliver the prasādam; tārā — they; ānandita hañā — in great jubilation.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuniyā — hearing; pasāri — the shopkeepers; saba — all; cāṅgaḍā uṭhāñā — taking big baskets; prasāda dite — to deliver the prasādam; tārā — they; ānandita hañā — in great jubilation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Hearing this, all the shopkeepers immediately came forward with big baskets of prasādam, which they jubilantly delivered to Lord Caitanya.
