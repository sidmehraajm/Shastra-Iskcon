# CC Antya 2.141

**Bengali:**

eta bali tāre snāna bhojana karāñā
āpana bhavana āilā tāre āśvāsiyā

**Transliteration:**

eta bali — saying this; tāre — him; snāna bhojana karāñā — inducing to bathe and take prasādam; āilā — returned; tāre āśvāsiyā — assuring him.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bali — saying this; tāre — him; snāna bhojana karāñā — inducing to bathe and take prasādam; āilā — returned; tāre āśvāsiyā — assuring him.

**Translation:**

Translation
Having said this, Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī induced Haridāsa to bathe and take prasādam. After thus reassuring him, he returned home.
