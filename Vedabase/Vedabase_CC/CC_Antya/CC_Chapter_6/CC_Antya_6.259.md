# CC Antya 6.259

**Bengali:**

cāri-śata mudrā, dui bhṛtya, eka brāhmaṇa
śivānandera ṭhāñi pāṭhāila tata-kṣaṇa

**Transliteration:**

cāri-śata mudrā — four hundred coins; dui bhṛtya — two servants; eka brāhmaṇa — one brāhmaṇa; pāṭhāila — sent; tata-kṣaṇa — immediately.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
cāri-śata mudrā — four hundred coins; dui bhṛtya — two servants; eka brāhmaṇa — one brāhmaṇa; pāṭhāila — sent; tata-kṣaṇa — immediately.

**Translation:**

Translation
Raghunātha dāsa’s father immediately sent four hundred coins, two servants and one brāhmaṇa to Śivānanda Sena.
