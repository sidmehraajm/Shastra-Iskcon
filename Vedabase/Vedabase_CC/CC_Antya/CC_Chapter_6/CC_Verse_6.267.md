# CC Antya 6.267

**Bengali:**

sei vipra bhṛtya, cāri-śata mudrā lañā
nīlācale raghunāthe mililā āsiyā

**Transliteration:**

sei vipra — that brāhmaṇa; cāri-śata mudrā — four hundred coins; lañā — bringing; nīlācale — at Jagannātha Purī; raghunāthe — with Raghunātha dāsa; mililā — met; āsiyā — coming.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei vipra — that brāhmaṇa; cāri-śata mudrā — four hundred coins; lañā — bringing; nīlācale — at Jagannātha Purī; raghunāthe — with Raghunātha dāsa; mililā — met; āsiyā — coming.

**Translation:**

Translation
The servants and brāhmaṇa brought four hundred coins to Jagannātha Purī, and there they met Raghunātha dāsa.
