# CC Antya 6.68

**Bengali:**

kona kona vipra upare sthāna nā pāñā
dui holnāya ciḍā bhijāya gaṅgā-tīre giyā

**Transliteration:**

kona kona — some; vipra — brāhmaṇas; sthāna nā pāñā — not having gotten a place; dui holnāya — in two earthen pots; ciḍā bhijāya — soak chipped rice; gaṅgā-tīre — on the bank of the Ganges; giyā — going.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kona kona — some; vipra — brāhmaṇas; sthāna nā pāñā — not having gotten a place; dui holnāya — in two earthen pots; ciḍā bhijāya — soak chipped rice; gaṅgā-tīre — on the bank of the Ganges; giyā — going.

**Translation:**

Translation
Some of the brāhmaṇas, not having gotten a place on the platform, went to the bank of the Ganges with their two earthen pots and soaked their chipped rice there.
