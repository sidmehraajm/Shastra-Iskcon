# CC Antya 1.216

**Bengali:**

dola anantare prabhu rūpe vidāya dilā
aneka prasāda kari’ śakti sañcārilā

**Transliteration:**

dola anantare — after the Dola-yātrā; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; rūpe — unto Rūpa Gosvāmī; vidāya dilā — bade farewell; aneka prasāda kari’ — endowing with all kinds of mercy; śakti sañcārilā — empowered him.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dola anantare — after the Dola-yātrā; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; rūpe — unto Rūpa Gosvāmī; vidāya dilā — bade farewell; aneka prasāda kari’ — endowing with all kinds of mercy; śakti sañcārilā — empowered him.

**Translation:**

Translation
After the Dola-yātrā festival ended, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu bade farewell to Rūpa Gosvāmī also. The Lord empowered him and bestowed upon him all kinds of mercy.
