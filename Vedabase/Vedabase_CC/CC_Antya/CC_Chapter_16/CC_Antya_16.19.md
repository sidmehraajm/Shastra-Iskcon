# CC Antya 16.19

**Bengali:**

ājñā deha’, — brāhmaṇa-ghare anna lañā diye
tāhāṅ tumi prasāda pāo, tabe āmi jīye”

**Transliteration:**

ājñā deha’ — permit me; brāhmaṇa-ghare — to the house of a brāhmaṇa; lañā diye — I shall offer; tāhāṅ — there; tumi — you; prasāda pāo — take prasādam; āmi — I; jīye — shall live.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ājñā deha’ — permit me; brāhmaṇa-ghare — to the house of a brāhmaṇa; lañā diye — I shall offer; tāhāṅ — there; tumi — you; prasāda pāo — take prasādam; āmi — I; jīye — shall live.

**Translation:**

Translation
“If you will permit me, I shall send some food to a brāhmaṇa’s house, and there you may take prasādam. If you do so, I shall then live very comfortably.”
