# CC Antya 12.87

**Bengali:**

āira caraṇa yāi’ karilā vandana
jagannāthera vastra-prasāda kailā nivedana

**Transliteration:**

āira — of Śacīmātā; caraṇa — to the lotus feet; yāi’ — going; karilā vandana — offered prayers; jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; vastra-prasāda — cloth and prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āira — of Śacīmātā; caraṇa — to the lotus feet; yāi’ — going; karilā vandana — offered prayers; jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; vastra-prasāda — cloth and prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
When he arrived, he offered prayers at her lotus feet and then offered her the cloth and prasādam of Lord Jagannātha.
