# CC Antya 12.116

**Bengali:**

prabhu kahe, — “paṇḍita, taila ānilā gauḍa ha-ite
āmi ta’ sannyāsī, — taila nā pāri la-ite

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; paṇḍita — My dear Paṇḍita; taila — oil; ānilā — you have brought; gauḍa ha-ite — from Bengal; āmi — I; ta’ — but; sannyāsī — a sannyāsī; nā pāri la-ite — I cannot accept.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; paṇḍita — My dear Paṇḍita; taila — oil; ānilā — you have brought; gauḍa ha-ite — from Bengal; āmi — I; ta’ — but; sannyāsī — a sannyāsī; nā pāri la-ite — I cannot accept.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said to Jagadānanda Paṇḍita, “My dear Paṇḍita, you have brought Me some oil from Bengal, but since I am in the renounced order, I cannot accept it.
