# CC Antya 9.40

**Bengali:**

pāṅca-gaṇḍāra pātra haya sannyāsī brāhmaṇa
māgile vā kene dibe dui-lakṣa kāhana?”

**Transliteration:**

pāṅca-gaṇḍāra — of five gaṇḍās; haya — are; sannyāsī brāhmaṇa — sannyāsīs and brāhmaṇas; vā — or; kene — why; dibe — shall give; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas of kauḍis..

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pāṅca-gaṇḍāra — of five gaṇḍās; haya — are; sannyāsī brāhmaṇa — sannyāsīs and brāhmaṇas; vā — or; kene — why; dibe — shall give; dui-lakṣa kāhana — 200,000 kāhanas of kauḍis..

**Translation:**

Translation
“Of course, a sannyāsī or brāhmaṇa may beg for up to five gaṇḍās, but why should he be granted the inappropriate sum of 200,000 kāhanas of conchshells?”
