# CC Antya 9.149

**Bengali:**

gopīnāthera nindā, āra āpana-nirveda
ei-mātra kahila — ihāra nā bujhibe bheda

**Transliteration:**

gopīnāthera nindā — the chastisement of Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; āra — and; āpana-nirveda — His indifference; ei — this; mātra — simply; kahila — I have described; ihāra — of this; nā bujhibe bheda — one cannot understand the depth of meaning.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopīnāthera nindā — the chastisement of Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; āra — and; āpana-nirveda — His indifference; ei — this; mātra — simply; kahila — I have described; ihāra — of this; nā bujhibe bheda — one cannot understand the depth of meaning.

**Translation:**

Translation
I have simply described the chastisement of Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka and Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s indifference. But the deep meaning of this behavior is very difficult to understand.
