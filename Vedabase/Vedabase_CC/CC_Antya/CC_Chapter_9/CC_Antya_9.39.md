# CC Antya 9.39

**Bengali:**

tomā-sabāra ei mata, — rāja-ṭhāñi yāñā
kauḍi māgi’ laṅ muñi āṅcala pātiyā

**Transliteration:**

tomā-sabāra — of all of you; ei mata — this is the opinion; rāja-ṭhāñi yāñā — going to the King; kauḍi māgi’ — begging kauḍis; muñi — I; āṅcala pātiyā — spreading My cloth.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tomā-sabāra — of all of you; ei mata — this is the opinion; rāja-ṭhāñi yāñā — going to the King; kauḍi māgi’ — begging kauḍis; muñi — I; āṅcala pātiyā — spreading My cloth.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Your opinion is that I should go to the King’s palace and spread My cloth to beg money from him.
