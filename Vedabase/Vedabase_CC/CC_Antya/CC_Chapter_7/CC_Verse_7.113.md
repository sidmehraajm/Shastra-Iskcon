# CC Antya 7.113

**Bengali:**

“bhāgavate svāmīra vyākhyāna kairāchi khaṇḍana
la-ite nā pāri tāṅra vyākhyāna-vacana

**Transliteration:**

bhāgavate — in my commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; vyākhyāna — explanation; kairāchi khaṇḍana — I have refuted; la-ite nā pāri — I cannot accept; tāṅra — his; vyākhyāna-vacana — words of explanation.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhāgavate — in my commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; vyākhyāna — explanation; kairāchi khaṇḍana — I have refuted; la-ite nā pāri — I cannot accept; tāṅra — his; vyākhyāna-vacana — words of explanation.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In my commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam,” he said, “I have refuted the explanations of Śrīdhara Svāmī. I cannot accept his explanations.
