# CC Antya 7.70

**Bengali:**

prasāda pāya vaiṣṇava-gaṇa bale, ‘hari’ ‘hari’
hari hari dhvani uṭhe saba brahmāṇḍa bhari’

**Transliteration:**

prasāda — the prasādam; vaiṣṇava-gaṇa — all the Vaiṣṇavas; bale — chant; hari hari — “Hari, Hari”; hari hari dhvani — the vibration of Hari, Hari; uṭhe — rises; saba brahmāṇḍa — the entire universe; bhari’ — filling.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prasāda — the prasādam; vaiṣṇava-gaṇa — all the Vaiṣṇavas; bale — chant; hari hari — “Hari, Hari”; hari hari dhvani — the vibration of Hari, Hari; uṭhe — rises; saba brahmāṇḍa — the entire universe; bhari’ — filling.

**Translation:**

Translation
Accepting the prasādam, all the Vaiṣṇavas chanted the holy names “Hari! Hari!” The rising vibration of the holy name of Hari filled the entire universe.
