# CC Antya 7.133

**Bengali:**

śrīdhara-svāmi-prasāde ‘bhāgavata’ jāni
jagad-guru śrīdhara-svāmī ‘guru’ kari’ māni

**Transliteration:**

śrīdhara-svāmi — of Śrīdhara Svāmī; prasāde — by the mercy; bhāgavata jāni — we can understand Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; śrīdhara-svāmī — Śrīdhara Svāmī; guru kari’ — as a spiritual master; māni — I accept.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śrīdhara-svāmi — of Śrīdhara Svāmī; prasāde — by the mercy; bhāgavata jāni — we can understand Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; śrīdhara-svāmī — Śrīdhara Svāmī; guru kari’ — as a spiritual master; māni — I accept.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Śrīdhara Svāmī is the spiritual master of the entire world because by his mercy we can understand Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. I therefore accept him as a spiritual master.
