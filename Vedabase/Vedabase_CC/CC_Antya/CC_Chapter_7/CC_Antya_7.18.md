# CC Antya 7.18

**Bengali:**

sarva-śāstre kṛṣṇa-bhaktye nāhi yāṅra sama
ataeva ‘advaita-ācārya’ tāṅra nāma

**Transliteration:**

sarva-śāstre — in all revealed scriptures; kṛṣṇa-bhaktye — in the devotional service of Lord Kṛṣṇa; nāhi — is not; yāṅra — of whom; sama — equal; ataeva — therefore; advaita — without a competitor; ācārya — ācārya.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sarva-śāstre — in all revealed scriptures; kṛṣṇa-bhaktye — in the devotional service of Lord Kṛṣṇa; nāhi — is not; yāṅra — of whom; sama — equal; ataeva — therefore; advaita — without a competitor; ācārya — ācārya.

**Translation:**

Translation
“He is unparalleled in His understanding of all the revealed scriptures and the devotional service of Lord Kṛṣṇa. Therefore He is called Advaita Ācārya.
