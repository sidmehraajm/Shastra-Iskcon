# CC Antya 7.166

**Bengali:**

paṇḍitera saujanya, brahmaṇyatā-guṇa
dṛḍha prema-mudrā loke karilā khyāpana

**Transliteration:**

paṇḍitera saujanya — the gentle behavior of Gadādhara Paṇḍita; brahmaṇyatā-guṇa — the attributes of a perfect brāhmaṇa; prema-mudrā — characteristic of love; loke — people; karilā khyāpana — proclaimed.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
paṇḍitera saujanya — the gentle behavior of Gadādhara Paṇḍita; brahmaṇyatā-guṇa — the attributes of a perfect brāhmaṇa; prema-mudrā — characteristic of love; loke — people; karilā khyāpana — proclaimed.

**Translation:**

Translation
Gadādhara Paṇḍita is celebrated all over the world for his gentle behavior, his brahminical attributes and his steady love for Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
