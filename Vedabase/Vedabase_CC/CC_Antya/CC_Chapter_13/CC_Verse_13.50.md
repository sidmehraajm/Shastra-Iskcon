# CC Antya 13.50

**Bengali:**

‘mukunda sarasvatī’ nāma sannyāsī mahājane
eka bahirvāsa teṅho dila sanātane

**Transliteration:**

mukunda sarasvatī — Mukunda Sarasvatī; nāma — named; sannyāsī — a sannyāsī; eka — one; bahirvāsa — outward covering; teṅho — he; dila — gave; sanātane — to Sanātana Gosvāmī.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mukunda sarasvatī — Mukunda Sarasvatī; nāma — named; sannyāsī — a sannyāsī; eka — one; bahirvāsa — outward covering; teṅho — he; dila — gave; sanātane — to Sanātana Gosvāmī.

**Translation:**

Translation
Previously, a great sannyāsī named Mukunda Sarasvatī had given Sanātana Gosvāmī an outer garment.
