# CC Antya 13.49

**Bengali:**

eka-dina sanātane paṇḍita nimantrilā
nitya-kṛtya kari’ teṅha pāka caḍāilā

**Transliteration:**

eka-dina — one day; sanātane — Sanātana Gosvāmī; paṇḍita</em> <em>nimantrilā — Jagadānanda Paṇḍita invited; nitya-kṛtya kari’ — after finishing his routine duties; teṅha — he; pāka caḍāilā — began to cook.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms

**Translation:**

Translation
One day Jagadānanda Paṇḍita, having invited Sanātana to the nearby temple for lunch, finished his routine duties and began to cook.
