# CC Antya 10.81

**Bengali:**

saba lañā prabhu kailā prasāda bhojana
sabāre vidāya dilā karite śayana

**Transliteration:**

saba lañā — with all of them; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; kailā — performed; prasāda bhojana — taking prasādam; vidāya dilā — bade farewell; karite śayana — to take rest.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saba lañā — with all of them; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; kailā — performed; prasāda bhojana — taking prasādam; vidāya dilā — bade farewell; karite śayana — to take rest.

**Translation:**

Translation
Then Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu took prasādam with all of them and then asked them to return to their dwellings and take rest.
