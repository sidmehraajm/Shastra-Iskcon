# CC Antya 18.109

**Bengali:**

hena-kāle more dhari, mahā-kolāhala kari’,
tumi-saba ihāṅ lañā āilā
kāṅhā yamunā, vṛndāvana, kāṅhā kṛṣṇa, gopī-gaṇa,
sei sukha bhaṅga karāilā!”

**Transliteration:**

hena-kāle — at that time; more dhari’ — picking Me up; mahā-kolāhala kari’ — and making a great tumult; tumi-saba — all of you; ihāṅ — here; lañā āilā — brought; kāṅhā — where; yamunā — the Yamunā River; vṛndāvana — Vṛndāvana; kāṅhā — where; kṛṣṇa — Kṛṣṇa; gopī-gaṇa — the gopīs; bhaṅga karāilā — you have broken.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms

**Translation:**

Translation
“Suddenly, all of you created a great tumult and picked Me up and brought Me back here. Where now is the river Yamunā? Where is Vṛndāvana? Where are Kṛṣṇa and the gopīs? You have broken My happy dream!”
