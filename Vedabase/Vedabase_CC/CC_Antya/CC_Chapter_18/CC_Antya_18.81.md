# CC Antya 18.81

**Bengali:**

rādhikādi gopī-gaṇa-saṅge ekatra meli’
yamunāra jale mahā-raṅge karena keli

**Transliteration:**

rādhikā-ādi — headed by Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; gopī-gaṇa-saṅge — with the gopīs; yamunāra — of the river Yamunā; jale — in the water; mahā-raṅge — in a great sporting attitude; karena keli — performs pastimes.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rādhikā-ādi — headed by Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; gopī-gaṇa-saṅge — with the gopīs; yamunāra — of the river Yamunā; jale — in the water; mahā-raṅge — in a great sporting attitude; karena keli — performs pastimes.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Lord Kṛṣṇa was in the water of the Yamunā in the company of the gopīs, headed by Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī. They were performing pastimes in a great sporting manner.
