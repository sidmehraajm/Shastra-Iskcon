# CC Antya 8.64

**Bengali:**

“sannyāsīra dharma nahe ‘indriya-tarpaṇa’
yaiche taiche kare mātra udara bharaṇa

**Transliteration:**

sannyāsīra — of a sannyāsī; nahe — is not; indriya-tarpaṇa — to gratify the senses; yaiche taiche — some way or other; kare — does; mātra — only; udara bharaṇa — filling the belly.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sannyāsīra — of a sannyāsī; nahe — is not; indriya-tarpaṇa — to gratify the senses; yaiche taiche — some way or other; kare — does; mātra — only; udara bharaṇa — filling the belly.

**Translation:**

Translation
Rāmacandra Purī advised, “It is not the business of a sannyāsī to gratify his senses. He should fill his belly some way or other.
