# ŚB 12.7.5

**Sanskrit:**

त्रय्यारुणि: कश्यपश्च सावर्णिरकृतव्रण: ।
वैशम्पायनहारीतौ षड् वै पौराणिका इमे ॥ ५ ॥

**Transliteration:**

trayyāruṇiḥ kaśyapaś ca
sāvarṇir akṛtavraṇaḥ
vaiśampāyana-hārītau
ṣaḍ vai paurāṇikā ime

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
trayyāruṇiḥ kaśyapaḥ ca — Trayyāruṇi and Kaśyapa; sāvarṇiḥ akṛta-vraṇaḥ — Sāvarṇi and Akṛtavraṇa; vaiśampāyana-hārītau — Vaiśampāyana and Hārīta; ṣaṭ — six; vai — indeed; paurāṇikāḥ — spiritual masters of the Purāṇas.

**Translation:**

Translation
Trayyāruṇi, Kaśyapa, Sāvarṇi, Akṛtavraṇa, Vaiśampāyana and Hārīta are the six masters of the Purāṇas.
