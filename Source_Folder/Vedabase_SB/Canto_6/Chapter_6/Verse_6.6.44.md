# ŚB 6.6.44

**Sanskrit:**

त्वष्टुर्दैत्यात्मजा भार्या रचना नाम कन्यका ।
सन्निवेशस्तयोर्जज्ञे विश्वरूपश्च वीर्यवान् ॥ ४४ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tvaṣṭur daityātmajā bhāryā
racanā nāma kanyakā
sanniveśas tayor jajñe
viśvarūpaś ca vīryavān

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tvaṣṭuḥ — of Tvaṣṭā; daitya-ātma-jā — the daughter of a demon; bhāryā — wife; racanā — Racanā; nāma — named; kanyakā — a maiden; sanniveśaḥ — Sanniveśa; tayoḥ — of those two; jajñe — was born; viśvarūpaḥ — Viśvarūpa; ca — and; vīryavān — very powerful in bodily strength.

**Translation:**

Translation
Racanā, the daughter of the Daityas, became the wife of Prajāpati Tvaṣṭā. By his semen he begot in her womb two very powerful sons named Sanniveśa and Viśvarūpa.
