# ŚB 12.6.73

**Sanskrit:**

सूत उवाच
एवं स्तुत: स भगवान् वाजिरूपधरो रवि: ।
यजूंष्ययातयामानि मुनयेऽदात् प्रसादित: ॥ ७३ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sūta uvāca
evaṁ stutaḥ sa bhagavān
vāji-rūpa-dharo raviḥ
yajūṁṣy ayāta-yāmāni
munaye ’dāt prasāditaḥ

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sūtaḥ uvāca — Sūta Gosvāmī said; evam — in this way; stutaḥ — offered glorification; saḥ — he; bhagavān — the powerful demigod; vāji-rūpa — the form of a horse; dharaḥ — assuming; raviḥ — the sun-god; yajūṁṣi — yajur- mantras; munaye — to the sage; adāt — presented; prasāditaḥ — being satisfied.

**Translation:**

Translation
Sūta Gosvāmī said: Satisfied by such glorification, the powerful sun-god assumed the form of a horse and presented to the sage Yājñavalkya yajur-mantras previously unknown in human society.
