# ŚB 12.6.4

**Sanskrit:**

पुराणसंहितामेतामश्रौष्म भवतो वयम् ।
यस्यां खलूत्तम:श्लोको भगवाननुवर्ण्यते ॥ ४ ॥

**Transliteration:**

purāṇa-saṁhitām etām
aśrauṣma bhavato vayam
yasyāṁ khalūttamaḥ-śloko
bhagavān anuvarṇyate

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
purāṇa-saṁhitām — essential summary of all the Purāṇas; aśrauṣma — have heard; bhavataḥ — from you; vayam — we; yasyām — in which; khalu — indeed; uttamaḥ-ślokaḥ — who is always described in choice poetry; bhagavān — the Personality of Godhead; anuvarṇyate — is fittingly described.

**Translation:**

Translation
I have heard from you this Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, which is the perfect summary of all the Purāṇas and which perfectly describes the Supreme Lord, Uttamaḥśloka.
