# ŚB 12.12.19

**Sanskrit:**

मन्वन्तरानुकथनं गजेन्द्रस्य विमोक्षणम् ।
मन्वन्तरावताराश्च विष्णोर्हयशिरादय: ॥ १९ ॥

**Transliteration:**

manv-antarānukathanaṁ
gajendrasya vimokṣaṇam
manvantarāvatārāś ca
viṣṇor hayaśirādayaḥ

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
manu-antara — of reigns of the various Manus; anukathanam — the detailed description; gaja-indrasya — of the king of the elephants; vimokṣaṇam — the liberation; manuantara-avatārāḥ — the particular incarnations of the Supreme Personality of Godhead in each manvantara; viṣṇoḥ — of Lord Viṣṇu; hayaśirā-ādayaḥ — such as Lord Hayaśīrṣā.

**Translation:**

Translation
The reign of each Manu, the liberation of Gajendra, and the special incarnations of Lord Viṣṇu in each manvantara, such as Lord Hayaśīrṣā, are described as well.
