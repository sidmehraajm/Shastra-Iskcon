# CC Antya 17.32

**Bengali:**

śuni’ prabhu gopī-bhāve āviṣṭa ha-ilā
bhāgavatera ślokera artha karite lāgilā

**Transliteration:**

śuni’ — hearing; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; gopī-bhāve — in the emotion of the gopīs; bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; artha — the meaning; karite lāgilā — began to explain.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuni’ — hearing; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; gopī-bhāve — in the emotion of the gopīs; bhāgavatera — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; artha — the meaning; karite lāgilā — began to explain.

**Translation:**

Translation
Upon hearing this verse, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, overwhelmed with the ecstasy of the gopīs, began to explain it.
