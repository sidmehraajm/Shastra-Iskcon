# CC Antya 7.82

**Bengali:**

prabhu kahe, — “bhāgavatārtha bujhite nā pāri
bhāgavatārtha śunite āmi nahi adhikārī

**Transliteration:**

prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu replied; bhāgavata-artha — the meaning of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; bhāgavata-artha — the purport of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; āmi nahi adhikārī — I am not the proper person.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu kahe — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu replied; bhāgavata-artha — the meaning of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; bhāgavata-artha — the purport of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; āmi nahi adhikārī — I am not the proper person.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord replied, “I do not understand the meaning of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. Indeed, I am not a suitable person to hear its meaning.
