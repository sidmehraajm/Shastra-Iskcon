# CC Antya 4.147

**Bengali:**

dūra haite daṇḍa-paraṇāma kare sanātana
prabhu bolāya bāra bāra karite āliṅgana

**Transliteration:**

dūra haite — from a distant place; daṇḍa-paraṇāma — offering obeisances and daṇḍavats; sanātana — Sanātana Gosvāmī; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bolāya — calls; bāra bāra — again and again; karite āliṅgana — to embrace.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dūra haite — from a distant place; daṇḍa-paraṇāma — offering obeisances and daṇḍavats; sanātana — Sanātana Gosvāmī; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bolāya — calls; bāra bāra — again and again; karite āliṅgana — to embrace.

**Translation:**

Translation
Sanātana Gosvāmī offered his obeisances and daṇḍavats from a distant place, but Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu called him again and again to embrace him.
