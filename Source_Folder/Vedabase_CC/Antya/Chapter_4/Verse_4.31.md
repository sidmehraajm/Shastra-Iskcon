# CC Antya 4.31

**Bengali:**

rātri-dine raghunāthera ‘nāma’ āra ‘dhyāna’
rāmāyaṇa niravadhi śune, kare gāna

**Transliteration:**

rātri-dine — day and night; raghunāthera — of Lord Rāmacandra; nāma — holy name; āra — and; dhyāna — meditation; rāmāyaṇa — the epic about the activities of Lord Rāmacandra known as the Rāmāyaṇa; śune — hears; kare gāna — chants.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rātri-dine — day and night; raghunāthera — of Lord Rāmacandra; nāma — holy name; āra — and; dhyāna — meditation; rāmāyaṇa — the epic about the activities of Lord Rāmacandra known as the Rāmāyaṇa; śune — hears; kare gāna — chants.

**Translation:**

Translation
“He always chanted the holy name of Raghunātha and meditated upon Him. He continuously heard about the activities of the Lord from the Rāmāyaṇa and chanted about them.
