# CC Antya 4.137

**Bengali:**

“ihāṅ āilāṅa prabhure dekhi’ duḥkha khaṇḍāite
yebā mane, tāhā prabhu nā dilā karite

**Transliteration:**

ihāṅ — here (to Jagannātha Purī); āilāṅa — I have come; prabhure — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; dekhi’ — by seeing; duḥkha khaṇḍāite — to diminish my unhappiness; yebā mane — what was in my mind; tāhā — that; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nā dilā karite — did not allow me to do.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ihāṅ — here (to Jagannātha Purī); āilāṅa — I have come; prabhure — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; dekhi’ — by seeing; duḥkha khaṇḍāite — to diminish my unhappiness; yebā mane — what was in my mind; tāhā — that; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nā dilā karite — did not allow me to do.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I came here to diminish my unhappiness by seeing Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, but the Lord did not allow me to execute what was in my mind.
