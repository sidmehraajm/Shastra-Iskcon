# CC Antya 9.134

**Bengali:**

kāhāṅ cāṅgera upara sei maraṇa-pramāda!
kāhāṅ ‘neta-dhaṭī’ punaḥ, — e-saba prasāda!

**Transliteration:**

kāhāṅ — on one hand; cāṅgera upara — on the cāṅga; maraṇa-pramāda — danger of death; kāhāṅ — on the other hand; neta-dhaṭī — the silken cloth; punaḥ — again; e-saba — all this; prasāda — mercy.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kāhāṅ — on one hand; cāṅgera upara — on the cāṅga; maraṇa-pramāda — danger of death; kāhāṅ — on the other hand; neta-dhaṭī — the silken cloth; punaḥ — again; e-saba — all this; prasāda — mercy.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I was lifted upon the cāṅga to be killed, but instead I was honored with this silken cloth. This is all Your mercy.
