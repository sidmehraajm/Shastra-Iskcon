# CC Antya 9.135

**Bengali:**

cāṅgera upare tomāra caraṇa dhyāna kailuṅ
caraṇa-smaraṇa-prabhāve ei phala pāiluṅ

**Transliteration:**

cāṅgera upare — on the cāṅga; dhyāna kailuṅ — I meditated; caraṇa-smaraṇa-prabhāve — by the power of remembering Your lotus feet; ei phala — these results; pāiluṅ — I have got.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
cāṅgera upare — on the cāṅga; dhyāna kailuṅ — I meditated; caraṇa-smaraṇa-prabhāve — by the power of remembering Your lotus feet; ei phala — these results; pāiluṅ — I have got.

**Translation:**

Translation
“On the cāṅga I began meditating upon Your lotus feet, and the power of that remembrance has yielded all these results.
