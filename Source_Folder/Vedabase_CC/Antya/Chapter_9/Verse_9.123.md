# CC Antya 9.123

**Bengali:**

gopīnātha ei-mata ‘viṣaya’ kariyā
dui-cāri-lakṣa kāhana rahe ta’ khāñā

**Transliteration:**

gopīnātha — Gopīnātha; ei-mata — in this way; viṣaya kariyā — doing business; dui-cāri-lakṣa kāhana — two to four hundred thousand kāhanas.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopīnātha — Gopīnātha; ei-mata — in this way; viṣaya kariyā — doing business; dui-cāri-lakṣa kāhana — two to four hundred thousand kāhanas.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘Having been appointed collector, Gopīnātha, in the same way, also generally spends 200,000 to 400,000 kāhanas as he likes.
