# CC Antya 16.65

**Bengali:**

se vatsara śivānanda patnī lañā āilā
‘purī-dāsa’-choṭa-putre saṅgete ānilā

**Transliteration:**

se vatsara — that year; śivānanda — Śivānanda Sena; patnī — the wife; lañā — bringing; āilā — came; purī-dāsa — Purī dāsa; choṭa-putre — the youngest son; saṅgete ānilā — he brought with him.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
se vatsara — that year; śivānanda — Śivānanda Sena; patnī — the wife; lañā — bringing; āilā — came; purī-dāsa — Purī dāsa; choṭa-putre — the youngest son; saṅgete ānilā — he brought with him.

**Translation:**

Translation
That year, Śivānanda Sena brought with him his wife and youngest son, Purī dāsa.
