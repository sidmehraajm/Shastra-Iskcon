# CC Antya 16.86

**Bengali:**

ei līlā nija-granthe raghunātha-dāsa
‘gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣe’ kariyāchena prakāśa

**Transliteration:**

ei līlā — this pastime; nija-granthe — in his own book; raghunātha-dāsa — Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei līlā — this pastime; nija-granthe — in his own book; raghunātha-dāsa — Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Translation:**

Translation
In his book known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī has described this incident very nicely.
