# CC Antya 16.89

**Bengali:**

bhoga sarile jagannāthera sevaka-gaṇa
prasāda lañā prabhu-ṭhāñi kaila āgamana

**Transliteration:**

bhoga sarile — when the food was taken away; jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; sevaka-gaṇa — the servants; prasāda lañā — taking the prasādam; kaila āgamana — came.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhoga sarile — when the food was taken away; jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; sevaka-gaṇa — the servants; prasāda lañā — taking the prasādam; kaila āgamana — came.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the ārati finished, the prasādam was taken out, and the servants of Lord Jagannātha came to offer some to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
