# CC Antya 16.107

**Bengali:**

prasādera saurabhya-mādhurya kari’ āsvādana
alaukika āsvāde sabāra vismita haila mana

**Transliteration:**

prasādera — of the prasādam; kari’ āsvādana — tasting; alaukika — uncommon; āsvāde — by the taste; sabāra — of everyone; vismita — struck with wonder; haila — became; mana — the mind.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prasādera — of the prasādam; kari’ āsvādana — tasting; alaukika — uncommon; āsvāde — by the taste; sabāra — of everyone; vismita — struck with wonder; haila — became; mana — the mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
As they tasted the uncommon sweetness and fragrance of the prasādam, everyone’s mind was struck with wonder.
