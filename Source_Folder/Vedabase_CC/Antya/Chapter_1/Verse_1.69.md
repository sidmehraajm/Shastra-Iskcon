# CC Antya 1.69

**Bengali:**

“pṛthak nāṭaka karite satyabhāmā ājñā dila
jānilu, pṛthak nāṭaka karite prabhu-ājñā haila

**Transliteration:**

pṛthak nāṭaka — different dramas; karite — to write; satyabhāmā — Satyabhāmā; ājñā dila — ordered; jānilu — now I understand; pṛthak nāṭaka — different dramas; karite — to write; prabhu-ājñā — the order of the Lord; haila — there was.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
pṛthak nāṭaka — different dramas; karite — to write; satyabhāmā — Satyabhāmā; ājñā dila — ordered; jānilu — now I understand; pṛthak nāṭaka — different dramas; karite — to write; prabhu-ājñā — the order of the Lord; haila — there was.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Satyabhāmā ordered me to write two different dramas,” Śrīla Rūpa Gosvāmī thought. “Now I understand that this order has been confirmed by Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
