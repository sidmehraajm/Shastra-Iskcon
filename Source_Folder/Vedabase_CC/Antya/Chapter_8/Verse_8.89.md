# CC Antya 8.89

**Bengali:**

bhojyānna vipra yadi nimantraṇa kare
kichu ‘prasāda’ āne, kichu pāka kare ghare

**Transliteration:**

bhojya-anna vipra — a brāhmaṇa at whose place an invitation could be accepted; yadi — if; nimantraṇa kare — invites; kichu — some; prasāda — prasādam; kichu — some; pāka kare — cooks; ghare — at home.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhojya-anna vipra — a brāhmaṇa at whose place an invitation could be accepted; yadi — if; nimantraṇa kare — invites; kichu — some; prasāda — prasādam; kichu — some; pāka kare — cooks; ghare — at home.

**Translation:**

Translation
When a brāhmaṇa at whose home an invitation could be accepted invited Him, the brāhmaṇa would purchase part of the prasādam and cook the rest at home.
