# CC Antya 8.75

**Bengali:**

sannyāsīke eta khāoyāñā kara dharma nāśa!
ataeva jāninu, — tomāra kichu nāhi bhāsa’

**Transliteration:**

sannyāsīke — sannyāsīs; khāoyāñā — inducing to eat; kara dharma nāśa — you spoil their religious principles; ataeva — therefore; jāninu — I can understand; tomāra — your; kichu nāhi bhāsa — there is no advancement.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sannyāsīke — sannyāsīs; khāoyāñā — inducing to eat; kara dharma nāśa — you spoil their religious principles; ataeva — therefore; jāninu — I can understand; tomāra — your; kichu nāhi bhāsa — there is no advancement.

**Translation:**

Translation
“ ‘Also, by inducing sannyāsīs to eat so much, you spoil their religious principles. Therefore I can understand that you have no advancement.’
