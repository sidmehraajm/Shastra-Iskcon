# CC Antya 8.13

**Bengali:**

āgraha kariyā tāṅre vasi’ khāoyāila
āpane āgraha kari’ pariveśana kaila

**Transliteration:**

āgraha kariyā — with great eagerness; tāṅre vasi’ — seating him; khāoyāila — he fed; āpane — personally; āgraha kari’ — with great eagerness; pariveśana kaila — administered the prasādam..

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āgraha kariyā — with great eagerness; tāṅre vasi’ — seating him; khāoyāila — he fed; āpane — personally; āgraha kari’ — with great eagerness; pariveśana kaila — administered the prasādam..

**Translation:**

Translation
With great eagerness Rāmacandra Purī seated Jagadānanda Paṇḍita and personally served him prasādam.
