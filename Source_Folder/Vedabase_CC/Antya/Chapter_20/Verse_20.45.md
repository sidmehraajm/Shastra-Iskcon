# CC Antya 20.45

**Bengali:**

eta bhāve rādhāra mana asthira ha-ilā
sakhī-gaṇa-āge prauḍhi-śloka ye paḍilā

**Transliteration:**

eta bhāve — in that mood; rādhāra — of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; mana — the mind; asthira ha-ilā — became agitated; sakhī-gaṇa-āge — before the friends, the gopīs; ye — which; paḍilā — She recited.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bhāve — in that mood; rādhāra — of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī; mana — the mind; asthira ha-ilā — became agitated; sakhī-gaṇa-āge — before the friends, the gopīs; ye — which; paḍilā — She recited.

**Translation:**

Translation
In that mood, the mind of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī was agitated, and therefore She spoke a verse of advanced devotion to Her gopī friends.
