# CC Antya 14.109

**Bengali:**

rādhā lañā kṛṣṇa praveśilā kandarāte
sakhī-gaṇa kahe more phula uṭhāite

**Transliteration:**

rādhā lañā — taking Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī along; kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; praveśilā — entered; kandarāte — a cave; sakhī-gaṇa — the gopīs; more — unto Me; phula — flowers; uṭhāite — to pick up.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rādhā lañā — taking Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī along; kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; praveśilā — entered; kandarāte — a cave; sakhī-gaṇa — the gopīs; more — unto Me; phula — flowers; uṭhāite — to pick up.

**Translation:**

Translation
“When Kṛṣṇa and Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī entered a cave together, the other gopīs asked Me to pick some flowers.
