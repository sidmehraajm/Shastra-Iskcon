# CC Antya 18.54

**Bengali:**

maḍā-rūpa dhari’ rahe uttāna-nayana
kabhu goṅ-goṅ kare, kabhu rahe acetana

**Transliteration:**

maḍā — of a dead body; rūpa — the form; dhari’ — accepting; rahe — remains; uttāna-nayana — with open eyes; kabhu — sometimes; goṅ-goṅ — the sound goṅ-goṅ; kabhu — sometimes; rahe — remains; acetana — unconscious.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
maḍā — of a dead body; rūpa — the form; dhari’ — accepting; rahe — remains; uttāna-nayana — with open eyes; kabhu — sometimes; goṅ-goṅ — the sound goṅ-goṅ; kabhu — sometimes; rahe — remains; acetana — unconscious.

**Translation:**

Translation
“That ghost has taken the form of a corpse, but He keeps his eyes open. Sometimes He utters the sounds ‘goṅ-goṅ,’ and sometimes He remains unconscious.
