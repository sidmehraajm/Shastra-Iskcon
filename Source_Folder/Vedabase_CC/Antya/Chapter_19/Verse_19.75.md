# CC Antya 19.75

**Bengali:**

ei līlā mahāprabhura raghunātha-dāsa
gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣe kariyāche prakāśa

**Transliteration:**

ei līlā — this pastime; mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; raghunātha-dāsa — Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — in his book known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei līlā — this pastime; mahāprabhura — of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; raghunātha-dāsa — Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī; gaurāṅga-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe — in his book known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.

**Translation:**

Translation
This pastime of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s has been described very nicely by Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī in his book known as Gaurāṅga-stava-kalpavṛkṣa.
