# CC Antya 19.32

**Bengali:**

ācambite sphure kṛṣṇera mathurā-gamana
udghūrṇā-daśā haila unmāda-lakṣaṇa

**Transliteration:**

ācambite — suddenly; sphure — there awoke; kṛṣṇera — of Lord Kṛṣṇa; mathurā-gamana — the departure for Mathurā; udghūrṇā-daśā — the ecstatic condition known as udghūrṇā; unmāda-lakṣaṇa — the symptom of madness.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ācambite — suddenly; sphure — there awoke; kṛṣṇera — of Lord Kṛṣṇa; mathurā-gamana — the departure for Mathurā; udghūrṇā-daśā — the ecstatic condition known as udghūrṇā; unmāda-lakṣaṇa — the symptom of madness.

**Translation:**

Translation
Suddenly there awoke within Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu the scene of Lord Kṛṣṇa’s departure to Mathurā, and He began exhibiting the symptom of ecstatic madness known as udghūrṇā.
