# CC Antya 6.59

**Bengali:**

dhuti pari’ prabhu yadi piṇḍāte vasilā
sāta-kuṇḍī vipra tāṅra āgete dharilā

**Transliteration:**

dhuti pari’ — putting on a new cloth; prabhu — Lord Nityānanda; yadi — when; piṇḍāte vasilā — sat on a high platform; sāta-kuṇḍī — the seven big, big earthen pots; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; dharilā — placed.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dhuti pari’ — putting on a new cloth; prabhu — Lord Nityānanda; yadi — when; piṇḍāte vasilā — sat on a high platform; sāta-kuṇḍī — the seven big, big earthen pots; vipra — the brāhmaṇa; dharilā — placed.

**Translation:**

Translation
After Nityānanda Prabhu had changed His cloth for a new one and sat on a raised platform, the brāhmaṇa brought before Him the seven huge pots.
