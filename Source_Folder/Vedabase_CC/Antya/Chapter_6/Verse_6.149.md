# CC Antya 6.149

**Bengali:**

aneka ‘prasāda’ dilā pathe khāibāre
tabe punaḥ raghunātha kahe paṇḍitere

**Transliteration:**

aneka prasāda — much prasādam; pathe khāibāre — to eat on his way; tabe — then; punaḥ — again; raghunātha kahe — Raghunātha dāsa said; paṇḍitere — to Rāghava Paṇḍita.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aneka prasāda — much prasādam; pathe khāibāre — to eat on his way; tabe — then; punaḥ — again; raghunātha kahe — Raghunātha dāsa said; paṇḍitere — to Rāghava Paṇḍita.

**Translation:**

Translation
He gave Raghunātha dāsa a large quantity of prasādam to eat on his way home. Then Raghunātha dāsa again spoke to Rāghava Paṇḍita.
