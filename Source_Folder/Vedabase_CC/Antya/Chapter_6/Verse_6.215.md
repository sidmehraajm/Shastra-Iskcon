# CC Antya 6.215

**Bengali:**

jagannāthera sevaka yata — ‘viṣayīra gaṇa’
sevā sāri’ rātrye kare gṛhete gamana

**Transliteration:**

jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; sevaka — servants; yata — all; viṣayīra gaṇa — generally known as viṣayīs; rātrye — at night; kare — do; gṛhete gamana — returning home.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; sevaka — servants; yata — all; viṣayīra gaṇa — generally known as viṣayīs; rātrye — at night; kare — do; gṛhete gamana — returning home.

**Translation:**

Translation
After finishing their prescribed duties, the many servants of Lord Jagannātha, who are known as viṣayīs, return home at night.
