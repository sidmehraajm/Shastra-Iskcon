# CC Antya 6.324

**Bengali:**

prabhu bale, — “niti-niti nānā prasāda khāi
aiche svāda āra kona prasāde nā pāi”

**Transliteration:**

prabhu bale — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; niti-niti — day after day; nānā prasāda — varieties of prasādam; aiche svāda — such a nice taste; āra — other; kona — any; prasāde — in the remnants of Lord Jagannātha’s food; nā pāi — I do not get.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
prabhu bale — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said; niti-niti — day after day; nānā prasāda — varieties of prasādam; aiche svāda — such a nice taste; āra — other; kona — any; prasāde — in the remnants of Lord Jagannātha’s food; nā pāi — I do not get.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said, “Of course, every day I eat varieties of prasādam, but I have never tasted such nice prasādam as that which Raghunātha is eating.”
