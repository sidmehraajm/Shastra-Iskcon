# CC Antya 13.57

**Bengali:**

anya sannyāsīra vastra tumi dhara śire
kon aiche haya, — ihā pāre sahibāre?”

**Transliteration:**

anya sannyāsīra — of another sannyāsī; tumi — you; dhara — keep; śire — on the head; kon — who; aiche haya — is such; ihā — this; pāre sahibāre — can tolerate.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
anya sannyāsīra — of another sannyāsī; tumi — you; dhara — keep; śire — on the head; kon — who; aiche haya — is such; ihā — this; pāre sahibāre — can tolerate.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Still, you have bound your head with a cloth given to you by another sannyāsī. Who can tolerate such behavior?”
