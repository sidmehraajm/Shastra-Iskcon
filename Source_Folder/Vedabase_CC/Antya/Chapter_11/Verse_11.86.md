# CC Antya 11.86

**Bengali:**

āpane kāśī-miśra āilā prasāda lañā
prabhure bhikṣā karāilā āgraha kariyā

**Transliteration:**

āpane — personally; kāśī-miśra — Kāśī Miśra; āilā — came; prasāda lañā — taking prasādam; bhikṣā karāilā — delivered prasādam to eat; āgraha kariyā — with great attention.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āpane — personally; kāśī-miśra — Kāśī Miśra; āilā — came; prasāda lañā — taking prasādam; bhikṣā karāilā — delivered prasādam to eat; āgraha kariyā — with great attention.

**Translation:**

Translation
Therefore Kāśī Miśra personally went there and delivered prasādam to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu with great attention and made Him eat.
