# CC Antya 11.19

**Bengali:**

saṅkhyā-kīrtana pūre nāhi, ke-mate khāiba?
mahā-prasāda āniyācha, ke-mate upekṣiba?

**Transliteration:**

saṅkhyā-kīrtana — the fixed amount of chanting; pūre nāhi — is not complete; ke-mate khāiba — how shall I eat; mahā-prasāda āniyācha — you have brought the mahā-prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saṅkhyā-kīrtana — the fixed amount of chanting; pūre nāhi — is not complete; ke-mate khāiba — how shall I eat; mahā-prasāda āniyācha — you have brought the mahā-prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
“I have not finished chanting my regular number of rounds. How, then, can I eat? But you have brought mahā-prasādam, and how can I neglect it?”
