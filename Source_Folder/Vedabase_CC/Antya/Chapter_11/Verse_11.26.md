# CC Antya 11.26

**Bengali:**

ebe alpa saṅkhyā kari’ kara saṅkīrtana”
haridāsa kahe, — “śuna mora satya nivedana

**Transliteration:**

ebe — now; alpa saṅkhyā — a reduced amount of chanting; kari’ — doing; kara saṅkīrtana — chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; śuna — kindly hear; mora — my; satya — real; nivedana — submission.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ebe — now; alpa saṅkhyā — a reduced amount of chanting; kari’ — doing; kara saṅkīrtana — chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra; śuna — kindly hear; mora — my; satya — real; nivedana — submission.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord concluded, “Now, therefore, please reduce the fixed number of times you chant the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra.”

**Purport:**

Purport
Haridāsa Ṭhākura replied, “Kindly hear my real plea.
