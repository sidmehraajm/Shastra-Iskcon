# CC Antya 11.80

**Bengali:**

vāṇīnātha paṭṭanāyaka prasāda ānilā
kāśī-miśra aneka prasāda pāṭhāilā

**Transliteration:**

vāṇīnātha paṭṭanāyaka — Vāṇīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; prasāda — prasādam; kāśī-miśra — Kāśī Miśra; aneka prasāda — varieties of prasādam.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vāṇīnātha paṭṭanāyaka — Vāṇīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; prasāda — prasādam; kāśī-miśra — Kāśī Miśra; aneka prasāda — varieties of prasādam.

**Translation:**

Translation
Not only did Svarūpa Dāmodara Gosvāmī bring prasādam, but Vāṇīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka and Kāśī Miśra also sent large quantities.
