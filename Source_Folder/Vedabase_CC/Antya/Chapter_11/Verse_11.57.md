# CC Antya 11.57

**Bengali:**

mahā-yogeśvara-prāya dekhi’ svacchande maraṇa
‘bhīṣmera niryāṇa’ sabāra ha-ila smaraṇa

**Transliteration:**

mahā-yogeśvara-prāya — just like a great mystic yogī; svacchande — at his will; maraṇa — dying; bhīṣmera niryāṇa — the passing of Bhīṣma; sabāra ha-ila smaraṇa — everyone remembered.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-yogeśvara-prāya — just like a great mystic yogī; svacchande — at his will; maraṇa — dying; bhīṣmera niryāṇa — the passing of Bhīṣma; sabāra ha-ila smaraṇa — everyone remembered.

**Translation:**

Translation
Seeing the wonderful death of Haridāsa Ṭhākura by his own will, which was just like a great mystic yogī’s, everyone remembered the passing away of Bhīṣma.
