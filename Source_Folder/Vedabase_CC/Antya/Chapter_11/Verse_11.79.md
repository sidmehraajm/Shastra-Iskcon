# CC Antya 11.79

**Bengali:**

ei-mate nānā prasāda bojhā bāndhāñā
lañā āilā cāri janera mastake caḍāñā

**Transliteration:**

ei-mate — in this way; nānā — various; prasāda — prasādam; bāndhāñā — packing; lañā āilā — brought; cāri janera — of the four persons; mastake — on the heads; caḍāñā — mounting.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ei-mate — in this way; nānā — various; prasāda — prasādam; bāndhāñā — packing; lañā āilā — brought; cāri janera — of the four persons; mastake — on the heads; caḍāñā — mounting.

**Translation:**

Translation
In this way varieties of prasādam were collected, then packed up in different loads and carried on the heads of the four servants.
