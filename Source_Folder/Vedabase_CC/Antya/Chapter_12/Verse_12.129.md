# CC Antya 12.129

**Bengali:**

“āpane prasāda laha, pāche muñi la-imu
tomāra āgraha āmi kemane khaṇḍimu?”

**Transliteration:**

āpane — Yourself; prasāda laha — take prasādam; muñi la-imu — I shall take; tomāra — Your; āgraha — insistence; āmi — I; kemane — how; khaṇḍimu — shall disobey.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āpane — Yourself; prasāda laha — take prasādam; muñi la-imu — I shall take; tomāra — Your; āgraha — insistence; āmi — I; kemane — how; khaṇḍimu — shall disobey.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Please first take prasādam Yourself, and I shall eat later. I shall not refuse Your request.”
