# CC Antya 12.150

**Bengali:**

“dekha, — jagadānanda prasāda pāya ki nā pāya
śīghra āsi’ samācāra kahibe āmāya”

**Transliteration:**

dekha — see; jagadānanda — Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; prasāda — prasādam; ki — or; nā — not; pāya — gets; śīghra āsi’ — coming hastily; samācāra — the news; kahibe — inform; āmāya — to Me.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
dekha — see; jagadānanda — Jagadānanda Paṇḍita; prasāda — prasādam; ki — or; nā — not; pāya — gets; śīghra āsi’ — coming hastily; samācāra — the news; kahibe — inform; āmāya — to Me.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord told him, “Go see whether Jagadānanda Paṇḍita is eating. Then quickly return and let Me know.”
