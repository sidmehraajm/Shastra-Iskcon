# CC Antya 3.63

**Bengali:**

nāmābhāsa haite haya saṁsārera kṣaya

**Transliteration:**

nāma-ābhāsa haite — even on account of nāmābhāsa; saṁsārera kṣaya — deliverance from material bondage.

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
nāma-ābhāsa haite — even on account of nāmābhāsa; saṁsārera kṣaya — deliverance from material bondage.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Even a faint light from the holy name of the Lord can eradicate all the reactions of sinful life.
