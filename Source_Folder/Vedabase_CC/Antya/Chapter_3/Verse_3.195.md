# CC Antya 3.195

**Bengali:**

haridāsa kahena, — kene karaha saṁśaya?
śāstre kahe, — nāmābhāsa-mātre ‘mukti’ haya

**Transliteration:**

haridāsa kahena — Haridāsa Ṭhākura said; kene — why; karaha saṁśaya — are you doubtful; śāstre kahe — it is stated in the revealed scriptures; nāma-ābhāsa</em> <em>mātre — simply by a glimpse of the chanting of the holy name; mukti haya — there is liberation.

**Translation:**

Translation
Haridāsa Ṭhākura said, “Why are you doubtful? The revealed scriptures say that one can attain liberation simply by a glimpse of offenseless chanting of the holy name.
