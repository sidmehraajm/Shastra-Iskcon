# CC Madhya 17.102

**Bengali:**

বিপ্র সব নিমন্ত্রয়, প্রভু নাহি মানে ।
প্রভু কহে, — ‘আজি মোর হঞাছে নিমন্ত্রণে’ ॥ ১০২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vipra saba nimantraya, prabhu nāhi māne
prabhu kahe, — ‘āji mora hañāche nimantraṇe’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vipra — the brāhmaṇas; nimantraya — invite; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; nāhi māne — does not accept; prabhu kahe — the Lord replies; āji — today; mora — My; hañāche — has been; nimantraṇe — invitation.

**Translation:**

Translation
When the brāhmaṇas of Vārāṇasī would invite Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu to lunch, the Lord would not accept their invitations. He would reply, “I have already been invited somewhere else.”
