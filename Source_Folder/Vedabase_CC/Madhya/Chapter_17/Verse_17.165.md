# CC Madhya 17.165

**Bengali:**

‘আর্য, সরল, তুমি — বৃদ্ধ ব্রাহ্মণ ।
কাহাঁ হৈতে পাইলে তুমি এই প্রেমধন?’ ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘ārya, sarala, tumi — vṛddha brāhmaṇa
kāhāṅ haite pāile tumi ei prema-dhana?’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ārya — advanced in devotional service; sarala — simple; tumi — you; vṛddha brāhmaṇa — elderly brāhmaṇa; pāile tumi — did you obtain; ei — this; prema-dhana — transcendental opulence of ecstatic love.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu said, “You are an elderly brāhmaṇa, you are sincere, and you are advanced in spiritual life. Wherefrom have you gotten this transcendental opulence of ecstatic love for Kṛṣṇa?”
