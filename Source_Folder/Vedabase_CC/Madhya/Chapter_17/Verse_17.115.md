# CC Madhya 17.115

**Bengali:**

শুনিয়া প্রকাশানন্দ বহুত হাসিলা ।
বিপ্রে উপহাস করি’ কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ১১৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śuniyā prakāśānanda bahuta hāsilā
vipre upahāsa kari’ kahite lāgilā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śuniyā — hearing; prakāśānanda — Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī; bahuta hāsilā — laughed very much; vipre — at the brāhmaṇa; kahite lāgilā — began to speak.

**Translation:**

Translation
Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī laughed very much to hear this description. Joking and laughing at the brāhmaṇa, he began to speak as follows.
