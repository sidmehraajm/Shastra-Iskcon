# CC Madhya 4.207

**Bengali:**

সাত ক্ষীর পূজারীকে বাহুড়িয়া দিল ।
পঞ্চক্ষীর পঞ্চজনে বাঁটিয়া খাইল ॥ ২০৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sāta kṣīra pūjārīke bāhuḍiyā dila
pañca-kṣīra pañca-jane vāṅṭiyā khāila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sāta kṣīra — seven pots of sweet rice; pūjārīke — toward the pūjārī; dila — gave; pañca-kṣīra — five pots of sweet rice; pañca-jane — to five men; vāṅṭiyā — distributing; khāila — ate.

**Translation:**

Translation
The seven remaining pots were pushed forward and delivered to the priest. Then the five pots of sweet rice the Lord had accepted were distributed among the five devotees, and they ate the prasādam.
