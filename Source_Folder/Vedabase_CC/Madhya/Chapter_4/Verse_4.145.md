# CC Madhya 4.145

**Bengali:**

‘মাধবপুরী শ্রীপাদ আইল’, — লোকে হৈল খ্যাতি ।
সব লোক আসি’ তাঁরে করে বহু ভক্তি ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘mādhava-purī śrīpāda āila’, — loke haila khyāti
saba loka āsi’ tāṅre kare bahu bhakti

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mādhava-purī — Śrī Mādhavendra Purī; śrīpāda — sannyāsī; loke — among the people; haila — there was; khyāti — reputation; saba loka — all people; āsi’ — coming; tāṅre — unto him; kare — do; bahu — much; bhakti — devotion.

**Translation:**

Translation
When Mādhavendra Purī came to Jagannātha Purī, people were aware of his transcendental reputation. Therefore crowds of people came and offered him all sorts of respect in devotion.
