# CC Madhya 15.29

**Bengali:**

কানাঞি-খুটিয়া, জগন্নাথ, — দুইজন ।
আবেশে বিলাইল ঘরে ছিল যত ধন ॥ ২৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kānāñi-khuṭiyā, jagannātha, — dui-jana
āveśe vilāila ghare chila yata dhana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kānāñi-khuṭiyā — Kānāñi Khuṭiyā; jagannātha — Jagannātha Māhāti; dui-jana — two persons; āveśe — in ecstatic love; vilāila — distributed; ghare — at home; chila — was; yata — all; dhana — riches.

**Translation:**

Translation
In ecstasy, Kānāñi Khuṭiyā, who was dressed as Nanda Mahārāja, and Jagannātha Māhiti, who was dressed as mother Yaśodā, distributed all the riches they had stocked at home.
