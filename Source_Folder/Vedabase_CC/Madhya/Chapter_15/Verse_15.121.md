# CC Madhya 15.121

**Bengali:**

এক দিন ম্লেচ্ছ-রাজার উচ্চ-টুঙ্গিতে ।
চিকিৎসার বাত্‌ কহে তাঁহার অগ্রেতে ॥ ১২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eka dina mleccha-rājāra ucca-ṭuṅgite
cikitsāra bāt kahe tāṅhāra agrete

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eka dina — one day; mleccha-rājāra — of the Muslim King; ucca-ṭuṅgite — on a high platform; cikitsāra bāt — talk of medical treatment; kahe — was speaking; tāṅhāra agrete — before him.

**Translation:**

Translation
“One day Mukunda dāsa, the royal physician, was seated with the Muslim King on a high platform and was telling the King about medical treatment.
