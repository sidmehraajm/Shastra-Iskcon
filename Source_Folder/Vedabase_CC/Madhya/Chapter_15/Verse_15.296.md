# CC Madhya 15.296

**Bengali:**

সেই অমোঘ হৈল প্রভুর ভক্ত ‘একান্ত’ ।
প্রেমে নাচে, কৃষ্ণনাম লয় মহাশান্ত ॥ ২৯৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sei amogha haila prabhura bhakta ‘ekānta’
preme nāce, kṛṣṇa-nāma laya mahā-śānta

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sei amogha — that same Amogha; haila — became; prabhura — of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; bhakta — devotee; ekānta — unflinching; preme nāce — dances in ecstasy; kṛṣṇa-nāma laya — chants the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra.

**Translation:**

Translation
Thereafter, Amogha became an unalloyed devotee of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. He danced in ecstasy and peacefully chanted the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa.
