# CC Madhya 5.34

**Bengali:**

এত বলি’ দুইজনে চলিলা দেশেরে ।
গুরুবুদ্ধ্যে ছোট-বিপ্ৰ বহু সেবা করে ॥ ৩৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta bali’ dui-jane calilā deśere
guru-buddhye choṭa-vipra bahu sevā kare

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bali’ — speaking this; dui-jane — both the brāhmaṇas; deśere — toward their own country; guru-buddhye — accepting the elderly brāhmaṇa as guru; sevā — services; kare — renders.

**Translation:**

Translation
After these talks, the two brāhmaṇas started for home. As usual, the young brāhmaṇa accompanied the elderly brāhmaṇa as if the older brāhmaṇa were a guru [spiritual master] and rendered him service in various ways.
