# CC Madhya 5.106

**Bengali:**

এত ভাবি’ সেই বিপ্র ফিরিয়া চাহিল ।
হাসিঞা গোপাল-দেব তথায় রহিল ॥ ১০৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

eta bhāvi’ sei vipra phiriyā cāhila
hāsiñā gopāla-deva tathāya rahila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
eta bhāvi’ — thinking like this; sei — that; vipra — brāhmaṇa; cāhila — saw; hāsiñā — smiling; gopāla-deva — Lord Gopāladeva, the Supreme Personality of Godhead; tathāya — there; rahila — stayed.

**Translation:**

Translation
Thinking this, the brāhmaṇa turned to look back, and He saw that Gopāla, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, was standing there smiling.
