# CC Madhya 24.82

**Bengali:**

‘ব্রহ্ম-আত্মা’-শব্দে যদি কৃষ্ণেরে কহয় ।
‘রূঢ়িবৃত্ত্যে’ নির্বিশেষ অন্তর্যামী কয় ॥ ৮২ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘brahma-ātmā’-śabde yadi kṛṣṇere kahaya
‘rūḍhi-vṛttye’ nirviśeṣa antaryāmī kaya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
brahma-ātmā-śabde — by the words brahma and ātmā; kṛṣṇere kahaya — Kṛṣṇa is indicated; rūḍhi-vṛttye — by the direct meaning; nirviśeṣa — impersonal; antaryāmī — the Supersoul; kaya — is said.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Although the words ‘brahma’ and ‘ātmā’ indicate Kṛṣṇa, their direct meaning refers only to the impersonal Brahman and the Supersoul respectively.
