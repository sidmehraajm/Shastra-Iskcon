# CC Madhya 16.95

**Bengali:**

জগন্নাথের প্রসাদ প্রভু যত পাঞাছিল ।
কড়ার, চন্দন, ডোর, সব সঙ্গে লৈল ॥ ৯৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

jagannāthera prasāda prabhu yata pāñāchila
kaḍāra, candana, ḍora, saba saṅge laila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
jagannāthera — of Lord Jagannātha; prasāda — the remnants of food; prabhu — Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; yata — all; pāñāchila — had obtained; kaḍāra — a kind of tilaka; ḍora — ropes; saba — all; saṅge laila — He took with Him.

**Translation:**

Translation
The Lord collected whatever remnants of food were left by Lord Jagannātha. He also took remnants of the Lord’s kaḍāra ointment, sandalwood and ropes with Him.
