# CC Madhya 16.197

**Bengali:**

মহাপাত্রে মহাপ্ৰভু করিলা বিদায় ।
কান্দিতে কান্দিতে সেই তীরে রহি’ চায় ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mahā-pātre mahāprabhu karilā vidāya
kāndite kāndite sei tīre rahi’ cāya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mahā-pātre — unto the mahā-pātra; karilā vidāya — bade farewell; kāndite kāndite — crying and crying; sei — that mahā-pātra; rahi’ cāya — stood and watched.

**Translation:**

Translation
Finally Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu bade farewell to the mahā-pātra. Standing on the riverbank and looking at the boat, the mahā-pātra began to cry.
