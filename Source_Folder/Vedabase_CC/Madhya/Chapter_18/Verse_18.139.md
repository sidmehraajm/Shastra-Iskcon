# CC Madhya 18.139

**Bengali:**

তবে ভট্টাচার্য সেই ব্রাহ্মণে লঞা ।
যুক্তি করিলা কিছু নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

tabe bhaṭṭācārya sei brāhmaṇe lañā
yukti karilā kichu nibhṛte vasiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
tabe — thereafter; bhaṭṭācārya — Bhaṭṭācārya; sei brāhmaṇe — the Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa; yukti karilā — consulted; kichu — something; nibhṛte vasiyā — sitting in a solitary place.

**Translation:**

Translation
After this, Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya took the Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa to a secluded place and consulted with him.
