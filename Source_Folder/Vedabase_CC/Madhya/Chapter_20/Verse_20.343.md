# CC Madhya 20.343

**Bengali:**

আর তিনযুগে ধ্যানাদিতে যেই ফল হয় ।
কলিযুগে কৃষ্ণনামে সেই ফল পায় ॥ ৩৪৩ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āra tina-yuge dhyānādite yei phala haya
kali-yuge kṛṣṇa-nāme sei phala pāya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āra tina-yuge — in the three other yugas; yei — whatever; phala — result; haya — there is; kali-yuge — in this Age of Kali; kṛṣṇa-nāme — by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In the other three yugas — Satya, Tretā and Dvāpara — people perform different types of spiritual activities. Whatever results they achieve in that way, they can achieve in Kali-yuga simply by chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra.
