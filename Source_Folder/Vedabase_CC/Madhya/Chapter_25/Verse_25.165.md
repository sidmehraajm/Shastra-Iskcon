# CC Madhya 25.165

**Bengali:**

সব কাশীবাসী করে নামসংকীর্তন ।
প্রেমে হাসে, কাঁদে, গায়, করয়ে নর্তন ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

saba kāśī-vāsī kare nāma-saṅkīrtana
preme hāse, kāṅde, gāya, karaye nartana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
saba kāśī-vāsī — all the inhabitants of Kāśī (Vārāṇasī); kare — performed; nāma-saṅkīrtana — chanting of the Hare Kṛṣṇa mantra; hāse — they laughed; kāṅde — they cried; gāya — chanted; karaye nartana — and danced.

**Translation:**

Translation
All the inhabitants of Kāśī [Vārāṇasī] began chanting the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra in ecstatic love. Sometimes they laughed, sometimes they cried, sometimes they chanted, and sometimes they danced.
