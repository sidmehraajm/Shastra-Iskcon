# CC Madhya 14.26

**Bengali:**

ছানা, পানা, পৈড়, আম্র, নারিকেল, কাঁঠাল ।
নানাবিধ কদলক, আর বীজ-তাল ॥ ২৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

chānā, pānā, paiḍa, āmra, nārikela, kāṅṭhāla
nānā-vidha kadalaka, āra bīja-tāla

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
chānā — curd; pānā — fruit juice; paiḍa — coconut; āmra — mango; nārikela — dried coconut; kāṅṭhāla — jackfruit; nānā-vidha — various kinds of; kadalaka — bananas; āra — and; bīja-tāla — palm-fruit seeds.

**Translation:**

Translation
There were curd, fruit juice, coconut, mango, dried coconut, jackfruit, various kinds of bananas and palm-fruit seeds.

**Purport:**

Purport
This is the first list of prasādam offered to Lord Jagannātha.
