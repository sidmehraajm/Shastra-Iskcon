# CC Madhya 14.28

**Bengali:**

মনোহরা-লাড়ু আদি শতেক প্রকার ।
অমৃতগুটিকা-আদি, ক্ষীরসা অপার ॥ ২৮ ॥

**Transliteration:**

manoharā-lāḍu ādi śateka prakāra
amṛta-guṭikā-ādi, kṣīrasā apāra

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
manoharā-lāḍu — a kind of sandeśa; śateka prakāra — hundreds of varieties; amṛta-guṭikā — round sweetmeats; ādi — and others; kṣīrasā — condensed milk; apāra — of various qualities.

**Translation:**

Translation
There were hundreds of different types of sweetmeats like manoharā-lāḍu, sweets like amṛta-guṭikā and various types of condensed milk.
