# CC Madhya 14.125

**Bengali:**

গোপী-সঙ্গে যত লীলা হয় উপবনে ।
নিগূঢ় কৃষ্ণের ভাব কেহ নাহি জানে ॥ ১২৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gopī-saṅge yata līlā haya upavane
nigūḍha kṛṣṇera bhāva keha nāhi jāne

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gopī-saṅge — with the gopīs; haya upavane — that are in those gardens; nigūḍha — very confidential; kṛṣṇera — of Lord Kṛṣṇa; bhāva — ecstasies; keha — anyone; nāhi — does not; jāne — know.

**Translation:**

Translation
“All the pastimes with the gopīs that take place in those gardens are very confidential ecstasies of Lord Kṛṣṇa. No one knows them.
