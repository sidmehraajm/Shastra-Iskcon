# CC Madhya 7.75

**Bengali:**

ভক্তগণ শীঘ্র আসি’ লৈল প্রভুর সাথ ।
বস্ত্র-প্রসাদ লঞা তবে আইলা গোপীনাথ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

bhakta-gaṇa śīghra āsi’ laila prabhura sātha
vastra-prasāda lañā tabe āilā gopīnātha

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
bhakta-gaṇa — devotees; śīghra — very swiftly; āsi’ — coming; laila — took; prabhura — of the Lord; sātha — the company; vastra — the garments; prasāda — and Lord Jagannātha’s prasādam; tabe — thereafter; āilā — came; gopīnātha — Gopīnātha Ācārya.

**Translation:**

Translation
Immediately all the devotees came and partook of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s company. Afterwards, Gopīnātha Ācārya came with the garments and prasādam.
