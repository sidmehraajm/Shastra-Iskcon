# CC Madhya 3.177

**Bengali:**

সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম নহে — সন্ন্যাস করিয়া ।
নিজ জন্মস্থানে রহে কুটুম্ব লঞা ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

sannyāsīra dharma nahe — sannyāsa kariñā
nija janma-sthāne rahe kuṭumba lañā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
sannyāsīra — of a sannyāsī; nahe — it is not; sannyāsa — sannyāsa; nija — own; janma-sthāne — at the birthplace; rahe — remains; kuṭumba — relatives; lañā — with.

**Translation:**

Translation
“After accepting sannyāsa, it is not the duty of a sannyāsī to remain at his birthplace, encircled by relatives.
