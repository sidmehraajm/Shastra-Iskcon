# CC Madhya 1.245

**Bengali:**

মথুরা পাঠাইলা তাঁরে দিয়া ভক্তিবল ।
সন্ন্যাসীরে কৃপা করি’ গেলা নীলাচল ॥ ২৪৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

mathurā pāṭhāilā tāṅre diyā bhakti-bala
sannyāsīre kṛpā kari’ gelā nīlācala

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
mathurā — to Mathurā; pāṭhāilā — sent; tāṅre — him; diyā — giving; bhakti-bala — the strength of devotion; sannyāsīre — unto the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs; kari’ — giving; gelā — went back; nīlācala — to Jagannātha Purī.

**Translation:**

Translation
After fully instructing Sanātana Gosvāmī, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sent him to Mathurā with empowered devotional service. In Benares He also bestowed His mercy upon the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs. He then returned to Nīlācala [Jagannātha Purī].
