# CC Madhya 13.26

**Bengali:**

রথে চড়ি’ জগন্নাথ করিলা গমন ।
দুইপার্শ্বে দেখি’ চলে আনন্দিত-মন ॥ ২৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

rathe caḍi’ jagannātha karilā gamana
dui-pārśve dekhi’ cale ānandita-mana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
rathe caḍi’ — riding on the car; jagannātha — Lord Jagannātha; karilā</em> <em>gamana — was passing; dui-pārśve — on both sides; dekhi’ — seeing; cale — goes; ānandita — full of pleasure; mana — mind.

**Translation:**

Translation
As Lord Jagannātha rode in His car and saw the beauty on both sides, His mind was filled with pleasure.
