# CC Madhya 8.109

**Bengali:**

শতকোটি গোপী-সঙ্গে রাস-বিলাস ।
তার মধ্যে এক-মূর্ত্যে রহে রাধা-পাশ ॥ ১০৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

śata-koṭi gopī-saṅge rāsa-vilāsa
tāra madhye eka-mūrtye rahe rādhā-pāśa

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śata-koṭi — hundreds of thousands; gopī-saṅge — with the gopīs; tāra madhye — among them; eka-mūrtye — by one of His transcendental forms; rahe — remains; rādhā-pāśa — by the side of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Although Kṛṣṇa was in the midst of hundreds of thousands of gopīs during the rāsa dance, He still kept Himself in one of His transcendental forms by the side of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī.
