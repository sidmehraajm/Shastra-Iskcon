# CC Madhya 11.137

**Bengali:**

বাসুদেব দেখি’ প্রভু আনন্দিত হঞা ।
তাঁরে কিছু কহে তাঁর অঙ্গে হস্ত দিয়া ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

vāsudeva dekhi’ prabhu ānandita hañā
tāṅre kichu kahe tāṅra aṅge hasta diyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
vāsudeva — Vāsudeva; dekhi’ — seeing; prabhu — Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu; ānandita hañā — becoming very pleased; tāṅre — unto him; kichu kahe — says something; tāṅra aṅge — on his body; hasta diyā — placing His hand.

**Translation:**

Translation
As soon as Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu saw Vāsudeva Datta, the older brother of Mukunda Datta, He immediately became very happy and, placing His hand on his body, began to speak.
