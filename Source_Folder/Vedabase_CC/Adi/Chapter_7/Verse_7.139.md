# CC Ādi 7.139

**Bengali:**

স্বরূপ–ঐশ্বর্যে তাঁর নাহি মায়াগন্ধ ।
সকল বেদের হয় ভগবান্‌ সে ‘সম্বন্ধ’ ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

svarūpa-aiśvarye tāṅra nāhi māyā-gandha
sakala vedera haya bhagavān se ‘sambandha’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
svarūpa — in His original form; aiśvarye — opulence; tāṅra — His; nāhi — there is none; māyā-gandha — contamination of the material world; sakala — in all; vedera — Vedas; bhagavān — the Supreme Personality of Godhead; se — that; sambandha — relationship.

**Translation:**

Translation
“In His original form the Supreme Personality of Godhead is full with transcendental opulences, which are free from the contamination of the material world. It is to be understood that in all Vedic literature the Supreme Personality of Godhead is the ultimate goal.
