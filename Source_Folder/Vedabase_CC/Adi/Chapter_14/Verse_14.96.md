# CC Ādi 14.96

**Bengali:**

অতএব এই লীলা সংক্ষেপে সূত্র কৈল ।
পুনরুক্তি–ভয়ে বিস্তারিয়া না কহিল ॥ ৯৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ataeva ei-līlā saṅkṣepe sūtra kaila
punarukti-bhaye vistāriyā nā kahila

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ataeva — therefore; ei-līlā — these pastimes; saṅkṣepe — in brief; sūtra — synopsis; kaila — made; punar-ukti — repetition; bhaye — being afraid of; vistāriyā — elaborate explanation; nā — did not; kahila — say.

**Translation:**

Translation
I have therefore made only a brief summary. Being afraid of repetition, I have not elaborated upon this subject matter.
