# CC Ādi 2.105

**Bengali:**

যদ্যপি ব্রহ্মাণ্ডগণের পুরুষ আশ্রয় ।
সেই পুরুষাদি সবার কৃষ্ণ মূলাশ্রয় ॥ ১০৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

yadyapi brahmāṇḍa-gaṇera puruṣa āśraya
sei puruṣādi sabhāra kṛṣṇa mūlāśraya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
yadyapi — although; brahma-aṇḍa-gaṇera — of the multitude of universes; puruṣa — the puruṣa-avatāra; sei — that; puruṣa-ādi — of the puruṣa-avatāras, etc.; sabhāra — of the assembly; kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; mūla-āśraya — original source.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Although the three puruṣas are the shelter of all the universes, Lord Kṛṣṇa is the original source of the puruṣas.
