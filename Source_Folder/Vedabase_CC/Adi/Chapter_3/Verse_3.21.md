# CC Ādi 3.21

**Bengali:**

আপনে না কৈলে ধর্ম শিখান না যায় ।
এই ত’ সিদ্ধান্ত গীতা–ভাগবতে গায় ॥ ২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

āpane nā kaile dharma śikhāna nā yāya
ei ta’ siddhānta gītā-bhāgavate gāya

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
āpane — personally; nā kaile — if not practiced; dharma — religion; śikhāna — the teaching; nā yāya — does not advance; ei — this; ta’ — certainly; siddhānta — conclusion; gītā — in the Bhagavad-gītā.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Unless one practices devotional service himself, he cannot teach it to others. This conclusion is indeed confirmed throughout the Gītā and Bhāgavatam.
