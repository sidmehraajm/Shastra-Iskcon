# CC Ādi 17.286

**Bengali:**

চতুর্ভুজ মূর্তি ধরি’ আছেন বসিয়া ।
কৃষ্ণ দেখি’ গোপী কহে নিকটে আসিয়া ॥ ২৮৬ ॥

**Transliteration:**

catur-bhuja mūrti dhari’ āchena vasiyā
kṛṣṇa dekhi’ gopī kahe nikaṭe āsiyā

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
catur-bhuja — four-armed; mūrti — form; dhari’ — accepting; āchena — was; vasiyā — sitting; kṛṣṇa — Lord Kṛṣṇa; dekhi’ — seeing; gopī — the gopīs; nikaṭe — nearby; āsiyā — coming there.

**Translation:**

Translation
Kṛṣṇa assumed His four-armed Nārāyaṇa form and sat there. When all the gopīs came, they looked at Him and spoke as follows.
