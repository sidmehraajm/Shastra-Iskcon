# CC Ādi 17.39

**Bengali:**

কলার পাত উপরে থুইল ওড়–ফুল ।
হরিদ্রা, সিন্দুর আর রক্তচন্দন, তণ্ডুল ॥ ৩৯ ॥

**Transliteration:**

kalāra pāta upare thuila oḍa-phula
haridrā, sindūra āra rakta-candana, taṇḍula

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
kalāra pāta — a banana leaf; upare — upon it; thuila — placed; oḍaphula — a particular type of flower; haridrā — turmeric; sindūra — vermilion; āra — and; rakta-candana — red sandalwood; taṇḍula — rice.

**Translation:**

Translation
On the upper portion of a plantain leaf he placed such paraphernalia for worship as oḍa-phula, turmeric, vermilion, red sandalwood and rice.
