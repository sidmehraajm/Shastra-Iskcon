# CC Ādi 17.321

**Bengali:**

অষ্টমে ‘চৈতন্যলীলা–বর্ণন’–কারণ ।
এক কৃষ্ণনামের মহা–মহিমা–কথন ॥ ৩২১ ॥

**Transliteration:**

aṣṭame ‘caitanya-līlā-varṇana’-kāraṇa
eka kṛṣṇa-nāmera mahā-mahimā-kathana

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
aṣṭame — in the eighth chapter; caitanya-līlā-varṇana-kāraṇa — the reason for describing Caitanya Mahāprabhu’s pastimes; eka — one; kṛṣṇa-nāmera — of the holy name of Lord Kṛṣṇa; mahā-mahimākathana — description of great glories.

**Translation:**

Translation
The eighth chapter gives the reason for describing Lord Caitanya’s pastimes. It also describes the greatness of Lord Kṛṣṇa’s holy name.
