# CC Ādi 12.34

**Bengali:**

আচার্যেরে স্থাপিয়াছে করিয়া ঈশ্বর ।
ইথে দোষ নাহি, আচার্য—দৈবত ঈশ্বর ॥ ৩৪ ॥

**Transliteration:**

ācāryere sthāpiyāche kariyā īśvara
ithe doṣa nāhi, ācārya — daivata īśvara

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
ācāryere — unto Śrī Advaita Ācārya; sthāpiyāche — he established; kariyā — mentioning; īśvara — as the Supreme Personality of Godhead; ithe — in this; doṣa — fault; nāhi — there is not; ācārya — Advaita Ācārya; daivata</em> <em>īśvara — He is actually the Supreme Personality of Godhead.

**Translation:**

Translation
“He has established Advaita Ācārya as an incarnation of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. There is nothing wrong in this, for He is indeed the Lord Himself.
