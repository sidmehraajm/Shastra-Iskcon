# CC Ādi 11.17

**Bengali:**

গদাধর দাস গোপীভাবে পূৰ্ণানন্দ ।
যাঁর ঘরে দানকেলি কৈল নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ১৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

gadādhara dāsa gopībhāve pūrṇānanda
yāṅra ghare dānakeli kaila nityānanda

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gadādhara dāsa — Gadādhara dāsa; gopī-bhāve — in the ecstasy of the gopīs; pūrṇa-ānanda — fully in transcendental bliss; yāṅra ghare — in whose house; dāna-keli — performance of dānakeli-līlā; nityānanda — Lord Nityānanda Prabhu.

**Translation:**

Translation
Śrīla Gadādhara dāsa was always fully absorbed in ecstasy as a gopī. In his house Lord Nityānanda enacted the drama Dāna-keli.
