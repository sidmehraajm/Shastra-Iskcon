# CC Ādi 13.25

**Bengali:**

‘গৌরহরি’ বলি’ তারে হাসে সর্ব নারী ।
অতএব হৈল তাঁর নাম ‘গৌরহরি’ ॥ ২৫ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘gaurahari’ bali’ tāre hāse sarva nārī
ataeva haila tāṅra nāma ‘gaurahari’

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
gaurahari — Gaurahari; bali’ — addressing Him thus; tāre — unto the Lord; hāse — laugh; sarva nārī — all the ladies; ataeva — therefore; haila — became; tāṅra — His; nāma — name; gaurahari — Gaurahari.

**Translation:**

Translation
When all the ladies saw this fun, they enjoyed laughing and called the Lord “Gaurahari.” From then on, Gaurahari became another of His names.
