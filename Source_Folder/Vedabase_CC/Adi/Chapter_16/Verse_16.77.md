# CC Ādi 16.77

**Bengali:**

‘শ্রীযুক্ত লক্ষ্মী’ অর্থে অর্থের বিভেদ ।
পুনরুক্তবদাভাস, শব্দালঙ্কার ভেদ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

**Transliteration:**

‘śrī-yukta lakṣmī’ arthe arthera vibheda
punar-uktavad-ābhāsa, śabdālaṅkāra-bheda

**Synonyms:**

Synonyms
śrī-yukta lakṣmī — Lakṣmī, possessed of opulence; arthe — in the sense; arthera — of the meaning; vibheda — difference; punar-uktavad-ābhāsa — tinge of punar-ukta-vat; bheda — different.

**Translation:**

Translation
“Describing Lakṣmī as possessed of śrī [opulence] offers a difference in meaning with a tinge of repetition. This is the second ornamental use of words.
