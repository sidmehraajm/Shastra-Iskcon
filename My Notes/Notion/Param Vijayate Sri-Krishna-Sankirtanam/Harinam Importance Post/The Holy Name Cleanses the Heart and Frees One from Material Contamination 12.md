_Quote of the day:"The Holy Name Cleanses the Heart and Frees One from Material Contamination"_

_Offenseless chanting leads to realization of the form, qualities and pastimes of the Lord: By offenseless chanting and hearing of the holy name of the Lord, one becomes acquainted with the transcendental form of the Lord_, then with the attributes of the Lord, and then with the transcendental nature of His pastimes, etc... Hearing of the holy name gradually promotes one to the stage of hearing about His form, about His attributes, His pastimes and so on, and thus the necessity of the chanting of His glories develops successively*.  
¯Srimad−Bhagavatam 2.1.11
