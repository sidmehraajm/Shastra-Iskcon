POINTS - ABANDON ALL RELIGION & SURRENDER

Shri Bhagvan Uvachah  
_**sarva-dharmān parityajya**_  
_**mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja**_  
_**ahaṁ tvāṁ sarva-pāpebhyo**_  
_**mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ**_  

Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me. I shall deliver you from all sinful reactions. Do not fear.
