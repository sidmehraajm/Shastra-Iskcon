### **Tools and Development**

- **The Skeleton Lab**
    - _Description_: A modular auto-rigging system in Maya using Python, designed to provide efficient, one-directional rigging setups.
    - _Highlights_: Streamlines rigging workflows across multiple characters; includes tools for joint positioning and skin data setups.
- **VN Skin Tools** (In collaboration with Vishal Nagpal)
    - _Description_: A Maya tool that simplifies the conversion of deformers into unified skin setups, enhancing workflow efficiency and precision.
    - _Highlights_: Seamless integration for new deformations into existing skin configurations; provides efficient solutions for enhancing character skinning.
- **Bifrost Modular Rigging Concept Proof**
    - _Description_: A proof-of-concept using Bifrost, showcasing the potential for procedural rigging in Autodesk’s ecosystem.
    - _Highlights_: Demonstrates the ability to create auto-rigging modules, introducing scalable and flexible rigging options for both characters and props.
