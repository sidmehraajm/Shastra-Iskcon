## **Translation**

puruṣo rāma-caritaṁ  
śravaṇair upadhārayan  
ānṛśaṁsya-paro rājan  
karma-bandhair vimucyate  

**O King Parīkṣit, anyone who aurally recites the narrations concerning the characteristics of Lord Rāmacandra’s pastimes will ultimately be freed from the disease of envy and thus be liberated from the bondage of furtive activities.**
