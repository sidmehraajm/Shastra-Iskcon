### One performing the duty correctly, is unaffected by sinful actions

_**brahmaṇy ādhāya karmāṇi**_  
_**saṅgaṁ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ**_  
_**lipyate na sa pāpena**_  
_**padma-patram ivāmbhasā**_  

One who performs his duty without attachment, surrendering the results unto the Supreme Lord, is unaffected by sinful action, as the lotus leaf is untouched by water.
