# Śrīla Prabhupāda Praṇati

## nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya kṛṣṇa-preṣṭhāya bhū-tale  
śrīmate bhakvedānta-svāmin i nāmine  

When will Srīla Rūpa Gosvāmī Prabhupāda, who has established within this  
material world the mission to fulfill the desire of Lord Caitanya, give me  
shelter under his lotus feet?  

## namas te sārasvate deve gaura-vāṇī-pracāriṇe  
nirviśeṣa-śūnyavādi-pāścātya-deśa-tāriṇe  

Our respecul obeisances are unto you, O spiritual master, servant of  
Sarasva Gosvāmī. You are kindly preaching the message of Lord  
aitanyadeva and delivering the Western countries, which are filled with  
impersonalism and voidism.
