# Step 1 Aachman

3 times -

take water in hand

om keshavaya namah

om narayanaya namah

om madhavaya namah

  

1 mat to sit and do pooja

  

## Waking up of deities & Doing aarti

- light and bell
- O krishna please wake up from your devine sleep and bless the three worlds with your auspicious glance
- Offer obiscences and pray pranam mantras
- wipe with clean cloth
    - gopinath , radharani ,narsimha dev, gaur nitai, panch tatva, sad goswami, guru parampara
- offer flowers from down to top , guru parmpara to gopinath
- offer tulasi dip in chandan if offering in summer water in winter, only offer to jagganath, gopinath and mahaprabhu
- offer water with 1 tulasi leave and the water will stay full day remove next morning
