**Tavaivasmi Tavaivasmi Na Jivami Tvayā VinaIti Vijnaya Devi Tvaṁ Naya Mam Caraṇantikam.**_- Shri Raghunath Das, Vilap Kusumanjali (96)_

Shri Raghunath Das Ji prays: “I am Yours! I am Yours! I cannot live without You! O Devi (Radha), please understand this and bring me to Your lotus feet.”
