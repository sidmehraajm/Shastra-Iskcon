**_“I offer my respectful obeisances unto the six Gosvamis–Sri Rupa Gosvami, Sri Sanatana Gosvami, Sri Raghunatha Bhatta Gosvami, Sri Raghunatha dasa Gosvami, Sri Jiva Gosvami, and Sri Gopala Bhatta Gosvami”_**

> [!important] **It was the six Goswamis who not only founded this holy city of Vrindavana but also firmly established the teachings of Lord Chaitanya Mahaprabhu through their various writings.**
> 
> They explained the doctrine and philosophy of Sri Chaitanya for the benefit of the whole world. The two brothers Rupa and Sanatana Goswamis were ordered by Lord Chaitanya in the year 1516 to go and live in Vrindavana and search out all the lost holy places connected with Lord Sri Krishna’s transcendental pastimes.
