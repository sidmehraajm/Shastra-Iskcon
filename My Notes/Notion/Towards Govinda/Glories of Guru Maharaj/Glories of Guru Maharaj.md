# Pranam Mantra

_**गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णु र्गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः**_  
_**गुरु साक्षात परब्रह्मा तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः**_  
  
  
Guru is the Creator (Brahma), Guru is the Preserver(Vishnu), GuruDeva is Destroyer(Maheshwara)  
Guru is the absolute (singular) Lord himself, Salutations to that Sri Guru  

## Glories of Guru Maharaj

In a song by Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura it is written that

_yasya prasādād bhagavat-prasādoyas_  
_yāprasādān na gatiḥ kuto ’pi_  

“By the mercy of the spiritual master one is benedicted by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. Without the grace of the spiritual master, one cannot make any advancement.”

  

**_mūkaṁ karoti vācālaṁ_**  
_**paṅguṁ laṅghayate girim**_  
_**yat-kṛpā tam ahaṁ vande**_  
_**śrī-guruṁ dīna-tāraṇam**_  
  
(Madhya 17.80)  

By the mercy of the guru, even a dumb man can become the greatest orator, and even a lame man can cross mountains. As advised by Lord Brahmā, one should remember this śāstric injunction if one desires success in his life.
