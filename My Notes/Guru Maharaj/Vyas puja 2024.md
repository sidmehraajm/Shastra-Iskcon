  

**Hare Krishna Dear Guru Maharaj**

All glories to Srila Prabhupada!

All glories to your mercy.

Please accept my most humble obeisances at your lotus feet.

  

Dear Eternal companion, as soon as I saw you, these words came from my heart: **Aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁ! What good fortune, what good fortune!**

I’d like to share some extracts from your book _Our Family Business_. Although I haven’t finished it yet, what I’ve read so far has deepened my understanding of your spiritual realisations, I can see you have truly poured your heart in it.

Śrīla Prabhupāda writes in his purport to _Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam_ 3.22.5

**"Thus it is actually a fact that if one meets a saintly person completely engaged in transcendental duties and achieves his favor, then one’s life mission becomes complete. What is not possible to achieve in thousands of lives can be achieved in one moment if there is an opportunity to meet a saintly person." (SB 3.22.5 purport)**

Engaging fully in selfless spiritual service is the only way to attract Kṛṣṇa, bringing true satisfaction. A sincere desire for such service indicates an awakening of spiritual intelligence. By receiving guidance from Kṛṣṇa’s authorized representative and wholeheartedly embracing it, one invokes the blessings of their spiritual master and the previous teachers in the line of pure devotion.

**“When ISKCON Boston set up a full-scale printing operation, Srila Prabhuada came to visit especially for the occasion. When he arrived at the Boston temple, he went first to see the new press. As soon as he entered the doorway, he glorified his spiritual master by chanting, ‘Jaya oṁ viṣṇupāda paramahaṁsa parivrājakācārya Śrī Śrīmad Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Gosvāmī Mahārāja, Śrīla Prabhupāda kī jaya!’ After these prayers, he said, ‘My guru mahārāja would be very pleased.’”**

**“Śrī Caitanya's order grants us the opportunity to practice the highest form of devotional service. Śrīla Śukadeva Gosvāmī says, ‘It is said that great personalities almost always accept voluntary suffering because of the suffering of people in general. This is considered the highest method of worshiping the Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is present in everyone’s heart.’”**

I witnessed this myself during your recent visit to Toronto.

I am certain that you are very dear to Śrīla Prabhupāda because you are not only carrying out what was closest to his heart, but also fulfilling the desire of his guru, Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura.

With deep humility, I offer this verse in prayer:

**tava kathāmṛtaṁ tapta-jīvanaṁ**  
**kavibhir īḍitaṁ kalmaṣāpaham**  
**śravaṇa-maṅgalaṁ śrīmad ātataṁ**  
**bhuvi gṛṇanti ye bhūri-dā janāḥ**  

This was sung for Krishna by the gopīs: **These narrations, transmitted by learned sages, eradicate one's sinful reactions and bestow good fortune upon whoever hears them.**

Your insignificant servant,

Siddarth

I
