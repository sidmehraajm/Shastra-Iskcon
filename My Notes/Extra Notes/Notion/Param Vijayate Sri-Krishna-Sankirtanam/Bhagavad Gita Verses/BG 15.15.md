POINTS -I am seated in everyone’s **heart,** knower of Vedas

_**sarvasya cāhaṁ hṛdi sanniviṣṭo**_  
_**mattaḥ smṛtir jñānam apohanaṁ ca**_  
_**vedaiś ca sarvair aham eva vedyo**_  
_**vedānta-kṛd veda-vid eva cāham**_  

I am seated in everyone’s heart, and from Me come remembrance, knowledge, and forgetfulness. By all the Vedas, I am to be known. Indeed, I am the compiler of Vedānta, and I am the knower of the Vedas.
