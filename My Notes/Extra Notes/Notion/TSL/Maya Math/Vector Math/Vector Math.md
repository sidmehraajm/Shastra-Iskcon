What is Vector?

A vector is an object that has both a magnitude and a direction. Geometrically, we can picture a vector as a directed line segment, whose length is the magnitude of the vector and with an arrow indicating the direction. The direction of the vector is from its tail to its head.

[![](https://mathinsight.org/media/image/image/vector.png)](https://mathinsight.org/media/image/image/vector.png)

  

- All Vectors are same in size

What is magnitude

The magnitude of a vector is the length of the vector. The magnitude of the vector aa  
 is denoted as ∥a∥  
It’s an absolute value meaning always positive
