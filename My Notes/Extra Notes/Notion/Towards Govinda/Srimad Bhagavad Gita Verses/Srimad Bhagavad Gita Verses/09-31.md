POINTS - My devotee never perishes!

_**kṣhipraṁ bhavati dharmātmā**_  
_**śhaśhvach-chhāntiṁ nigachchhati**_  
_**kaunteya pratijānīhi**_  
_**na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśhyati**_  

  

He quickly becomes righteous and attains lasting peace. O son of Kuntī, declare it boldly that My devotee never perishes
