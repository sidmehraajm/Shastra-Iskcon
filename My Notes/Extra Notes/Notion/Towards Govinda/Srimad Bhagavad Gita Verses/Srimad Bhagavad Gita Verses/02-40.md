POINTS - There is no loss ever in BHAKTI

_**nehābhikrama-nāśo ’sti**_  
_**pratyavāyo na vidyate**_  
_**sv-alpam apy asya dharmasya**_  
_**trāyate mahato bhayāt**_  

In this endeavor, there is no loss or diminution, and a little advancement on this path can protect one from the most dangerous type of fear.
