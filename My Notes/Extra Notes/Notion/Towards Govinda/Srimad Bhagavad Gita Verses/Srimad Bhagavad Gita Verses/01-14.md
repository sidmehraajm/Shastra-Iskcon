_**tataḥ śvetair hayair yukte**_  
_**mahati syandane sthitau**_  
_**mādhavaḥ pāṇḍavaś caiva**_  
_**divyau śaṅkhau pradadhmatuḥ**_  

_On the other side, both Lord Kṛṣṇa and Arjuna, stationed on a great chariot drawn by white horses, sounded their transcendental conchshells._

Madhava is used to explain whenever and wherever the Lord is present, the goddess of fortune is also there because the goddess of fortune never lives alone without her husband.

The sounding of the transcendental conchshells indicated that there was no hope of victory for the other side because Kṛṣṇa was on the side of the Pāṇḍavas
