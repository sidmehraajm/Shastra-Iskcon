Dear Srila Prabhupada,

_**om ajnana-timirandhasya jnananjana-salakaya**_  
_**cakshur unmilitam yena tasmai sri-gurave namah**_  
  

Please accept my humble prostrated obeisances at your lotus feet, again and again.  
  

ohe! vaiṣṇaba ṭhākura, doyāra sāgara,  
e dāse koruṇā kori'  
diyā pada-chāyā, śodho he āmāya,  
tomāra caraṇa dhori  
  
”On this auspicious day of your appearance, it is a wonderful opportunity for me to remember your selfless devotional acts towards Krishna and your unmatched efforts in guiding us fallen souls back to our beloved Krishna. There are no words or acts that can ever thank you enough for what you have done for us”  

These recent months have been life-changing. My Bhakti, which had faltered badly last year, has been rekindled due to the blessings of some pure Vaishnavas during this year's Toronto Rath Yatra.  
A devotee asked me if I had thought about accepting a guru, and it sparked a realization in my heart. I wondered what I had been doing for almost four years in the movement without even chanting properly. Everything changed since then, with the help of some great Vaishnavas. I've started chanting 16 rounds daily and have resolved to do so for the rest of my life without fail.  
  
I feel incredibly fortunate to have met HH Gopal Krishna Goswami Maharaj. His darshan has given my life profound meaning, and I even had the opportunity to touch his feet. I also met HG Vaisesika Dasa, who has empowered me greatly on my journey towards Krishna. I hope and pray to Krishna that he accepts me as his disciple next year or whenever Krishna desires. I can't fully comprehend it, but after meeting these two great souls, the words that come to my mind are 'aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁ.’  
  
I consider myself a very fallen and neophyte devotee, not qualified to offer anything. Nevertheless, I would like to share some verses that are close to my heart, thanks to the teachings of these great Vaishnavas. If it were not for you and your disciples, we fallen souls might have never heard or known about these verses. The Bhagavatam is my life and soul, and I wish and hope that I never lose my love for it in this life or any other lives I may have to live.
