Q2. Feedback  
A. Did you get woken up with a jolt  
- Yes definitely many times during classes as well as conversations in the temple  
  
B Need for scriptural study  
- Before I heard this many times that only devotional love is necessary for worshiping Krsna, but now I understand that with proper knowledge and understanding only one can develop pure devotional service, also that scriptural study helps us solidify & protect ourselves from adharma, sense enjoyment & everything which is ānukūlyena kṛṣṇānuśīlanam  
  
C. Not this body but soul  
- Absolutely this i knew but now starting to tell myself more and more whenever someone says anything bad to me or if i start seeing faults in others i try to think that we are not this body but we are all parts and parcels of krishna  
  
D. Do you know how to live as a soul in this world  
-by making 24 hours kc by making all our activities kc, all activities somehow dovetailing towards krishna, trying to achieve hopefully one day, yukta-vairāgya  
  
E. Do you realise to trust the mind as a outsider  
- The lecture from hg chaitnya charan prabhu was super amazing, now I will never trust this outsider, before i considered it as my friend but this mind should be in shackles so it doens’t suggest useless pleasures  
  
F. Controlling senses and mind is not by restricting them but by engaging them in service of krishna according to scriptures  
- This point as well, don’t want that suppression to get burst out one day instead use all senses in service of krishna  
  
G. Revive relationship with krishna, with devotees  
- Thank you for your association Prabhu, I have found al your answers to my questions very relevant and they have helped me in advancing more towards Gopinath, also I thank you deeply for letting me come for service, Being able to serve even very little has given me a lot of bliss and I am sure I will try to do service all my life as much as I am able to.
