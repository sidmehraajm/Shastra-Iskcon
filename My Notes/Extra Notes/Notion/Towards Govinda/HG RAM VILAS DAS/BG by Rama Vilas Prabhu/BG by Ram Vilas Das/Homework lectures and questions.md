3 - BG 2.1 Ahemdabada

- _Paramo nirmatsarāṇāṁ satāṁ vastavya-vastu vedyam_ [SB 1.1.2]. In the _Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam_ it is said that if you want to know the real truth, _vastavya-vastu,_ then one must become _paramo nirmatsaram. Nirmatsara. Matsara, matsara_ means envious, and _nirmatsara,_ not envious. And _parama,_ first-class _nirmatsara._
