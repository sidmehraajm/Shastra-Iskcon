## What does condition soul mean

Conditioned soul means the living being, the spirit soul who is **conditioned by these laws of material nature**. The laws of material nature is that you have to accept a certain type of body according to your natural tendency to behave in a certain way which is to serve and if we don’t serve Krishna we will be slaves of maya and our senses.

Although our constitutional position is to serve, but artificially we want to give up service and we want to enjoy.  
The conditioned soul is bound up, as though shackled by iron chains. He is bound up by the false ego, and the mind is the chief agent which is driving him in this material existence. When the mind is in the mode of goodness, his activities are good; when the mind is in the mode of passion, his activities are troublesome; and when the mind is in the mode of ignorance, he travels in the lower species of life. It is clear, however, in this verse, that the conditioned soul is covered by the material body, with the mind and the senses, and when he is liberated this material covering perishes, but his spiritual body manifests itself in its individual capacity.(BG 15.7 purport)
