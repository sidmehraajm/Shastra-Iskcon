Q1 What does fearless mean. (Provide sources)

Fearless is a mental state when one thinks he is **_safe or protected_** and **having no fear**(English meaning)

- One should be **confident** that in all circumstances **Kṛṣṇa will protect him** from all difficulties. There is **no need of thinking** how one should keep the **body** and **soul together**.(Purport BG 18.66)
- One who, with a regulated mind, recites with devotion this Bhagavad-gītā scripture which is the bestower of all virtue, will attain to a holy abode such as Vaikuṇṭha, the residence of Lord Viṣṇu, **which is always free from the mundane qualities based on fear and lamentation.**(GĪTĀ-MAHĀTMYĀ Text 1 - Adi S., mentioned in BG Intro)

Realisations -

- There is **no need to fear anything**, because the Lord takes all responsibility for **one who surrenders unto Him**, and He indemnifies such a person **against all reactions of sins.**
- Also The **soul is Eternal** as mentioned in BG 2.20 there is no reason to fear for something which **cannot be destroyed** with the material body, is **immortal** so there is no point in having fear for something **which is temporary** and is in **a temporary place** which is full of **miseries**.  
    _duḥkhālayam aśāśvatam_  
      
    temporary world, which is full of miseries  
    _(BG 8.15)_  
      
    

Q2 Do you think seeing is believing, what all do you see around. (Provide sources)

Seeing cannot be believing because we have imperfect senses. (BG Intro)

We see the material and mundane things around us, but instead of all that we should always try to see krishna in everything to do that we should see krishna as explained in bg 7 chapter  
  

Realisations

- "No one can understand the transcendental nature of the name, form, quality and pastimes of Sri Krishna through his materially **contaminated senses**. Only when one becomes spiritually saturated by transcendental service to the Lord are the transcendental name, form, quality and pastimes of the Lord revealed to him." (Bhakti-rasamrta-sindhu 1.2.234)
- Bg 7.25, naham prakashah sarvasya yoga-maya-samavritah: “I am not visible to everyone, because there is a curtain called yoga-maya covering the eyes of ordinary persons,” just like a cloud covering the eyes’ sight. One cannot see the sun in full vision when there is a cloud. Similarly, yoga-maya has created a cloud, on account of which we cannot see God.
- “I worship Govinda, the primeval Lord, who is Syamasundara, Krishna Himself with inconceivable innumerable attributes, whom the pure devotees see in their heart of hearts with the eye of devotion tinged with the salve of love.” Prema means love, and anjana means ointment. When the ointment of love is smeared over the eyes, then those who are devotees – santah, saintly persons – can always see Krishna within the heart - Brahma-samhita (5.38)
