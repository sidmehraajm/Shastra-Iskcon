2. How temptations of mind overpower?  
    - Past impressions because of Indulgences overwhelm so much, that it gets impossible to resist it  
    
3. How mind acts as enemy?  
    - It tries to indulges us in sense enjoyment, gives anxiety  
    - Lies in 4 major ways (magnifies problems, Imagines Pleasures, Neglects Opportunities, Denies reality )  
      
    
4. Describe components of the acronym M I N D?  
    M - Magnifies problems: overthinking does not solve the problems, and keeps us glued to the issue and drains all our energy  
    I - Imagines Pleasures: sees and fantasise about it and drains so much energy because of this daydreaming (Vision is different from daydreaming knowing the ultimate goal of life :Krishna )  
    N - Neglects opportunities : This life is not a practise run, the present is all that we have and all that we will ever have  
    D - Denies reality and refuses to acknowledge that the problem is itself  
      
    
5. When to control the mind?  
    - By recognising when mind is not in control  
    - When its trying to take the least path of resistance
