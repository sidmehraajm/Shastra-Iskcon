Q1. striking points and questions

- all senses should be used in service of krishna
- Urges of Speech can be controlled by speaking krishn katha
- Urges of Mind can be controlled by always thinking about krishna
- Staying away from anger is the best to use in service of krishna,
- Urges of tongue & belly can be controlled by honouring krishna prasadam
- Urges of Genitals can be controlled by begetting krishna conscious children and following the 4 regulative principles

  

Q2. Read krishna book ch 60 - Talks between krishna and Rukmini (populate points about senses & other realisations)

- The king’s of this world are the senses, they are most formidable & control everyone, but krishna is never under the control of the senses; rather, he’s the controller of the senses, Hṛṣīkeśa.

---

  

- those who have some attachment to Your lotus feet, also give up supremacy over the material world because they consider the material position to be the darkest region, which checks the progress of spiritual enlightenment
- Krishna does not need to possess anything as he himself is everything, he doesn’t possess anything but no one is richer than him
- Krishna accepts everything offered in devotion by the devotees
- persons who are puffed up with material possessions think of utilising their property for sense gratification
- Devotees like Narad Muni who are very dear to you, do not care for anything but Your Lordship.
- No one can be equal or above to Krishna, he’s supreme source of everything
- Krishna gives himself to the great souls who have renounced all material comforts
- Krishna is the reservoir of all good qualities

Q3. Read Krishna Book chapter 72 (Liberation of Jarasandh) (populate points about senses & other realisations)

- One who has not conquered his senses cannot conquer the Supreme Personality of Godhead
- To conquer the senses means to engage them constantly in the service of the Lord, That is the secret of Devotional Service
- One who thus engages his senses becomes purified, and with purified senses the devotee can actually render transcendental loving service to the Lord and conquer Him.

---

- one has to depend upon krishna for everything if one think himself as the doer and doesn’t take shelter of krishna he cannot succeed
- If Krishna wants to kill someone, no one can save that person. Similarly, if He wants to save someone, no one can kill him.(Rakhe Krsna Mare Ke, Mare Krsna Rakhe Ke)

- **“You say You always maintain enmity with the worldly kings. But who are the worldly kings? I think the worldly kings are the senses. They are most formidable, and they control everyone. Certainly You maintain enmity with these material senses. You are never under the control of the senses; rather, You are the controller of the senses, Hṛṣīkeśa.**
- **Lord Kṛṣṇa told King Yudhiṣṭhira that He is conquered by the love of one who has conquered his senses. One who has not conquered his senses cannot conquer the Supreme Personality of Godhead. This is the secret of devotional service. To conquer the senses means to engage them constantly in the service of the Lord. The specific qualification of all the Pāṇḍava brothers was that they always engaged their senses in the Lord’s service. One who thus engages his senses becomes purified, and with purified senses the devotee can actually render transcendental loving service to the Lord and conquer Him.**
