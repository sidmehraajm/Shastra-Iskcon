Only do donations through Vaishnavas and only which will help someone get back to Krishna

Try to do go seva in a devotee’s Goshala

Instead of looking for negative try to look for positive qualities in devotees

Always try to be compassionate for people and try to get them closer to Krishna

Krishna always accepts everything offered by the devotee

1. _**utsāhāt**_ **- by enthusiasm**
    - Endeavour with intelligence in KC
    - It’s expressed through service and commitment
    - Yukta Vairagya - Use everything for the service of Gopinath
2. _**niścayāt -**_ **endeavoring with confidence**
    - Krishna will always protect me
    - No confidence in other paths
3. _**dhairyāt**_ **- by patience**
    - Taking instructions from guru
    - executing devotional service
    - taking instruction from Guru and Krishna
4. **tat-tat-karma — practice of rules and regulations of bhakti**
    - neglect of principles will destroy bhakti
    - presenting the message in an appealing way is different from changing the message so it appeals
5. **saṅga-tyāgāt — by giving up the association of nondevotees**
    - easiest way to avoid contamination si to avoid association of non devotees
    - not look down upon them but keep protecting ourself
    - avoid anti devotional association
6. **satah - of the great previous ācāryas**
    - should not avoid association of devotees
