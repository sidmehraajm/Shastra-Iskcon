लोकाभिरामं रणरङ्गधीरं राजीवनेत्रं रघुवंशनाथम् ।  
कारुण्यरुपं करुणाकरंतं श्रीरामचंद्रं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥  
**Lokaabhi-Raamam Ranna-Rangga-Dhiiram**  
**Raajiiva-Netram Raghu-Vamsha-Naatham |**  
**Kaarunnya-Rupam Karunnaa-Karantam**  
**Shriiraamacamdram Sharannam Prapadye ||**  

  
(I take Refuge in Sri Rama) Who is Pleasing to the People, Who is Calm and Composed in the Battle Field, Whose Eyes are like Blue Lotuses, and Who is the Lord of the Raghu Dynasty,  
Who is an Embodiment of Compassion and Showers His Compassion to All; I take Refuge in the Lotus Feet of Sri Ramachandra.
