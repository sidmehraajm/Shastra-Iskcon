### **Phase 2: Awareness & Engagement (April 1 – April 11)**

- **April 1–4:**
    - Begin offline distribution on community bulletin boards and high-traffic areas.
- **April 5–8:**
    - Launch social media campaigns with engaging posts and reels.
    - Engage actively in Toronto-based Facebook groups and online communities.
- **April 9–11:**
    - Follow up with press outreach and submit event listings.
    - Confirm volunteer schedules and finalize street team plans.

---

### **Phase 3: Active Promotion & Engagement (April 12 – June 30)**

- **Ongoing Email & Social Media:**
    - Continue targeted email updates and regular social media posts.
    - Maintain the planned schedule of reels (approximately 4 per month).
- **Volunteer & Community Outreach:**
    - Regularly update partners and community groups with event news.
    - Leverage user-generated content to increase organic reach.
- **Offline Efforts:**
    - Sustain poster and postcard distribution, ensuring materials remain visible.
- **Interactive Campaigns:**
    - Run contests and interactive posts with low-cost prizes.
    - Monitor engagement and adjust tactics based on performance metrics.

---

### **Phase 4: Final Push & Event Execution (July 1 – July 11)**

- **Countdown & Last-Minute Reminders:**
    - Ramp up daily countdown posts and real-time engagement.
    - Host live Q&A sessions or brief virtual meet-ups.
- **Final Email Blast:**
    - Send a last-minute reminder with key event details and logistics.
- **On-Ground Readiness:**
    - Confirm all volunteer schedules and on-site promotional materials.
- **Real-Time Engagement:**
    - Share live updates, behind-the-scenes content, and last-minute excitement.
- **Post-Event Follow-Up:**
    - Collect feedback via surveys and thank participants through email and social media.

---

## 4. Additional Enhancements & Best Practices

- **Performance Metrics:**
    - Use analytics from social media platforms and email campaigns to gauge success.
    - Adjust content and distribution strategies based on open rates, engagement, and feedback.
- **QR Code Integration:**
    - Ensure every printed material includes a QR code that directs users to social channels, registration pages, or event updates.
- **Volunteer Recognition:**
    - Highlight volunteer contributions on social media to boost morale and encourage participation.
- **Cross-Promotion:**
    - Consider small partnerships with local businesses and community organizations for mutual promotion.
- **Post-Event Analysis:**
    - Plan a debrief meeting to review what worked and gather insights for future events (e.g., a dedicated volunteer page or updating promotional materials).
